Canine Distemper
What is Canine Distemper?
Canine Distemper is a highly contagious viral disease that primarily affects dogs, but can also infect other animals such as foxes, raccoons, and skunks. The virus attacks the respiratory, gastrointestinal and nervous systems of dogs or other animals. It is typically transmitted through respiratory secretions and contaminated surfaces.
Symptoms
Fever
Runny nose or eyes
Cough
Vomiting and diarrhea
Neurological problems
Treatment and Prevention
There is no cure for distemper, but supportive care can help to manage symptoms. This may include antibiotics, fluids and nutritional support. If you notice any of these symptoms in your animal, you should contact their veterinarian.
The best way to prevent distemper is through
vaccination
. Puppies should begin to receive a distemper series at the 6 - 8 week age period. Boosters are recommended every 1 - 3 years following.
