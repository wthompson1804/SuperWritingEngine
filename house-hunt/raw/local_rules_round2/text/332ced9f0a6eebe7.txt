Overnight Street Parking Permits
are available for purchase online 24 hours a day or in person between the hours of 10:00a.m. to 4:00p.m.  Below please find parking information and mandatory requirements to obtain a permit.
The City of East Orange requires a parking permit for all streets everyday of the week that allow overnight parking between the hours of 2a.m. to 6a.m. Overnight Street Parking Permits are issued to East Orange residents only that can provide the required documentation stated below.  
		  The permit allows residents to park on certain streets that is approved for
overnight parking between the hours of 2a.m. to 6a.m.; with a permit, residents are still required to obey all parking rules and regulations posted.
Parking Fees
The Overnight Parking Permit Fee is $150 per car for the period of January 1st to December 31st, all permits expire on December 31st. The permit cost is pro-rated based upon the month of purchase; all permits incur a transaction fee.

Existing Permit Holders:
Login to the permit portal using your existing login (your email is the username) and your password to purchase an Overnight Street Parking Permit.  Please contact us if you forgot your username and password to reset it or click the forgot the password link.
REQUIREMENTS TO PURCHASE AN OVERNIGHT STREET PERMIT:
You must upload the following Mandatory Documents:
1.VALID DRIVER’S LICENSE
2. VALID REGISTRATION
3.VALID AUTO INSURANCE
4.PROOF OF RESIDENCY:
If the above mandatory documents do not have your East Orange address you must also 
			upload the following documents as proof of residency:
a.Valid Lease Agreement
b.IRS Letters
c.Property Tax Bill, Deed, Property Tax Card, Mortgage Statement
5.SECONDARY PROOF OF RESIDENCY
If you do not have the above documents, we will accept the following secondary 
			documents dated within 90 days of your application:
Bank Statements
Utility Bills
Credit Card Statements
You can change your address on the New Jersey Motor Vehicle Commission and submit the receipt, visit:
https://www.nj.gov/mvc/drivertopics/addchange.htm

PROOF FOR SENIOR RATE –
Residents must be age 65 years old to qualify for the senior rate; the
			age will be verified based upon the driver’s license submitted.
PROOF OF DISABILITY –
FOR DISABLED PERMITS 
Residents must submit a valid Disability Card issued by the NJDMV and/or an approved letter from the Social 
Security Administration as proof of disability in addition to the required documents.

No
          Overnight Parking Streets:
There are streets in East Orange where overnight street parking is not allowed. If you park on those streets you will be ticketed and towed even if you have a permit.
Please do not park on these streets overnight. The signs on these streets will indicate if overnight parking is not allowed. 
		  Read all street signs carefully prior to parking.
