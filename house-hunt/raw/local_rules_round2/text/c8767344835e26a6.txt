Alva Street - Residential Permit Parking
Posted April 17, 2026
Alva Street is now a Residential Permit Parking Street between the hours of 2:00 AM and 6:00 AM.
Residents of Alva Street can obtain these Residential Parking permits through the Code Enforcement Department at Belleville Town Hall, Monday through Friday 8:00 A.M. to 4:00 P.M..
Any questions concerning what documentation must be presented to obtain these permits, please contact Code Enforcement at 973-450-3410.
