Volunteer Positions Filled
Thank you very much for taking the time to submit a Volunteer Information Form for consideration. We appreciate your interest in the Borough.
Volunteer positions or vacancies open from time to time, and we encourage you to complete the Volunteer Interest Form for consideration.
If you were not appointed, please do not be discouraged. The submitted applications will be kept on file for 3 years for future consideration and/or as vacancies occur.
On behalf of the Borough, we thank you for your time, interest, and effort and look forward to an opportunity to work with you in the future.
