Park & Facility Rentals
Roxbury Township offers a wide array of fields & facilities that are available to rent. Whether it be something as simple as a child's birthday party or a large scale event with thousands of attendees, the Recreation Department has something that will probably work for you. For rental inquiries, please email Ryan Williams
williamsr@roxburynj.us
or call 973-448-2014.  Please note that this only applies for Roxbury Twp facilities, any Roxbury BOE facility needs to be booked through the Roxbury BOE.
