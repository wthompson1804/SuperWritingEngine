                    East Orange, New Jersey, nicknamed “The Crossroads of New Jersey,” is
                    conveniently located at the intersection of the Garden State Parkway and
                    Interstate 280, just several miles from Newark Liberty International Airport and
                    less than 30 minutes from New York City.

                    With approximately 70,000 residents, the 3.9 square miles of East Orange are
                    adjacent to the municipalities of Orange, West Orange, South Orange,
ABOUT EAST ORANGE




                    Montclair, Glen Ridge, Bloomfield and Newark. Originally founded as a
                    township in 1863, East Orange became incorporated as a city on January 1,
                    1909. By the close of the 19th century, East Orange was an agriculturally-rich
                    area populated by oil tycoons and Newark industrialists.

                    Known for its spacious homes and wide tree-lined streets, East Orange
                    experienced a huge population surge between 1940-1950, paving the way
                    for the addition of luxury high-rise buildings and a boom of commercial
                    businesses along Main Street and Central Avenue. Once called the “Fifth
                    Avenue of New Jersey,” Central Avenue attracted shoppers from around
                    the region. In 1945, East Orange was ranked the 20th best place to live in
                    the country among cities of a similar size and named the “Cleanest City in
                    America” three times in the late1960s.

                    Transportation continues to be one of East Orange’s greatest assets. Today,
                    the city is designated as a Transit Village and its Brick Church and East
                    Orange Train Stations offer daily direct service to midtown Manhattan.

                    Currently home to a diverse cultural mecca of people representing
                    countries from around the globe, East Orange combines the best of urban
                    and suburban living. Spacious and affordable housing, safe neighborhoods,
                    main commercial corridors, distinctive Caribbean and Southern cuisine,
                    transit access, and plenty of historic green spaces make East Orange a
                    quintessential city for urban renewal.

                    East Orange City government is divided into three branches: the Executive Branch
                    under the supervision of the Mayor; the Legislative Branch comprised of the City
                    Council and the Municipal Court which serves as the Judicial Branch. The Mayor
                    and City Council are elected to serve a four-year term. The 10-member Council
                    consists of two representatives from each of the city’s five geographic wards. Five
                    members are elected to the City Council every odd year from each ward.

                    The Mayor is the city’s Chief Executive Officer, responsible for the enforcement of
                    city ordinances, the preparation and submission of the city’s annual operating
LOCAL GOVERNMENT




                    budget to the Council, and the appointment and supervision of city department
                    directors. The Mayor may also recommend legislation to the Council deemed in the
                    best interest of the community. Each year, the Mayor reports to the City Council on
                    the previous year’s municipal operations.

                    The City Council is the local legislative body responsible for appropriating funds and
                    enacting ordinances and resolutions governing the city. The City Council reviews
                    and approves the Mayor’s appropriations and annual budget. The Council reviews
                    and approves the Mayor’s appointments to City Departments and several Citizen
                    Advisory Boards and Commissions.

                    Each New Year’s Day, the City Council meets to organize for the year. It adopts its
                    rules and selects one of its members to act as Chairperson during the year-long
                    legislative session. The Chair appoints each Council Member to oversee a standing
                    committee. These standing committees serve as Council liaisons to city
                    departments, public agencies, civic groups, and public service organizations.

                    The City Council Chairperson serves as an ex-officio member of every committee
                    and is liaison to the Mayor, Essex County Commissioner Board, the NJ State
                    Legislature, the United States Congress, and the East Orange Parking Authority.

                    Public meetings are held bi-monthly in City Council Chambers located at
                    44 City Hall Plaza, East Orange, NJ. To contact your City Council Member,
                    please call the City Council Office at (973) 266-5120.

                        Snow Removal
                        During snow emergencies, all emergency parking rules are in effect. Motorists should
                        adhere to all NO PARKING signs on county and city roads. Overnight parking rules will
                        be strictly enforced.

                        The Department of Public Works will begin plowing when snow depths reach two
                        inches and freezing temperatures indicate no melting. Residents are asked to NOT
                        shovel snow into the street. The only way to avoid additional snow removal is to wait
                        until DPW workers have completed their final clean-up of your road.

                        Businesses and multi-dwelling owners of 5 or more units must clear snow 4 hours after it
                        stops, and single and multi-family homeowners must clear snow 8 hours after stops.
                        Failure to adhere to this city ordinance may result in a fine. For more information or
                        inquiries, please call the Department of Code Enforcement at 973.266.5320.

                      PARKING REGULATIONS
                      The City of East Orange requires all cars that are parked on the street overnight to
                      purchase and display a parking permit. Overnight street parking permits are only valid on
                      authorized streets that allow overnight parking with signs that indicate Permit Required to
                      Park. The overnight street parking permit is not valid for any street that indicates NO
                      PARKING 2AM-6AM ANY DAY.

                      HOW TO PURCHASE A PARKING PERMIT
                      Daily Visitor Overnight Permits (one day single use) can be purchased online at
                      www.eoparking.org or at the City of East Orange Licensing Office at City Hall from 8:30am
                      to 4:30pm. Call 973-266-5130 for more information. Annual Residential, Senior, and
                      Disabled Overnight Street Parking Permits and Lot Parking Permits are sold by the East
                      Orange Parking Authority (EOPA). Please see below for details.

                      COST FOR OVERNIGHT LOT PARKING PERMIT
                      $55/$80 per month per car

                      • EOPA Parking Lot Permits shall only be used by the original applicant and shall
                        not be loaned, transferred or used by another vehicle.

                      • Lot permits are sold at the EOPA, 60 Evergreen Place, #200, or via
                        www.eoparking.org on a first-come, first-served basis. Overnight permits between
                        8pm to 8am are $55 per month or $80 per month for all day parking.

                      • Illegally parked vehicles will be ticketed and/or towed at the owner’s expense.

                      • Parking lot permits are not prorated and are only valid for the month it is purchased.
PARKING REGULATIONS




                      • A parking lot permit is valid only in the lot specifically selected during purchase.

                      • EOPA lot permits are not valid for street parking, unless approved.

                      • Call EOPA at 973.672.1116 if you think your vehicle was towed from an EOPA lot(s).

                      RESIDENTIAL, SENIOR AND DISABLED OVERNIGHT ON-STREET PARKING PERMITS
                      $150 annual fee per vehicle (Prorated based upon month of purchase).
                      $75 for Senior and Disabled permit(s) are not prorated.

                      • To purchase a residential overnight permit, visit www.eoparking.org.

                      • Alternative side parking rules for 2am-6pm must be followed to avoid getting a ticket.

                      • To obtain a permit, applicants must provide a valid driver's license, vehicle registration,
                        insurance card, and proof of residency, such as a lease or PSE&G bill. A vehicle that is
                        registered to a college student that resides in East Orange may obtain a parking
                        permit at no cost by providing a copy of the student’s current transcript and college
                        identification.

                        visiting www.eoparking.org.

                      • Residential overnight street permits are not valid for EOPA lots.

                      • Call EOPD if your vehicle was towed from a city street at 973-266-5000.
