Bingo & Raffle Licensing
A qualified organization MUST receive an identification number from the Control Commission prior to making an application for a Bingo or Raffle license.
An organization should register for the identification number from the Legalized Games of Chance Control Commission at:
Legalized Games of Chance Control Commission
124 Halsey Street - 7th Floor
Newark, NJ 07101
Tel: (973) 273.8000
Upon obtaining the identification number from the Legalized Games of Chance, the organization must then go to the municipality where the games are to be conducted. A copy of the identification number must be brought along when applying for the license.
At the time an organization applies to a municipality for a license to hold a Bingo or Raffle, the municipality will give them the application and advise how much the fee will be.
After properly filling out the forms and returning them with payment, the license application must be approved by a Resolution at the Council Meeting and forwarded to the Bureau of Criminal Investigation at the Police Department.
Once this process is complete, the applications are sent to the Legalized Games of Chance for approval. There is a two week waiting period after the application is mailed.
If you require additional information, click on the link provided in the left hand column.
** In order to ensure receipt of all necessary licenses in sufficient time, application and fees should be submitted at least three (3) months prior to the date of the event.
Elections and Voter Registration
To register in NJ you must be:
A U.S. citizen
At least 18 years old on or before the next election
Residing at your present address for at least 30 days
You are not eligible to register or vote if you are on parole or probation, or if you are serving a prison sentence for violating state or federal law
You must complete an application, available for download at the state site.

Marriage/Civil Union Licenses
Step 1 - State law requires the parties to apply for a marriage or civil union license in the city where either applicant resides. If both parties reside out of the state, then they must apply in the City where the ceremony will be performed.
Step 2 - Both
MUST
be 18 years or older.
Step 3 - Identification is required for both parties, (i.e.: Birth Certificate, Driver's License, or Passport). Social Security Card or Social Security Number is also required. Proof of residency is required.
Step 4 - When applying, both parties and one witness over the age of 18 years must be present. If either party has been married before, final divorce papers or certificate of death must be presented at the time of the application.
Step 5 - State law also requires a 72-hour waiting period before marriage or civil union licenses may be issued. Licenses are valid for 30 days after date of issue. License fee is $28.00.
Anyone applying for a marriage license should come into the office between the hours of
9:00 A.M. – 11:00 A.M. or from 1:00 P.M. – 3:00 P.M. to apply for the marriage license
There will be no more wedding ceremonies in Bayonne Municipal Court, effective August 1, 2025.
For further information, please contact us at (201) 858-6029 or visit the State website by clicking on the links provided in the left column under Quick Links.
