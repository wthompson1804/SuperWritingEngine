Winter Break Adventure Week
To celebrate the coming of Winter, and provide some extracurricular fun for students during their School Break, the Department of Recreation hosted several activities through the week of December 26 to 29.
Set in Fred Erxleben and Miller Evans Logan Recreation Centers, every day was a new activity.
MOVIE SHOWING
With a projector, flavorful popcorn, and sizzling hot chocolate, the children enjoyed a cozy viewing of
Sing 2
(2021).
GAME DAY
Board games, twister, ping pong, pool, and arts & crafts, attendees had a fun day with their friends and even made new ones.
ARTS & CRAFTS
In the midst of coloring, logic puzzles, and bracelet making, Elizabeth youth came together and discussed their New Year's Resolutions.
INTERNATIONAL FOOD DAY
Recreation Staff put together creative foods from all cultures of the world for Elizabeth residents to try.
...and much more!
Stay tuned for how our Department will celebrate Winter next year!
