Winter basketball clinic
This 6-week clinic is perfect for boys and girls in grades 1 and 2. It's designed for beginners, and continuing players are welcome too. Kids will learn the fundamentals of basketball in a fun, safe environment.
About the program
Led by experienced instructors from Drills, Skills & Thrills (DST) Basketball, LLC, this clinic emphasizes skill development through games, drills, and friendly competitions. Lowered baskets are used to help players build confidence and improve their technique.
Participants will work on:
Dribbling and ball handling
Offensive and defensive skills
Weekly scrimmages
2027 schedule
Dates:
Fridays, January 8 to February 19, 2027 (no clinic on February 12)
Snow make-up date: February 26, if needed
Time:
6 to 7 pm
Location:
Van Derveer Elementary Gym (51 Union Ave.)
What to wear
Sneakers, shorts, and a t-shirt

Registration
Deadline:

Eligibility:

Note:
Space is limited to the first 40 participants
