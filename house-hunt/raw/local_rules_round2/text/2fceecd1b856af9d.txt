PERMIT TERMS AND CONDITIONS

Permit fee is prorated by month if purchased later in the calendar year.
The permit hangtag must be displayed from the vehicle's rearview mirror when parked.
In addition, residents must display the annual Ridgewood Resident or Non-Resident Parking Sticker on the
passenger window located behind the driver.
For flexibility, the permit can be used in up to 3 different vehicles, but NOT at the same time.
One permit allows the residents and non-residents to park in one parking space in specific parking lots or on
certain levels of the Hudson Street Garage.
Copy of driver’s license and car registration required for each car listed on the application.
