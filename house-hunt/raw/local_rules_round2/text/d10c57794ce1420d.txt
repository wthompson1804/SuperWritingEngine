Garage Sales
Planning a garage sale in Glen Cove? Be sure to follow the city's guidelines to ensure a smooth and legal sale! A
garage sale permit is required
and must be obtained at least
one week prior
to your sale date. You can
apply online
[HERE]
or request an application by calling the City Clerk’s Office at
516-676-3345
to have one mailed to you.
Permits are
$15
and are valid for
two consecutive days
, with the option to list rain dates. Keep in mind that
only one sale is allowed every six months per property
.
Important Reminder:
Posting garage sale signs on public property is
strictly prohibited
and can result in a
$250 fine
. Be sure to keep signage on private property only.
