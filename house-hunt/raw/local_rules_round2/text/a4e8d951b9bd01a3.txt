Rabies & Animal Control Services
The Department conducts a free annual rabies vaccination program for residents' dogs and cats in January of each year. This year's clinic will be held on Wednesday, January 14, 2026,  from 7 pm - 8:30 pm at the East Rutherford Civic Center. Licenses may be obtained at that time as well. Appointments are
mandatory
-
No walk-ins
!  Please call 201-933-3444, ext. 509, to schedule
State law requires all dogs to be licensed. All dog licenses expire on December 31st and must be renewed in January of each year. Proof of valid rabies vaccination must be shown and valid through November of the current year. Fees are $10 for spayed/neutered and $13 for non-spayed/neutered - Cash or check only
The Borough of East Rutherford contracts with TYCO for animal control services. They can be reached at 201-652-4554
