Resident Parking Information
Each resident parking permit is valid for one year, expiring on December 31. All permits must be renewed by January 31 of the following year. The annual fee is $10.00 per registered vehicle for residents of streets or portions thereof where residential parking restrictions apply.
Required Documentation for Each Vehicle:
When registering your vehicle through the portal, the following documents are required:
Valid
driver’s license
Vehicle
registration
Proof of
insurance
Permits will only be issued for vehicles owned or principally operated by the resident. All documents provided must include the current address.
