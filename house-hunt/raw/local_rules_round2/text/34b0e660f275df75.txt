Request residential and visitor parking permits
There are certain streets where parking is limited or in high demand. Parking permits give residents (and their guests) priority to park in available parking spaces.
Permit types
Each household is entitled to an unlimited number of residential parking permits and 2 visitor parking permits.
Residential permits
Residents on streets with restricted parking must register for free parking permit(s), which are:
Issued for any vehicle(s) owned by a resident of that street
Required to be displayed on the vehicle's left rear window
Valid for 1 year
Visitor permits
When you register for a residential parking permit, you will automatically receive 2 visitor parking permits.
The visitor permits are portable and must hang on the vehicle's rearview mirror
You should reuse these permits. Remember to collect the visitor permit when your guest(s) leave.
Exemptions
These vehicles do not need a permit to park on the street while providing services to a residence:
Contractors, service and delivery vehicles showing the name and address of a commercial business
Emergency vehicles, public service, telephone and other public utility vehicles
How to register
Confirm that you need a parking permit. See the list of roads below or
call the Clerk's office
.
Gather copies of the registration cards for each vehicle
Gather copies of the driver's licenses for each vehicle owner
Submit the Residential Parking Permit application online
or in person at Borough Hall. You will need to provide copies of the registration cards and licenses at this time.
Streets with restricted parking
You need a permit to park on these streets, from 9 am to 8 pm:
Adams Street, both sides, from Post Street to Eastern States Parkway
Davenport Street, west side, from West Cliff Street to West High Street (except for the most northerly parking stall which is reserved for the St. Thomas A.M.E. Zion Church)
East Cliff Street, both sides, between Grove Street and North Bridge Street
Fairmont Avenue, both sides, entire length
Grant Avenue, both sides, entire length
Lee Way, both sides, entire length
Lisa Terrace, both sides, from Lee Way to Reimer Street
Madison Street, both sides, from Post Street to Eastern States Parkway
Mechanic Street, west side, entire length
Monroe Street, both sides, from Post Street to Eastern States Parkway
You need a permit to park on these streets, from 7 am to 4 pm:
Mastogen Drive, both sides, entire length
Mountain Avenue, both sides, from Altamont Place and West Cliff Street to Prospect Street
North Doughty Avenue, both sides, from West Cliff Street to Prospect Street
North Middaugh Street, both sides, from Altamont Place to Prospect Street
Prospect Drive, both sides, entire length
Prospect Street, both sides, from North Doughty Avenue to North Middaugh Street
Speer Street, both sides, entire length
West Cliff Street, both sides, from North Doughty Avenue to Mountain Avenue
West Summit Street, both sides, from Mercer Street to North Middaugh Street
