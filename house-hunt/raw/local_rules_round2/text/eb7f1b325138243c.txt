General Parking Info
There is a 3-hour limit on all General Parking paid by kiosk/meter.
Residents may purchase one-day parking passes for use in the Train Station lot, 3rd Level of Hudson Street Garage, and Cottage Place Lot.
Kiosks/meters in the Cottage lot do NOT have the 3-hour limit.
Payments must be made at the kiosk/meter or through ParkMobile.

Overnight Parking Permit Info
Overnight Parking Permits can be obtained at the Village Hall Reception Desk
Overnight Parking Permits are available for sale to all residents living in the Central Business District (except for those in the multi-family housing developments), who do not have parking provided to them by their landlord.
The hours for Overnight Parking Permits are from 8 pm to 8 am all nights.
Locations for the Overnight Parking Permits:
In the Prospect lot bounded by Van Neste Square, Dayton Street, and Prospect Street;
In the Hudson Street Garage 2nd level only;
In the Wilsey Square parking site bounded by the New Jersey Transit Railroad and Wilsey Square;
In the Cottage lot bounded by Cottage Place and East Ridgewood Avenue.
Overnight Parking Permits are issued for 3 months ($85) or 6 months ($170) and must be paid by
Cash
or
Check
only.  Checks can be made out to

Free - Ridgewood Resident Annual Sticker
The free Ridgewood Resident Sticker must be obtained in order to purchase Premium or Non-Premium Parking Permits, and use One Day Parking Passes.
The free Resident Sticker is also required to park a vehicle at the Train Station lot (Station Plaza), and if the vehicle owner does not display a Premium Parking Permit or One Day Parking Pass, the Ridgewood Resident must pay for parking by kiosk or ParkMobile.
A new sticker must be obtained each calendar year.
Free - Non-Resident Annual Sticker
The free Non-Resident Sticker must be obtained in order to purchase Premium or Non-Premium Parking Permits.
This sticker may not be used by Non-Residents to park at the Train Station lot (Station Plaza), unless the vehicle owner displays a Premium Parking Permit.
A new sticker must be obtained each calendar year.
senior parking permit
Senior Parking Permits for
Ridgewood residents
age 65 and older
are available at the reception desk at Village Hall.
The annual permit allows eligible residents to park in the Central Business District - up to three hours a day - for a flat fee of
$75 a year
. A prorated permit for the remainder of 2026 is available for
$37.50
.
To purchase a permit, please bring:
Your NJ driver's license with your Ridgewood address
Your vehicle registration with your name and Ridgewood address
Exact change, check or credit card
Please note: Senior Parking Permits are not valid in 15-minute parking spaces, commuter lots or on the fourth floor of the Hudson Street Parking Garage.
