Snow Removal
In anticipation of snow all On Street permit holders are required to relocate their vehicle(s) to the Third Street Garage (You can park on any level). To allow for plowing/snow removal.
Any resident that is looking for off street parking, due to inclement weather can also go to the Third Street Garage (park on any level).
There is no fee required.
If you have any questions, please contact the Parking Authority during business hours Monday-Friday 8:30am to 4:30pm.
If our office is closed please contact the South Orange Police Department at 973-763-3000 with any questions.
