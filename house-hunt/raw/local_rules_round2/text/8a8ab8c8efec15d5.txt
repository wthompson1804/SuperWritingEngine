Welcome! This section connects residents with the most frequently used City services, programs and resources. With a population of over 141,000, the City of Elizabeth is New Jersey’s fourth largest city. As the Union County seat, Elizabeth contains most major governmental offices and courts servicing the County. Whether you're looking for trash collection schedules, permits, or community events, you'll find everything you need here.
