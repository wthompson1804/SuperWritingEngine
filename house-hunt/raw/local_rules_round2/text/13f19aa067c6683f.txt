Holiday Giving Tree 2026
Are you interested in sponsoring a local family this holiday season by providing gifts for under the tree? Stop by Borough Hall, 230 Hamilton Street, anytime between 8:30 AM-4:30 PM, Monday 11/23 to Thursday 12/10 and choose an envelope from the 2026 Holiday Giving Tree!
Unwrapped gifts and wrapping materials must be dropped off to Borough Hall on or before Friday, 12/11 at 4:00 PM.
