Animal Control /Animal Shelter
Animal Control Officers are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, and are responsible for enforcing town ordinances pertaining to animals.
This includes the investigation of animal welfare concerns and referral to a Humane Law Enforcement Officer when appropriate.
We do not trap and remove healthy wildlife including snakes, possum, skunk, groundhogs, foxes, deer and healthy raccoons.
Services Provided:
Picking up contained stray dogs. We normally do not pick up stray cats as many cats are indoor/outdoor owned cats. We will give them a few days to return home. Please never feed a stray cat.
Picking up injured/sick/stray domesticated animals (dogs & cats) and injured or sick wildlife.
Removing wildlife from living quarters (note: attics and walls are not considered living quarters, and a local pest control company should be contacted.
Investigation of animal bites and quarantining animals involved in bites.
Animal nuisance complaint investigations and inquiries
Picking up deceased domestic stray animals (dogs/cats)
Investigation of welfare concerns and referral Humane Law Enforcement Officer when indicated.
Provide education and oversight about TNR (Trap, Neuter, Return)
