Municipal Parking Lots
Posted January 05, 2026
Be advised that payment for parking at any one of the Township's Municipal Parking lots is now required 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.  You can pay for parking by using the ParkMobile App.  Please find the nearest Park Mobile sign and use your phone to process the payment.  Simply scan the QR code with your phone and follow the instructions.  If you do not have a smartphone, please call 833-785-1200 and process your payment with ParkMobile customer service.
