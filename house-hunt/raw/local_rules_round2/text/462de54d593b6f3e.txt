Mission Statement
To create and establish off-street parking for those using the Fairfield Center and Southport train stations.
About Us
The Parking Department of Fairfield manages parking, parking lots and station houses at the
Fairfield Center
and
Southport train stations
.
Those utilizing the Fairfield Center Train Station may park at the Fairfield Center Station Lot.
Those utilizing the Southport Train Station may park at the Southport Station Lot or Trinity Church Lot.
The Parking Department:
Issues
Parking Permits
for Fairfield Center and Southport Train Stations.
Maintains permit
Waitlists
for train station parking.
Collects
Day Parking
fees (payable at the Kiosk or via Text to Pay).
Manages
Overnight Parking
.
Makes capital improvements to the Station Houses and Lots.
Rules & Regulations pertaining to parking can be found
here
.
The Fairfield Center and Southport stations are owned by the State and maintained by the
Parking Department
.
