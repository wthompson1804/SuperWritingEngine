Animal Control
The East Brunswick Police Department Animal Control Office contracts with the City of New Brunswick to provide animal control services to the Township of East Brunswick. Animal Control will take appropriate action to address issues or concerns and can educate residents about their rights and possible courses of action.
Animal Control will
NOT
handle
nuisance wildlife
or pest problems. The capture of nuisance wildlife (i.e., healthy-appearing raccoons not threatening humans, fox dens, wildlife in attics, groundhogs burrowing in yards, etc.) is NOT a service that Animal Control is required to provide. These problems can best be solved by a local exterminator or pest control operator. Animal Control will only respond to sick or injured wildlife posing a public safety or health threat. If the wildlife is passing through or is located under your shed or porch without showing signs of injury, illness, or aggression, there is no need to call Animal Control.
If you have an animal-related issue, contact the Animal Control Office at 732-390-6960 or email
AnimalControl@ebpd.net
. If it is an emergency, contact the East Brunswick Police Department by dialing 9-1-1.
Animal Control services provided to the Township include:
Responding to sick, attacking, or abnormally acting wildlife.
Rescuing stray animals and returning them to their owners if located.
Responding to domestic animals attacking or threatening public safety.
Investigating animal neglect and cruelty complaints.
Investigating animal bites and handling quarantines when/if necessary.
Removal of DOA wildlife carcasses from Township public property (property owners are responsible for removing deceased wildlife from their property).
Canvass for unlicensed dogs and cats and take enforcement action when necessary.
Enforcing animal-related Township ordinances and State Statutes.
Animal Control is
NOT
responsible for and
WILL NOT
:
Capture or trap nuisance wildlife or feral cats.
Pick up dead wildlife carcasses from the roadside (e.g., raccoons, opossums, squirrels, etc.).
Respond to snakes located outside of homes.
Pick up exotic pets that are under the jurisdiction of NJ Division of Fish, Game, & Wildlife.
Go on roofs, go under porches, or climb trees for the purposes of retrieving animals.
Take surrendered animals.
Licensing Requirement
Under
Township Ordinance Chapter 83
and New Jersey state law, all dogs and cats seven (7) months of age or older and kept in the Township of East Brunswick for more than ten (10) days are required to be licensed annually. Licensing your pets with the township ensures they are up to date on their rabies vaccinations and helps prevent the spread of disease within our pet population. Rabies is a fatal but preventable disease that affects warm-blooded mammals, including humans.
If you need a rabies vaccination, the township offers a free clinic in November each year.
If you need a rabies vaccination for your dog or cat, Middlesex County offers free clinics year-round at various locations. For details, please visit the
Middlesex County Inspection Division of Health Services webpage
.
For more information about licensing, please visit the
Animal Licenses webpage
.
Animal Cruelty
Illegal animal cruelty can include intentional abuse, such as beating or mutilating an animal, but it can also include neglect, such as failing to provide proper shelter, food, or medical care. In New Jersey, the relevant laws can be found under Chapter 22 of Title 4 of the Revised Statutes.
The East Brunswick Police Department has a designated Humane Law Enforcement Officer (HLEO) whose responsibilities are in addition to their normal day-to-day function as a police officer within the Township of East Brunswick. The HLEO is responsible for animal welfare within the Township of East Brunswick and is authorized to investigate and sign complaints, arrest violators, and otherwise act as an officer in the detection, apprehension, and arrest of offenders against the animal welfare and animal cruelty laws of the State and the municipality's ordinances. If you suspect animal cruelty or abuse, contact the East Brunswick Police Department at 732-390-6900 for assistance.
To report animal cruelty or abuse involving livestock (including horses, cattle, and poultry), contact the New Jersey Department of Agriculture, Division of Animal Health, by calling 609-671-6400 or via email at
state.veterinarian@ag.nj.gov
.
