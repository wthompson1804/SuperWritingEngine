Village of Hempstead – Public Hearing Notice: Proposed Homestead Tax Option
The Village of Hempstead Board of Trustees will hold a public hearing on Tuesday, November 17, 2026, at 6:15 PM at Village Hall to consider the adoption of a proposed Local Law authorizing a Homestead Tax Option pursuant to New York State Real Property Tax Law § 1903.
The proposed legislation would establish separate real property tax rates for homestead (residential) and non-homestead (non-residential) property classes following a qualifying property revaluation project and certification as an approved assessing unit.
The purpose of this proposal is to help mitigate disproportionate shifts in the property tax burden for residential homeowners, subject to applicable eligibility requirements, class share adjustments, and other provisions of law.
Public Hearing Details:
Date: Tuesday, November 17, 2026
Time: 6:15 PM
Location: Village Hall
Address: 99 James A. Garner Way, Hempstead, NY 11550
The proposed Local Law is available for public inspection at the Office of the Village Clerk, located at Village Hall, during regular business hours.
All interested persons will have an opportunity to be heard regarding this proposal.
The Village of Hempstead encourages residents and interested members of the public to attend and participate in this public hearing.
By Order of the Board of Trustees, Incorporated Village of Hempstead.

Village of Hempstead – Public Hearing Notice
The Village of Hempstead Board of Trustees will hold a public hearing on Tuesday, November 17, 2026, at 6:00 PM at Village Hall to consider a proposed Local Law amending Chapter 119, Article VI, entitled “Veterans Exemption.”
The proposed legislation would add a new provision, § 119-31, entitled “Full Exemption for Seriously Disabled Veterans,” pursuant to New York State Real Property Tax Law § 458-a(11).
The proposal seeks to provide a full real property tax exemption for qualifying residential properties serving as the primary residence of veterans who have been determined by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs to be permanently and totally disabled as a result of military service, subject to applicable eligibility requirements and limitations.

Proposed Amendments to the Village’s Cannabis Opt-Out Law
The Village of Hempstead will hold a public hearing regarding a proposed local law to amend
Chapter 140, Article I of the Village Code
, which currently addresses the prohibition of adult-use cannabis retail dispensaries and on-site consumption establishments.
The proposed legislation would partially repeal Local Law No. 1 of 2022, allowing the Village to participate in New York State’s adult-use cannabis program specifically to authorize and regulate adult-use retail dispensaries.
Key provisions of the proposed local law include:
Authorization of Retail Dispensaries:
The Village would partially reverse its previous opt-out decision and permit adult-use cannabis retail dispensaries to operate within authorized areas, subject to applicable Village regulations and approvals.
Continued Prohibition of On-Site Consumption:
The proposed law would maintain the Village’s prohibition on adult-use cannabis establishments where customers consume cannabis on the premises. Local Law No. 1 of 2022 would remain in effect regarding this prohibition.
Amendments to the Village Code:
The legislation would revise Chapter 140 to remove references to the prohibition of retail dispensaries while retaining the provisions prohibiting on-site consumption establishments.
State and Local Regulatory Authority:
The proposed changes would establish the legal framework for the Village to authorize and regulate retail dispensaries in accordance with New York State Cannabis Law and applicable local requirements.
Effective Date:
The proposed law addresses when the amendments would take effect following filing with the New York State Secretary of State, subject to applicable referendum requirements.

Proposed Local Law on Cannabis Retail Dispensaries
The Village of Hempstead will hold a public hearing tonight, October 13, 2026, regarding a proposed local law to amend Chapter 141 of the Village Code concerning cannabis retail establishments and business operations.
The proposed legislation would repeal the Village’s prior prohibition on adult-use cannabis retail dispensaries and establish regulations governing their operation within the Village.
Key provisions of the proposed local law include:
Permitted Locations: Retail dispensaries would be allowed only in designated Business A, Business B, Business C, and Industrial Districts.
Restrictions Near Youth Facilities: Dispensaries would be prohibited within 500 feet of designated public youth facilities, including Brierley Park, Campbell Park, Kennedy Memorial Park, and other Village recreational facilities.
Hours of Operation: Retail dispensaries could operate Monday through Saturday from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM and Sunday from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
Parking Requirements: Establishments would need to meet applicable off-street parking requirements or qualify under specified provisions for access to designated municipal parking fields.
Application and Review Process: Applicants would be subject to municipal notification, Building Department review, pre-submission conferences, and Planning Board review when applicable.
Business Activity Restrictions: Cannabis cultivation, processing, manufacturing, testing, distribution, warehousing, and certain wholesale activities would be restricted to designated Light Manufacturing and Industrial Districts, subject to applicable requirements.
Special Events: Cannabis-related showcase events on Village property, public streets, sidewalks, and municipal parking areas would require Village approval and applicable state authorization.
Enforcement and Penalties: Violations could result in civil penalties of up to $2,000 for a first offense and up to $5,000 for subsequent offenses, as well as suspension, revocation, or denial of applicable permits and approvals.
Residents are encouraged to attend tonight’s public hearing to learn more about the proposed legislation and share their comments with the Mayor and Board of Trustees.

Sign the Petition: Resident Parking for Hempstead
The Village of Hempstead is asking the New York State Legislature for permission to create a resident parking permit program. Under current State law, the Village cannot reserve street parking for residents without State approval.
If approved, the Village could create resident parking zones, issue permits to residents and passes for their guests, and enforce parking rules so neighborhood spaces stay open for the people who live here. The Village is also updating its overnight parking rules, which would require a permit to park on the street overnight, tentatively from 2:00 a.m. to 6:00 a.m.
Residents are encouraged to sign the petition and stay informed about the upcoming public hearing at Hempstead Village Hall, 99 James A. Garner Road. The public hearing originally scheduled for Tuesday, October 6, 2026, at 5:45 PM has been postponed and will be rescheduled. A new date and time will be announced.

Public Notice: Mirschel Park Community Center Project
The Village of Hempstead is providing public notice regarding the Finding of No Significant Impact (FONSI) and Notice of Intent to Request Release of Funds for the proposed Mirschel Park Community Center Project.
The Village plans to request the release of $2 million in Community Development Funding/Congressionally Directed Funding to support construction of a new community center at Mirschel Park, 99 Atlantic Avenue, Hempstead, NY.
The planned two-story facility will include recreational activities, meeting rooms, a kitchen, accessible spaces, and a covered outdoor pavilion for community programming, gatherings, and picnics. The project is intended to expand programs and community resources, particularly for school-aged children and residents in the surrounding area.
Public Comments: Written comments regarding the Environmental Review Record (ERR) must be received by October 6, 2026 to be considered prior to the Village authorizing submission of its request for release of funds.
Residents and interested parties are encouraged to review the complete notice for information regarding the environmental review, public comment process, and procedures for objections.
Please read the full notice below:
NOTICE OF FINDING OF NO SIGNFICANT IMPACT AND NOTICE OF INTENT TO REQUEST RELEASE OF FUNDS
September 21, 2026
Village of Hempstead
99 James Garner Way
Hempstead, NY 11550
516-489-3400
These notices satisfy two separate but related procedural requirements for activities to be undertaken by the Village of Hempstead.
REQUEST FOR RELEASE OF FUNDS
On or about October 7, 2026 the Village of Hempstead will submit a request to the HUD administering agency for the release of Community Development Funds/Congressional Directed Funding to undertake a project known as the Mirschel Community Recreational Center for the purpose of constructing the Mirschel Park Community Center (Community Center). The community center will be a new facility that will allow the Village to sponsor new programs, especially for school aged children, and bring more community resources to disadvantaged residents living in this dense residential area.
As planned, the new community center will be a two-story building with a full basement that will be used for recreational activities, meeting rooms and a kitchen.
The roof will support building mechanical equipment and a small solar photovoltaic power generation system designed to reduce the energy costs of the Community Center. All spaces within the building and access to the building are designed to be accessible to the physically impaired. The exterior of the building will be sheathed in a brick masonry base with a glass and exterior insulation storefront wall system. The roofing systems are a combination of rubber membrane and standing seam roofing materials.
Adjacent to the Community Center, a 400-sf open outdoor covered pavilion is planned to provide cover from the sun and rain and will be used for picnics and community programming and gatherings. Park visitors will be able to view ongoing activities on the Public Green from this location. Outdoor restrooms access is also located under this structure.
The Village was awarded $2,000,000 in Community Development Funding/Congressionally Directed Funding and the project will be located in the Village of Hempstead in an existing park located on 99 Atlantic Avenue, Hempstead, NY 11550.
FINDING OF NO SIGNIFICANT IMPACT
The Village of Hempstead has determined that the project will have no significant impact on the human environment. Therefore, an Environmental Impact Statement under the National Environmental Policy Act of 1969 (NEPA) is not required. Additional project information is contained in the Environmental Review Record (ERR) on file at Village of Hempstead, Clerk’s Department, 99 James A. Garner Way, Hempstead, NY 11550. and may be examined or copied weekdays 8:15A.M to 4:15P.M.
PUBLIC COMMENTS
Any individual, group, or agency may submit written comments on the ERR to the Village of Hempstead. All comments received by October 6, 2026 will be considered by the Village of Hempstead prior to authorizing submission of a request for release of funds. Comments should specify which Notice they are addressing.
ENVIRONMENTAL CERTIFICATION
The Village of Hempstead certifies to HUD that Keisha Marshall in her capacity as Village Attorney consents to accept the jurisdiction of the Federal Courts if an action is brought to enforce responsibilities in relation to the environmental review process and that these responsibilities have been satisfied. HUD’s approval of the certification satisfies its responsibilities under NEPA and related laws and authorities and allows the Village of Hempstead to use Program funds.
OBJECTIONS TO RELEASE OF FUNDS
HUD will accept objections to its release of fund and the Village of Hempstead certification for a period of fifteen days following the anticipated submission date or its actual receipt of the request (whichever is later) only if they are on one of the following bases: (a) the certification was not executed by the Certifying Officer of the Village of Hempstead; (b) the Village of Hempstead has omitted a step or failed to make a decision or finding required by HUD regulations at 24 CFR part 58; (c) the grant recipient or other participants in the development process have committed funds, incurred costs or undertaken activities not authorized by 24 CFR Part 58 before approval of a release of funds by HUD; or (d) another Federal agency acting pursuant to 40 CFR Part 1504 has submitted a written finding that the project is unsatisfactory from the standpoint of environmental quality. Objections must be prepared and submitted in accordance with the required procedures (24 CFR Part 58, Sec. 58.76) and shall be addressed to HUD administration office at New York Regional Office, 26 Federal Plaza, Suite 3541, New York, NY 10278. Emailed objections may be sent to CPDRROFNYC@hud.gov. Potential objectors should contact HUD to verify the actual last day of the objection period.
Keisha Marshall
Village Attorney
