Overnight Parking
There is no overnight parking (2am to 6am) anywhere in
South Orange without temporary permission from the
Parking Authority or
Police Department or a valid parking permit.

Short-Term Overnight Parking
Please give our office a call if you have a visitor that needs overnight parking. We will be able to determine whether they are eligible for street parking or municipal lot parking.
Call South Orange Parking Authority between 8:30am - 4:30pm.
Call South Orange Police Department before/after SOPA's business hours: (973) 763- 3000
There is a $5.00 fee per night for overnight parking for visitors/guest. You can pay in person or over the phone with a credit card during regular business hours.
Permit Parking
To purchase overnight parking permits, you must be a resident and live in a qualifying residence.
For details please see the residential parking permit tab.
