Parking Meters
Overview
Parking Meters are in effect from May 1st to September 30th.
The City of Long Branch has a smart meter parking system powered by the ParkMobile app. The new system uses pay-by-plate, doing away with numbered parking spots and increasing the ease and options for paying. ParkMobile meters will accept Cash and Credit. Apple Pay and Google Pay can be used via the app. Utilizing the app further simplifies the process by storing your credit card and plate information so you only need to enter information once. Meters are located in several areas throughout the City, including North Beach, Beachfront North, Pier Village, and on the oceanfront from Brighton to Morris Avenues.
ParkMobile allows you to pay for parking in 3 simple steps:
Select Location
Choose Duration
Confirm Payment
Benefits of ParkMobile
Get fast and free access to the easy-to-use parking app.
GPS is used to automatically identify where you are parked.
Receive smart notifications during your parking sessions.
Pay only for the actual parking time.
Download the ParkMobile app

Text-To-Pay
The City has added the ParkMobile Text-To-Pay parking system. Please see posted signs in metered areas for details and instructions.

Time Limits
No limits. You may add money to the meter to stay longer.
Metered Hours in Pier Village are 10 am to Midnight.
Metered Hours outside of Pier Village are 9 am to 5 pm.
Remember to obey other parking restrictions such as no parking in Pier Village between the hours of 3 am and 5 am.
Handicapped Parking
All handicapped parking spaces are free of charge with placard or plates. (As per State Law and the ADA act)
If all handicapped spaces are taken, you may park in another space but are required by NJ state law to pay the maximum time allotment on that meter one time. Once that is paid, you may park in that spot for up to 24 hours with a handicapped plate or placard.
Questions about the meters? Call 732-571-5684
