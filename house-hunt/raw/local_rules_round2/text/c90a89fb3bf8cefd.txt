Hello and thank you for your interest in the Hamden Farmers Market. The Market is an annual event held outdoors on Thursdays between June and September, weather permitting.
The goal of the Market is to stimulate Hamden's small-business economy by connecting fresh, quality local produce, artisanal goods, and resources directly to our residents, communities and neighbors.
Markets are organized in two halves, with some vendors switching out halfway through the season. Join the Market Members list in the side bar to the left under "Visit the Market" to stay up-to-date with Market news, activities and guests.
Markets will be held in the big field at Town Center Park this year to allow for proper social distancing with the main entrance being the Hamden Middle School at 2623 Dixwell Ave. Town Center Park is located at 2761 Dixwell Ave., Hamden CT 06518
