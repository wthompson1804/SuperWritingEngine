Recreation Department
The Recreation Department offers a wide variety of high quality and affordable year-round leisure programs for Springfield residents of all ages, as well as extensive senior citizen services and a Community Pool. Programs are intended to support, regulate, and encourage participation in a healthy and active lifestyle; and to facilitate the use and enjoyment of Township parks and recreational facilities for the residents of Springfield.

Community Pool
The Summit Area YMCA is the operator of the Springfield Community Pool in summers 2021-2026.  Information on pool membership, Camp Cannonball, Camp Cannundus, Sports Camp, Swim Team, Swim Lessons, Lap Swim and other summer programs is available online at
https://www.thesay.org/scp
.  You can also contact the Summit Area YMCA to rent the Springfield Community Pool clubhouse party room all year long!  Questions?  Call the Summit Area YMCA at (908) 273-3330.

Registration is open for Fall 2026 after-school extra-curricular activities and camps, run by private partners of the Springfield Recreation Department. CHILDREN & TEENS
Our kids are talented, hard-working and full of good ideas. We encourage and empower them to bring their goals to fruition with our many programs and coordinated recreation spaces and events. Take a look at all we have to offer and let us know if you have any questions or suggestions for our staff. If you haven’t been to the remodeled community pool yet., this is the perfect time to go!
It’s our pleasure to serve the community and we take playing here very seriously. Come join the fun!
