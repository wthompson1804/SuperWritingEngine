Hamden Animal Shelter
Hamden Animal Shelter
Project Description
The Town of Hamden currently leases space from the Town of North Haven to use their animal shelter. For a town of over 60,000 people to lack an animal shelter is abnormal. The Town's Animal Control team, Police Department Administration, and Engineering have worked with a consultant to build out a needs assessment describing a facility that will meet the Town's needs for an animal shelter. That study is shown in links on the relevant documents section of this page.
Hamden’s Engineering Department is spearheading the construction of the animal shelter at 466 Putnam Ave in collaboration with Hamden Animal Control, Hamden Police Department, and the Mayor’s Office. An analysis to inform a conceptual floor plan and a site design at 466 Putnam Avenue have been completed. This site plan is shown in the capital plan presented for FY 2025-2026. We are now preparing to issue a Request for Qualifications (RFQ) for the design of the animal shelter this fall.
A space needs assessment and preliminary construction cost estimate was prepared by a consultant (DLI) under the direction of Engineering, Animal Control, Mayor’s Office, and Police Department. The total estimated amount needed to construct the animal shelter is $2,635,400 +/- $514,080. This estimate is based on co-locating the Animal Shelter and New Fire Station 2 at the same property and leverages the resources each project brings for site planning, design and construction. The space needs assessment is also included in the FY 2025-2026 capital plan submission.
Funding Stack (10/08/2025)
The following outlines funds to be used in the construction of the animal shelter:
• Animal Shelter Gift Fund: $165,343.44
• LoCIP: $250,000.00
• Animal Facility Capital Account # 36663-5427: $147,738.00
• Reimbursements for open space from the State of CT at 370 Brooksvale Ave Rear: $432,250.00
• Proceeds from Sale of Property at 490 Main St.: $290,000.00
The above amounts to $1,285,331.44.
