Overnight Parking
No vehicles are permitted to be parked on any public streets or public parking lots Within the Village of Ridgewood between 2:00AM and 6:00AM.   Village ordinance 265-36.   If you would like to request permission to park your vehicle on a Village street or municipal parking lot overnight, please complete the form in the link below.  Ensure you understand all rules and limitations prior to submitting a request.
