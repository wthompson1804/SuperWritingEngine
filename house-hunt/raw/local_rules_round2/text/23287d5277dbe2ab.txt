Locations
East Brunswick Township operates two park and ride facilities for commuters.
The Transportation and Commerce Center, located at 551 Old Bridge Turnpike, East Brunswick, NJ 08816 near Route 18 North and Tices Lane
Neilson Plaza, located at 7 Tower Center Boulevard, East Brunswick, NJ 08816, off Route 18 North and Naricon Place near NJ Turnpike Exit 9
Hours & Availability
The Transportation and Commerce Center is open Monday through Friday and has over 1400 spaces for daily parking at $5 per day.
Neilson Plaza is open 7 days a week and has over 1000 daily parking spaces at $5 per day.
Service
Suburban Transit provides continuous rush hour service for commuters at each location. Buses travel either directly to New York City’s Port Authority Bus Terminal or downtown Manhattan.
Those seeking to travel intrastate can do so via one of Suburban’s many bus routes to the Port Authority Bus Terminal or downtown Manhattan.
In addition, those who need to travel to Newark or Kennedy Airports can do so by parking at Neilson Plaza and utilizing the Princeton Airporter van located next door at the Hilton Hotel.
