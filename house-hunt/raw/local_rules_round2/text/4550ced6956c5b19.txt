South Orange Parking Authority (Board)
The South Orange Parking Authority is an independent Authority, established in 1979. We are responsible for the enforcement and operation of the municipal parking lots, metered spaces, overnight on-street parking, commuter shuttle jitney buses and senior bus services. Its operations are funded by permit and meter revenues. The Parking Authority Board of Commissioners meets on the third Tuesday of every month by Virtual conference call at  7:00 p.m.
