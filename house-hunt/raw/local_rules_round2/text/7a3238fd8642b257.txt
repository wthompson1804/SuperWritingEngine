Dog Licenses (Mail-in Registration)
Dog License
Renewals begin June 1st. The renewal period is June 1st-June 30th annually. Please do not renew your license before June 1st.
Dogs are licensed each year during June and can be obtained using online registration and credit card payment - Click on the Dog License Webform
T
here is a
late fee
for renewals received after July 1st. A fee of $1 is charged for every month you are late. New dogs are exempt from any late charges.
You can also mail in your registration using the form.
All new and renewal licenses require a copy of your dog's current rabies certificate from the vet. Licenses for a new dog that has not been licensed in the prior year also require a copy of the spayed/neutered certificate from the vet, if applicable.
If licensing via walk-in registration, be sure to bring the required documents and payment [see fee schedule below] with you at the time of application. Walk-in registration is discouraged during the COVID-19 crisis.
If licensing via mail-in registration, be sure to enclose a self-addressed, stamped envelope - in addition to the required documents and proper payment [see fee schedule below.
To obtain dog licenses, please contact
Beacon Falls Town Clerk's Office
10 Maple Avenue
Beacon Falls, CT 06403
