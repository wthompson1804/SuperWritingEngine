Apply for a pet license for your cat or dog
If you have a dog or cat, you need a pet license.
Need to know
Dogs cost $12 for neutered or $15 for non-neutered
Cats cost $11 for neutered or $14 for non-neutered
You need to renew your license every year
Before you start
If your pet is spayed our neutered, you need to provide proof when you apply.
You also need to provide a rabies certificate:
For cats, the certificate needs to be valid the day you apply for your license
For dogs, the certificate needs to be valid through October 31 of the licensing year
Steps
Complete the online pet license application
Pay the license fee
Pay for a cat license
with your bank account (ACH debit transaction)
Pay for a dog license
with your bank account (ACH debit transaction)
Mail or drop off a check payable to “Borough of Somerville”
Pay with exact cash at Borough Hall
What's next
We will send your pet license and tag by mail.
You need to renew your license every year:
Renew your cat license
by June 30
Renew your dog license
by January 31
