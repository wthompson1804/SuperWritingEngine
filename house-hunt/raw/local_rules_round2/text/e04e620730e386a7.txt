Dog Licenses
Connecticut law requires all dogs 6 months and older to be licensed annually. Licenses are valid July 1 through June 30th. June is renewal month.
License or Renew Your Dog License Your Way
The Hamden Town Clerk’s Office offers several ways to license or renew your dog license.
You may renew or apply:
In person at the Town Clerk’s Office
By mail
Using the secure Town Clerk drop box
Online, for eligible renewals only
We are always happy to assist residents in person, and we love seeing you and your pets’ paperwork come through our office. The online renewal option is simply an added convenience for residents who prefer to renew from home.
New Online Renewal Option Beginning June 1, 2026
Beginning June 1, 2026, residents with existing Hamden dog licenses can renew online via Hamden's secure renewal portal.
Online renewals will be available
June 1 through August 30, 2026
.  Residents are encouraged to renew by
June 30
to avoid late fees.
Before You Begin
Please have:
Dog tag number
Last name associated with the license
Updated rabies information, if applicable
Payment method
Benefits include:
Renew anytime, anywhere
Secure online payment
Faster processing
No trip to Hamden Government Center required
Please note
: Online transactions include a $3.50 convenience fee charged by the payment processor and a $0.59 postage fee. These fees are paid directly to the vendor and are not retained by the Town of Hamden.

Welcoming a New Dog Into Your Household?
If you recently welcomed a dog into your household, Connecticut law requires licensing within 30 days.
New dog licenses require:
Current rabies certificate signed by your veterinarian
Spay/neuter documentation (if applicable)
Completed application
Appropriate payment
Dog License Mail-In Application (PDF)
.

Tags
A dog without a tag is presumed unlicensed and not protected by law.
A dog tag must not be used on any dog other than the dog described in the license bearing the corresponding numbers.
Dog Park Usage
Users of Hamden’s Dog Park must have their dogs legally licensed to use the Dog Park at Ridge Road and Waite Street (across from Bassett Park). Dog litter bags are provided. For rules regarding the Dog Park, visit the
Hamden Dog Park website
.
