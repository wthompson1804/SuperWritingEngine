WELCOME TO OUR RECREATION PAGE
Red Bank Recreation is dedicated to enriching our community by offering high-quality programs, activities, and special events for residents of all ages.
Throughout the year, we provide exciting opportunities for youth and adults to stay active, build friendships, and have fun.
The winter season kicks off with our popular indoor soccer programs for children ages 5-17, giving young athletes the chance to sharpen their skills and enjoy friendly competition. Basketball programs, operated by Jersey Fusion, are also offered during the winter and may extend into the spring, summer, or fall seasons.
As the weather warms, spring brings a variety of opportunities for young participants, including youth track and field, as well as baseball and softball programs for all age groups. Beyond sports, Red Bank Recreation proudly hosts community events that bring neighbors together, such as the annual
Spring Egg Hunt
and the
Community Pride Celebration
at Riverside Gardens Park, creating lasting memories and strengthening community connections.
The town also hosts
The Red Bank 5K Classic Race
, which is organized by a committee that involves different entities such as Red Bank River Center, YMCA, Parks and Recreation and others. The race features 5K Run/Walk, Kids Broad Street Dash, post event activities and after party. Some of the proceeds go to our Parks and Recreation Dept. and The Community YMCA to better serve our community programs.
For our busiest time of the year, the summer season, we offer camps for children and the famous Summer Series in the Park. Our Summer Series include movies, fitness and jazz. We welcome everyone to come to Riverside Gardens Park to enjoy these free events. Special thanks to the
Community YMCA
and
Jazz Arts Project
for coordinating the Summer Series with us.
During the fall season, we prepare an amazing Halloween Parade with live music, bands, games and much more. Recreational soccer for children Pre-K to 8th grade. Flag football is also offered for our youth by the Red Bank AYF. Other events offered in the fall are Hispanic Heritage Celebration, Halloween Movie night in the Park, Evening Adult basketball, Buddyball program and Evening classes at the Senior Center for Adults and Youth.
To stay up to date with Red Bank events and programs be sure to subscribe to our
Borough website
. You can receive alerts about weather, traffic, events, borough hall meetings, ordinances, and much more. Parks and Recreation is also on social media!
