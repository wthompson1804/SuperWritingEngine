Dog Licenses
As per Connecticut General Statue-22-238 all dogs must be licensed when they become 6 months old.
A new dog may be licensed anytime throughout the year (see Application below).
Dog License Renewals are mailed out in June every year for dogs that were licensed the previous year.
Required Documentation:
Proof of current Rabies Vaccination
Application to license a dog for the first time in Oxford (see below)
Optional: Spay/Neuter Certificate
Fee(s):
$8   Spayed / Neutered
$19 Not Spayed / Not Neutered
$1   Late Fees being in July and accrue monthly
$2   To License / Renew through the mail please include a self-addressed stamped envelope
or
an additional $2 for postage & handling.
Acceptable Forms of Payment:
Cash
Check made out to Oxford Town Clerk
We
do not
accept credit or debit cards
Ways to Submit Request:
In person during business hours: Monday - Thursday 9am-5pm or Friday 8am-noon
Mail: Oxford Town Clerk 486 Oxford Rd, Oxford, CT 06478
Drop Box attached to the front outside of our building that can be used 24/7
