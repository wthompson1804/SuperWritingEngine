Connecticut Energy Assistance Program
WEST HAVEN, Oct. 15, 2026 — The city is accepting applications for the federally funded Connecticut Energy Assistance Program.
Appointments for all heating sources can be made by calling 203-937-3572.
Applications will be processed via phone by appointment Monday through Thursday. No walk-ins will be accepted.
Applicants who have difficulty speaking English are asked to have a translator.
Eligibility for assistance is based on the income of an applicant’s household. To qualify, applicants must provide pay stubs, including monthly Social Security benefits and pension incomes, for all household members 18 and older.
Applicants must also provide Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program benefits, Social Security numbers and birth dates for all household members, as well as current utility bills for the household.
On-Street Parking Passes for ‘Residents Only’ Streets
The West Haven Police Department has posted signs on streets that are typically used by those who do not reside in West Haven but want to visit our beaches and are avoiding parking lot fees. This could cause issues within a neighborhood. For example, this leaves limited parking for those who live on the streets, or trash is left behind, etc.
Homes located on streets with “Residents Only” parking restrictions in front of their house are eligible for two types of color-coded parking passes.
Passes must be displayed on the passenger-side dashboard and be visible through the front windshield so parking patrol officers can easily confirm the vehicle is authorized to park, even if it is not registered in West Haven.
1. Guest Parking Passes
Each homeowner in the district receives two (2) numbered Guest Parking Passes.
Passes allow family and friends without West Haven plates to park on the street during their visit.
Passes can be emailed and printed from your home, or you can visit the Department of Human Resources on the second floor of City Hall, 355 Main St.
2. Event Parking Permits
Use this permit when you are hosting a party or gathering that requires more than two guest vehicles.

Beach Parking
For Residents & Nonresidents on a First-Come, First-Served Basis
Nonresidents must pay to park in all shoreline lots in West Haven by using a “Pay for Parking” smartphone app.
Nonresidents can pay to park by scanning the QR code on the Premium Parking sign in each lot or texting the lot code to 504504. Both options will connect them to the Premium Parking website to complete the transaction. Nonresidents can also download the Premium Parking app on the Apple or Google app stores.
The daily parking fee for nonresidents is $6 per hour, or $40 per day, payable by credit card only. A nonresident seasonal parking pass for $250 is also available.
PLEASE NOTE:
The Sea Bluff Beach parking lot costs $40 for all-day parking on Friday, Saturday and Sunday. There is no hourly option to park on those days.
The Dawson Avenue Beach parking lot is for residents and annual pass holders only.
Parking is
FREE
for residents, provided their vehicles, including motorcycles and new, leased or military-exempt vehicles, are registered in West Haven and they are not delinquent on their motor vehicle taxes. A “new” vehicle means it was registered in West Haven after Oct. 1, 2026.
Taxpayers can check if they owe back taxes and make a payment at
My Tax Bill
.
The city is transitioning from using beach stickers to license plate numbers for proving that vehicles are registered in West Haven. Residents’ license plate numbers are registered in a tax office database. Each parking lot is equipped with a license plate scanner.
Beach parking lots are for use by both residents and nonresidents on a first-come, first-served basis. The lots are: Oyster River, South Street Upper, South Street Lower, Dawson Avenue, Sea Bluff, Bradley Point, Rock Street, Oak Street, Palace Street, Altschuler Plaza, Morse Park and Sandy Point, as well as the April Street boat launch.
All beach parking lots close a half-hour after sunset.
Parking violators will receive an invoice of at least $100 — a $100 citation, plus the $40 daily fee — from New Orleans-based Premium Parking, West Haven’s contractor for digitally driven paid parking. Anyone who has a question about a parking invoice or dispute must call Premium Parking directly at 844-236-2011.

Nonresident Parking (Valid January 1-December 31, 2026)
Nonresident annual parking for West Haven’s beaches can be obtained through the mail by sending the following information: a copy of your driver’s license, a copy of your registration, a $250 check payable to the City of West Haven and your phone number.
Please send all requested materials to:
City of West Haven
Department of Human Resources
Attention: Marissa Franco
355 Main St.
West Haven, CT 06516
A receipt will be mailed to you. Cash is only accepted for walk-ins.
Parking can also be purchased in person through the Department of Human Resources on the second floor of City Hall, 355 Main St., with exact cash or check for $250 and your registration and license.
Annual parking can be purchased on the Premium Parking smartphone app.
Nonresident stickers are no longer issued. All parking is governed by your license plate only.
