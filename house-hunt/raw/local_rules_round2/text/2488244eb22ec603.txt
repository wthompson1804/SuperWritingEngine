               DOG PARK RULES AND REQUIRED ETIQUETTE
       Dog owners and visitors must be residents of Glen Cove and over the age of 18.
       Dogs must be registered with the City of Glen Cove Clerk’s Office Dog License
       Program and have current vaccinations.
       Dog owners and their dogs enter the off-leash area at their own risk.
       Dog owners and visitors are responsible and liable for the actions and behavior
S      of their dogs at all times.
A      Dogs must wear collars with identification tags at all times.
       Dog owners must remove leash as soon as they arrive within the boundaries of
F      the fenced dog park designated area. Keep the leash with you at all times. All
E      dogs shall be leashed in all other areas of the park at all times.
       Dog owners must stay within view and voice control of your dog at all times.
T      Never leave your dog unattended.
Y      Dogs with a history of aggressive behavior are not permitted at the dog park.
       Dog owners must clean-up after their dogs and properly dispose of waste in
       designated on-site containers.
F      Female dogs in heat, puppies under 4 months of age and animals other than
       dogs are not permitted within the park. It is strongly recommended that male
I      dogs be neutered.
R      Children under the age of 12 must be supervised at all times and accompanied
       by an adult over the age of 18.
S
    Prohibited Items and Actions
T
       Prongs, spike and choke collars are not permitted in the park.
       Dog food, raw hide chews, treats and dog toys are not permitted in the park
       Alcohol, smoking, and glass containers are strictly prohibited.
       Prevent dogs from digging holes and fill in any holes created by your dog.
       Small dogs (under 25 lbs.) are only allowed in the small dog area.
       Large dogs (over 25 lbs.) are only allowed in the large dog area.
       No more than 2 dogs per owner may be permitted.
