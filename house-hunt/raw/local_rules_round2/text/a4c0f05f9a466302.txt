Winter Wonderland
SAVE THE DATE - Saturday, December 5, 2026 from 1:00pm to 6:00pm!!
Winter Wonderland, East Brunswick’s Winter Season Festival, is a great day out for the whole family. Events, including the Holiday Market and Lighting Ceremony, will take place from 1:00 - 6:00pm at the East Brunswick Municipal Complex (Civic Center Drive).
Activities will include Live Music, Dancers, Carolers, Cookies with Claus (
sold out
), Seasonal Photo Area, Kids Activities & Crafts, Food Trucks, Train Rides, Story Time Tent, Snow Maze, Facepainting, Airbrush Tattoos, Toys for Tots, Letters to Santa Drop Off, and S'More!!!
Winter Wonderland is free to attend! Items are available for purchase at the Holiday Market and Food Trucks.
Please look over the parking and informational map below so you can plan accordingly for Saturday.  Note that a large crowd is expected throughout the day, so for your safety, please follow directions of Public Safety Officers and Park Rangers stationed in the parking lots and assisting with traffic on Ryders Lane. A handicap drop off area is located in the Library parking lot.
HOLIDAY MARKET & FOOD VENDORS
What better way to find those special gifts and seasonal delights than at a Holiday Market! This outdoor sale of fine art, handmade craft, jewelry, décor, baked goods, and specialty foods is sure to please. Running from 1:00pm - 6:00pm, the Holiday Market is part of East Brunswick Township's Winter Wonderland event!
ENTERTAINMENT/EVENTS
LIGHTING CEREMONY
The Township's Annual Tree & Menorah Lighting Ceremony will be held at 6pm by the Gazebo at the Municipal Pond.
