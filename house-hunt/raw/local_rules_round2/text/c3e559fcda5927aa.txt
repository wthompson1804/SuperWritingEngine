Recreation Advisory Board
The Recreation Advisory Board acts as a vital bridge between the community and local government, providing citizen input on programs, parks, and facilities. The Board meets with Recreation staff once per month and will often volunteer at events or assist with programming.
﻿
Established under
ORDINANCE NO. 2026-04
, the Board shall consist of seven voting-members consisting of one Chair and six regular members. The Recreation Superintendent shall be a nonvoting, advisory member of the Recreation Advisory Board.
