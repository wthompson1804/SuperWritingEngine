Free parking information
Parking is free in all municipal lots during a declared snowstorm.
Free parking holidays
Meters and pay stations will indicate when parking is free. Parking is not charged on any municipal street or in any municipal lot on the following holidays:
New Year's Day
Martin Luther King Jr. Day
Memorial Day
Independence Day
Labor Day
Thanksgiving Day
Christmas Day
From Thanksgiving Day through New Year’s Day, parking is free on Saturdays in all municipal lots, except Lot 8.
