Dog Licensing
By law ALL dogs must be licensed - Please License Your Dog for 2026 either in person or by mail
Late Fees of $25 Will Be in Effect on February 15th
The Department conducts a free annual rabies vaccination program for residents' dogs and cats in January of each year.
State law requires all dogs to be licensed. All dog licenses expire on December 31st and must be renewed in January of each year. Proof of valid rabies vaccination must be shown and valid through November of the current year. For questions, please call the Health Department.
Requirements for obtaining a dog license by mail:
Please send the following to the mailing address
Completed
Dog License Application (PDF)
Check made out to the Borough of East Rutherford in the amount of:
$10 for each spayed/neutered dog
$13 for each non-spayed / non-neutered dog
Enclose proof of rabies vaccination (Please note - rabies vaccination must be good through at least November of this year)
Self-addressed stamped envelope
