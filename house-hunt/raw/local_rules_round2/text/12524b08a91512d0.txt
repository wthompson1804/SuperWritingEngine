Pay a parking ticket
Pay traffic and parking tickets online at NJMCdirect.
Online payment hours
Monday to Thursday: 4:30 am to 11:15 pm
Friday: 4:30 am to 10:15 pm
Saturday: 4:30 am to 3:15 pm
Sunday: 1 pm to 11:15 pm
Disputes
If you plan to plead not guilty to a traffic summons, you must
notify the Municipal Court
of your intention prior to the date at the bottom of the ticket. If you have any questions, contact the Court Administrator at (908) 725-6375 or email
Jenni.Peterson@NJCourts.gov
.
Lost ticket
Use
Municipal Court Case Search
to lookup municipal traffic or complaint information online.
