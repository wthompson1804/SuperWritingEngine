Severe Weather Parking Ban Activation
To protect public safety and help Department of Public Works crews expedite snow removal, West Haven residents and businesses are reminded to observe all parking regulations before, during and after severe storms.
Per city ordinance
Chapter 188
, parking is prohibited on the even-numbered side of a road during a declared parking ban, unless the road is posted with a “No Parking” sign on the odd side.
A parking ban declaration will include a beginning and ending time, unless severe weather dictates that the ban remain in effect until further notice.

Chapter 188
of the West Haven Code, police or parking lot enforcement officers can ticket and tow vehicles violating the ordinance at the owner’s expense. Violators are subject to a $100 fine.

Sidewalk Snow Removal
Residents and businesses are reminded to shovel their sidewalks within 24 hours after a storm or face a $25 fine “for each day that the violation persists.”
According to the “Removal of Snow and Ice” ordinance under
Chapter 195
of the West Haven Code, people are given 24 hours to remove snow from sidewalks on and bordering their properties. Violators are subject to the $25-per-day fine, per the ordinance.
To report a complaint, call the Police Department’s nonemergency line at 203-937-3900. Complaints are kept confidential.
Plowing or blowing snow into city streets is prohibited, and violators are subject to a $60 fine for each offense, according to the ordinance.
Residents are urged to help firefighters keep hydrants clear of snow.
Damaged Mailboxes
Residents and businesses with mailboxes damaged by snow thrown from a plow are the responsibility of the property owner. The city will only repair mailboxes damaged by the striking of a plow blade if there is visible evidence, such as paint or tire tracks.
Snow removal around mailboxes is the property owner’s responsibility.
Contact Information
For more information, call the Highway Department at 203-937-3644.
