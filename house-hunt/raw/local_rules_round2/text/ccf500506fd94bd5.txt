THIRD STREET GARAGE
THIRD STREET GARAGE
Garage Commuter (Right Side)          5am - 9:30am       Mon-Fri           Permit Reserved
Public Paid Parking (Right Side)         After 9:30am        Mon-Fri           $1.00 Hr or $7.00 per Day
Public Paid Parking (Right Side)         5am - 6pm           Saturday          $1.00 Hr or $7.00 per Day
Public Paid Parking (Left Side)           5am - 6pm           Mon-Sat           $1.00 Hr or $7.00 per Day
Public Parking is Free                         After 6pm             Mon-Sat          All Day Sunday
No Overnight Parking                         2am – 5am           Sun-Sat           Need Permission from SOPA
Use Park Mobile Pay by Phone App:
Zone #81034
Or use the Pay Station in the Breeze-way as you exit on foot. Pay by Plate – Remember Your Plate Number. Payment is Credit, Cash or Coin. No Refunds or Change Given.
Garage Commuter Permits are reserved for SO Residents only.
These permits are $700 per year.
Maintenance and Security is provided by Pro Park
Cameras are monitored by Pro Park Command Center.
Patrols are conducted by the SO Police Dept and SOPA.
Directions:
The Third St Garage is part of the Third & Valley apartment complex, located off of Third Street at the end of Sloan Street.
NJ TRANSIT
NJ TRANSIT at SOPAC
Transit Commuter (White Spaces)     5am - 9:30am              Mon-Fri           Permit Reserved
Public Parking (Yellow Spaces)          5am - 9:30am             Mon-Fri           $5.00 per Day
Any White or Yellow Space                 After 9:30am – 6pm    Mon-Fri           $1.00 per Hr.
No Overnight Parking                         2am – 5am                  Sun-Sat            Not Allowed
Use Park Mobile Pay by Phone App:
Zone #23000
Use the Pay Station in the lot or at the under-pass for Daily Parking.
Pay by Plate – Remember Your Plate Number.
Credit, Cash or Coin. No Refunds or Change Given.
These permits are $840 per year.
Permits are for the general public, there is a Waiting List.
Patrols are conducted by the SO Police Dept. and SOPA.
Directions:
Church St. off of West South Orange Ave is the entrance into the rear parking lot. SOPAC Way off of West South Orange Ave is the entrance into the front of the lot. The lot is behind the train station on the west side of the tracks.
MOUNTAIN STATION (Vose Ave & West Montrose)
Paid Public Parking (Vose Ave)         5am -1pm                  Mon-Fri          $4.00 per Day
Public Parking is Free                        1pm - 2am                 Mon-Fri           All Day Sat & Sun
Transit Commuter (Meeker St.)          5am- 1pm                 Mon- Fri          Permit Reserved
Use Park Mobile Pay by Phone App:
Zone #23001
Use the Pay Station on Vose Ave side.
Pay by Plate – Remember Your Plate Number.
Payment is Credit, Cash or Coin. No Refunds or Change Given.
These permits are $480 per year.
Permits are for the general public.
Patrols are conducted by the SO Police Dept. and SOPA.
Directions:
Daily parking is on Vose Ave and West Montrose St. (east side of the tracks)
Permit parking is on Meeker St and West Montrose St. (west side of the tracks)
