Permits and Licensing Center
The Town of Hamden facilitates permits and licensing primarily through the viewpoint online permitting portal
Click here for the Town of Hamden Viewpoint Online Permitting Portal
.
Certain applications are not yet integrated with the system and require other means of submission. Please select your category below to learn more about the permits and licenses issued by Hamden, and to submit your application.
Building Dept.
The Hamden Building Department facilitates permits and licenses through the
Click here for the Building Department Online Permitting Portal
. Please refer to the information below, including "
Required Inspections
", prior to submitting an application. If you have questions regarding your permit application, contact the Building Department at 203-287-7160.
Click your desired permit application to begin.
Building Permits
Including but not limited to permits for additions, alterations, roofs, decks, siding, replacement doors/windows, pools, solar, signs and tents.
Electrical Permits
Including low voltage, fire alarm system work (solar use Building Permit only)
Plumbing Permits
Including Fire Sprinklers
Heating & Mechanical Permits
Including HVAC, Piping, Hoods/Ducts, Fire Suppression Systems
Demolition Permit
Required to completely demolish a structure
Chimney Liner
Required permit for chimney lining
A building permit costs $24.26 for the first $1,000 of construction cost and $24.26 for each $1,000 of construction costs thereafter.
Certificate of occupancy\completion: $36.00
Electrical Permits (Solar) : $4.50 pkw
For Commercial and Residential plan reviews, please call 203-287-7160
Commercial construction projects (all buildings except detached 1 or 2 family homes) require Fire Marshal Plan review, approval and inspections. Fire Marshal Plan Review fee (including site inspections) is $55 for the first $10,000 of construction cost and $5 for each $1000 of construction costs thereafter. Fire Marshal Plan Review and associated fees are integrated in the relevant Building Permit application.

Fire Marshal
The Hamden Fire Marshal facilitates permits and licenses through the
Click here for the Fire Marshal Online Permitting Portal
. Please refer to the information below prior to submitting an application. If you have questions regarding your permit application, contact the Fire Marshal at 203-407-5880.
Information:
To apply for Fire Alarm Permits, Sprinkler Permits, Hoods/Ducts with Suppression Systems visit the
Building Department
and apply for the relevant permit there. Fire Marshal Review is integrated with those permit applications:
Fire Alarm System:
Apply for an Electrical Permit and check Fire Alarm under Work to be Performed section of application
Sprinkler System:
Apply for a Plumbing Permit and check Fire Sprinkler under Work to be Performed section of application
Hoods/Ducts & Suppression Systems:
Apply for a Mechanical Permit and check appropriate box under Work to be Performed section of application
Tents:
Apply for a Building Permit and check Tent - temporary under Type of Work section of application
Fire Marshal Plan Review fee is $55 for the first $10,000 of construction cost and $5 for each $1000 of construction costs thereafter. Fire Marshal review fee and inspections for tents is $30 per tent.
FM UST-AST
Underground / Aboveground Storage Tank Installation or Removal Permit - Use this application if you intend to alter, clean, repair, line with a protective coating, remove, abandon, place temporarily out of service, or otherwise dispose of a flammable or combustible liquid tank. Permit Fee: $50 per tank on residential property / $100 per tank on commercial property.
RECORDS
Use this application to request records from the Fire Department and/or Fire Marshal.
FM Operations and Materials Permit
To maintain, store, use, or handle materials; to conduct processes that could produce conditions hazardous to life or property; or to install equipment used in connection with such activities.
FM Operating Permit
Use this application to request inspections of buildings for licensing requirements. Permit Fees vary from $40-$80 depending on occupancy type.

Excavation Permits
covers all operations that will require excavation within the Town’s right of way, backfilling and restoration.
Excavation Permit fee: $195.00 up to 200 Sq. Ft. or $250 per 500 Sq. Ft. or portion thereof for long trenches
Permanent Patch Fee: $1000 + $11.00 per Sq. Ft for Local
Roads and $15.00 per Sq. Ft. for Collector or Arterial Roads.
To determine sq. ft. the following formula is to be used:
(Length of cut) x (Width of cut) = Sq. Ft.
Larger excavations may require additional fees at the discretion of the Town Engineer.
