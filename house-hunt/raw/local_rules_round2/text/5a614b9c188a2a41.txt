Visit the Hamden Farmers Market 2021
Visitor Parking
Visitor Parking
The Farmers Market will take place in the large, open field of Town Center Park this year, in front of the Hamden Rotary Club concert Gazebo. The main entrance to the market on the field will be through the main entrance to the Hamden Middle School at 2623 Dixwell Ave.
Main Entrance: Hamden Middle School - 2623 Dixwell Ave.
(drive down to parking field using service road behind Hamden Middle)
Additional Entrance: Hamden Miller Library - 2901 Dixwell Ave.
(park in the Red Barn/Josh's Jungle parking lot)
