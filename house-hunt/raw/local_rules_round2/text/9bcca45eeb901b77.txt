Alternate Side Parking
The East Rutherford Police Department Traffic Division would like to remind all of our residents and visitors of the borough's Alternate Side Parking Restrictions.
A common misconception is that these restrictions are "Street Cleaning Restrictions" and are not enforced in inclement weather or if street cleaning equipment is not operating. This is not correct. Alternate Side Parking is enforced all year round, regardless of weather conditions, to allow routine street maintenance, including the planting, removal, trimming of trees, curb repair/painting, snow removal, and other maintenance programs to be completed. It also assists the police department in identifying possibly abandoned or stolen vehicles.
Thank you for your continued cooperation.
Lt. Stan Rymarz
Traffic Division Supervisor
