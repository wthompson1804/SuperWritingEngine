Parking
The Red Bank Parking Utility is located at 42 West Front Street.
Office hours:
Monday through Friday  9:00am to 5:00pm,
Saturday hours are available by appointment only.
The Borough of Red Bank offers convenient on-street parking and surface lots throughout the town.
Time limits and hours of enforcement for parking are posted on signs in every metered area, as well as on each parking meter.  The purpose of time limits is to ensure that parking spaces regularly become available for visitors and patrons of the downtown. Paid parking is enforced through the use of street meters and parking lot kiosks Monday through Saturday from 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. Parking throughout town is free after 9 p.m. on weekdays and all day Sunday.
Some permit parking spaces are restricted to permit holders,
Monday through Friday from 9 a.m. to 2 p.m. in the following lots:
Gold Street
Union Street
White Street
Eastside
After 2 p.m. these permit spaces become metered parking open to the public.
All meters and kiosks accept coins, credit cards and mobile app payments. It is illegal to put anything other than these coins in a meter, or to tamper with a meter in anyway.
Pay at Kiosk or Skip the meter and pay with your cell phone!

Malfunctioning Meters
Please stay with meter and immediately report malfunctioning meters to Borough Parking Utility at 732-345-8135. When calling, please indicate the meter ID number (e.g., BS19E) located under the glass dome behind the time display.

Holiday Parking:
Holiday Parking is in effect during the holiday season! For 2025, the Borough of Red Bank is pleased to offer
FREE all-day parking from December 11 through December 26
. We encourage residents and visitors to take advantage of this free parking while you dine, shop, and enjoy everything Red Bank has to offer during the holidays.
Inclement Snow Weather & Parking:
Under
§ 680-3J
of the Red Bank Municipal Code (Parking and Traffic), whenever snow has fallen and the accumulation is such that it covers the street, an emergency condition is deemed to exist and no vehicle may be parked on Borough streets between 11:00 p.m. and 7:00 a.m..
This parking prohibition remains in effect until after the snow has ceased and streets are sufficiently plowed and cleared such that parking will not interfere with traffic or emergency vehicles.
In case of Snow, all residents must move their cars are directed by the Borough and may use Municipal Lots until the parking restrictions are lifted. Parking fees in municipal lots will be waived while restrictions are in effect.
Where can you park during snow storms?
Municipal lots will be available for overnight parking. Follow our alert system for direct information. Municipal lots is avialable below.

Residential Parking Permits?
Municipal Residential Parking Permits
Residents may apply to the office of the Borough Parking Utility located at 42 West Front Street for a residential parking permit. Driver License and a valid vehicle registration card are required.
Please disregard any Residential permits that have an expiration date as there is no need to renew them once you have received one. The best location to place the Residential Permit is on the front windshield a few inches above the Inspection Sticker. All permits can also be displayed visibly in the rear driver's side window or driver's/driver's passenger side window,
which are not tinted.
Municipal Public Parking Lot Permits
Permits may be obtained at the office of the Borough Parking Utility located at 42 West Front Street.
All permits must be displayed visibly
from the rear view mirror when parked.
