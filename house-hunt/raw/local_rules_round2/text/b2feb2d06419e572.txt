About the parks
Winter basketball league
This entry-level league is open to students in grades 3 to 8 and offers intertown games in partnership with recreation departments from surrounding towns. Local travel is required.
About the program
Teams are organized by gender and grade level
Grades 3 and 4
Grades 5 and 6
Grades 7 and 8
Registration is first come, first served
Players will be waitlisted if a team is full or lacks a volunteer coach
Practices
Begin in December at Somerville Middle School or Van Derveer Elementary School
Held 1 night per week for 1 hour
Scheduled based on coach availability. Coaches will contact players with details once schedules are set.
Games
Held on Friday evenings or during the day on Saturdays
Game schedules will be shared in late December
2027 season
Dates:
January 8 to February 27, 2027
Times:
Vary by team. Coaches will share practice and game times.
Registration
Deadline:
November 1, 2026. Late registrations will not be accepted.
Fee:
$80 (includes team jersey, distributed by coaches)
Eligibility:
Participants must live in or attend school in Somerville
Note:
Space is limited
