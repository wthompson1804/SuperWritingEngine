About Us
The East Orange Parking Authority was created by Ordinance #14 of the City of East Orange in 1970 pursuant to NJSA 40:HA. The East Orange Parking Authority is a 
public body corporate and political subdivision of the State with the same territorial boundaries as the City of East Orange.
Our office is located at 60 Evergreen Place, Suite 200, East Orange New Jersey. Our hours are 9:00a.m. to 5:00p.m. Monday thru Friday.
MISSION
The East Orange Parking Authority is dedicated to promoting the free flow of traffic by providing, managing, and maintaining public street parking. It also constructs clean, safe, and affordable street parking lots and facilities to relieve traffic congestion. The East Orange Parking Authority mission is to construct, improve, maintain, and operate off-street parking facilities, to promote parking and traffic improvements and thereby facilitate the free flow of traffic through city streets.  The East Orange Parking Authority contributes to the redevelopment of the City of East Orange by:
Managing and operating an on-street parking program through metered parking.
Managing and issuing overnight residential permits for approved streets.
Providing and maintaining parking facilities that are clean, safe, and affordable.
Enforcing all State, County and Local Municipals laws related to parking regulations.
Offering a variety of payment options for the convenience of residents and the general public.

Board Members
The East Orange Parking Authority Board consists of one (1) resident from each of the City’s five (5) wards which are appointed by the Mayor and the East Orange City Council
The monthly meetings are held at 6:00 p.m. at 60 Evergreen Place, Suite 200, East Orange, New Jersey, 07018. The Parking Authority through its appointed Commissioners, Executive Director, and its employees, are devoted to protecting the quality of life for residents and business owners in our community.
