Municipal Code
The municipal code is a collection of the local laws, or ordinances, that are enforced in the Borough of Somerville. An ordinance is first introduced and then passed by the Mayor and Borough Council, after a public hearing. New ordinances are listed in each Borough Council meeting agenda.
The Clerk's office
updates the municipal code. The code is organized by chapters and sections. You may browse common topics in the index or enter a search term at the top of the page.
