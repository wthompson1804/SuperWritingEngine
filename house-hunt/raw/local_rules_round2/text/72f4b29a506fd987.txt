Welcome to Bound Brook!
Bound Brook is a historic and vibrant community where residents can live, work, play, and connect.
Located along the Raritan River in Somerset County, Bound Brook is a close-knit borough of approximately 1.6 square miles and 10,600 residents. First settled in the late 1600s, the Borough is bordered by Middle Brook to the west and Green Brook—also historically known as the Bound Brook—to the east.
A Small Town with Big Connections
Bound Brook combines the character and convenience of a small town with easy access to the cultural, employment, and transportation opportunities of the greater New York metropolitan area.
﻿
The Borough features a walkable downtown and convenient transportation options, including the NJ TRANSIT Raritan Valley Line, Interstate 287, Route 22, and Route 28/Union Avenue. These connections make Bound Brook an attractive community for residents, commuters, visitors, and businesses alike.
A Community with Something for Everyone
Throughout the year, Bound Brook brings residents and visitors together through community celebrations, recreational programs, cultural activities, and special events. From seasonal festivals and outdoor activities to the Memorial Day Parade, Battle of Bound Brook reenactment, Movies in the Park, Halloween festivities, and holiday celebrations, there are many opportunities to experience the community and connect with neighbors.
Residents can also enjoy local youth and adult recreation programs, parks, playgrounds, and community swimming pools during the summer.
Bound Brook's railroad heritage also makes the Borough a popular destination for rail enthusiasts who visit the area to watch and photograph trains traveling through the historic downtown station.
History, Culture & Recreation
Bound Brook's rich history can be seen throughout the community. The historic Bound Brook Memorial Library serves as an important community resource, offering traditional library services along with educational, cultural, and community programming.
Adjacent to the library is the historic Presbyterian Cemetery, where gravestones date back to the 1700s and veterans of the Revolutionary War, Civil War, and other conflicts are laid to rest.
The Raritan River is another defining feature of the Borough, providing scenic views and opportunities for outdoor recreation, including fishing and kayaking. The historic Queens Bridge offers a picturesque connection across the river and serves as one of the community's recognizable landmarks.

Resident Permit Parking
Purchase a Resident Parking Permit
Each resident parking permit is valid for one year, expiring on December 31. All permits must be renewed by January 31 of the following year. The annual fee is $10.00 per registered vehicle for residents of streets or portions thereof where residential parking restrictions apply.
Required Documentation for Each Vehicle:
When registering your vehicle through the portal, the following documents are required:
Valid driver’s license
Vehicle registration
Proof of insurance
Permits will only be issued for vehicles owned or principally operated by the resident. All documents provided must include the current address.

The Borough of Bound Brook has designated 5 spaces in Municipal Parking Lot #6 located on East Main Street exclusively for use as electric vehicle charging stations.
Rules and Regulations
Electric vehicles are authorized to park in spaces designated as electric vehicle charging station spaces only during the time when the vehicles are connected for electric charging purposes or up to four hours, whichever is less.
Owners/operators shall be required to remove the vehicle from the charging station space within 15 minutes past the time the vehicle is no longer charging. Vehicles sitting in a charging station space past the fifteen-minute grace period shall be subject to a sitting fee, a violation, and further subject to towing at the owner’s expense in accordance with § 7-3 of this chapter.
It shall be unlawful for any person to park or leave standing a vehicle in a stall or space designated for the recharging of electric vehicles unless the vehicle is connected for electric charging purposes. Any vehicle violating this requirement is subject to parking fees, a violation and towing at the owner’s expense in accordance with § 7-3 of this chapter.
It shall be unlawful for any person to obstruct, block or otherwise bar access to a designated electric vehicle charging station.
