Parking Operations
Parking Operations is a division within the Department of Infrastructure & Operations (I&O). Other departments within E&IO are Public Works, Sewer, and Parking Operations.
Duties
Parking Operations is responsible for the following municipal operations:
Spring Street Garage maintenance and operations
Spring Street Garage parking permit system management
Parking meter program maintenance and operations
Parking meter bag rental program
Electric Vehicle charging stations maintenance and operations
