Park & Recreation Meeting Schedule (2026)
Please be advised that the Town of Beacon Falls Parks and Recreation Commission has scheduled its 2026 Regular Monthly Meetings to be held on the third Monday of each month at 7:00 pm in the Assembly Room at Town Hall, 10 Maple Avenue, Beacon Falls, CT 06403.
Posted below are the Meeting dates for the year. Meetings with asterisks (**) are Special Meetings, as they are scheduled on a different night, due to holidays. If any meetings are cancelled, proper notice will be posted with the Town Clerk. Special Meetings and Public Hearings will be scheduled as necessary
