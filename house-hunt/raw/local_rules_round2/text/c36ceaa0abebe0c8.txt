Hydrant Flushing is Twice a Year:
In the spring and fall, fire hydrant valves are opened to allow water to flow through the pipes. Systematically opening the hydrants enhances water quality by flushing sediment from the main line pipes, verifying the proper operation of hydrants and valves, and ensuring that the hydrant is operational in case of a fire emergency.  If you see discolored water, please allow your tap to run until it is clear.   Discoloration is a normal part of the process.  Avoid washing laundry until water runs clear at the tap
Water & Sewer Line Upgrades
Homeowners may request a water and/or sewer line upgrade by calling the Borough of Red Bank at (732)530-2770. You will receive a quote in the mail in about 10 days .
All quotes are good for 90-days.
