Discover the Township’s Parks, Recreation & Social Services
The Township’s Department of Parks, Recreation, and Social Services proudly maintains an array of parks, open spaces, and recreational facilities—encompassing over 300 acres of developed land. It also hosts a broad selection of programs tailored to serve all ages and abilities; there’s truly something for everyone to enjoy.
Geick Park
, one of our main parks, features a state-of-the-art synthetic surface baseball field, soccer field, and dedicated areas for softball and lacrosse. It also hosts
Camp R.O.B.I.N.
, a cherished program offering enriching experiences for individuals with special needs. Beyond our scenic parks, the Department offers an average of 50 seasonal programs. These include children’s dance and movement classes, creative arts and crafts, STEM enrichment, ceramics workshops and fitness and exercise programs for adults.
