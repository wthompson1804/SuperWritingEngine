Woodbridge Regional Animal Control
All animal control services: 203-387-2511
Woodbridge Regional Animal Control provides enforcement of all Connecticut Statutes, local and State laws and ordinances pertaining to dogs, cats and other animals. Their responsibilities include, but are not limited to responding to calls for:
Animal cruelty complaints
Animal nuisance
Cat bites
Dog bites
Stray, roaming, abandoned, sick or injured animals
Woodbrige Animal Control Officers conduct investigations and inspections, issue citations, written warnings and apply for arrest warrants when needed. Animal Control ensures public safety in the Town that they protect and serve: Woodbridge, Bethany and Seymour.
