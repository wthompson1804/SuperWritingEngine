The City Clerk is responsible for the issuance of marriage licenses for the State of New York and the recording of birth, marriage and death certificates in Glen Cove as required by the State of New York. In addition, the City Clerk issues yard/garage sale permits, racing and wagering permits for gambling, bingo and raffles, block party permits, and dog licenses for the City of Glen Cove. The City Clerk maintains the minutes of all City Council meetings. Copies of the City of Glen Cove zoning books and maps are available for purchase from the Office of the City Clerk. The City Clerk also handles all Freedom of Information Law (F.O.I.L.) requests and claims against the City of Glen Cove.
