Overnight Parking
According to township ordinance 152-11.1 Vehicles are prohibited from
parking on the streets of South Orange between the hours of 2 am and 6
am.  Unless, the Parking Authority has issued a decal allowing
overnight parking.
Temporary permission for overnight, on street parking is
available  through the Parking Authority for residence with extenuating
circumstances.
