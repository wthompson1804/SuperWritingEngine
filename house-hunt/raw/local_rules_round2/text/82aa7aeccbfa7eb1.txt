Building Department
Mission Statement
Through the Administration of the New Jersey Uniform Construction Code, to ensure the health, safety and welfare of the public.
The Building Department is here to assist the public, contractors and design professionals by providing quality service that is knowledgeable, efficient and comprehensive. The department effectively promotes and provides professional plan review, permitting and inspections of proposed and ongoing construction projects in the community.  This department is entrusted with the responsibility to promote code awareness and expertise, assuring building safety and code compliance for the residents.
Current Construction Code Requirements
All New Construction Applications must be filed under the most current Construction Codes adopted by the New Jersey Department of Community Affairs (NJ DCA).  For more information, view
NJ DCA Code and Regulations
.
Construction Permit Inspection
Due to the recent change in the code 5:23-2.18(c) all inspection requests must be submitted in writing at least 24 hours prior to requested inspection date and shall be performed within three business days, or other such time within 30 days, as agreed upon by the enforcing agency and the owner.
