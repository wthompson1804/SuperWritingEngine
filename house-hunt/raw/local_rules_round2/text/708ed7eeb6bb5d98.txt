How do I obtain a police report
Police and Accident reports may be picked up in person at the Roxbury Township Police Department Headquarters (Please park in designated visitor spots outside the police station). Reports can be picked up from 9am-4pm Monday through Friday. Please allow 7-10 working days for reports to be processed.
Individuals requesting reports must show a government issued ID to confirm their identity.
Please contact the records department prior to your arrival so she can ensure your report is completed and available when you arrive for pick up.
Records telephone number: (973) 448-2039
Records fax number: (973) 448-9069
