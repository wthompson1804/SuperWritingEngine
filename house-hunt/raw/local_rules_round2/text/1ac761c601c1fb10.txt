                                                    2026 Jersey City Dog License Application
       Licenses may be purchased for a period of 12 Months (1 Year expires 04/30/27) or 36 Months (3 Years expires 04/30/29).

                          Please complete the form below and return it along with your check/money order payable to:
                                     City of Jersey City, Department of Health and Human Services.
                                                           Dog Licensing Bureau
                                                 Dr. Martin Luther King, City Hall Annex.
                                                  1 Jackson Square Jersey City, NJ 07305

                          You may also apply/renew online https://eforms-main.govpilot.com/NJ/jerseycity

                                      All fees are effective 05/01/2026 and are non-refundable
                                              1 (One) Year License (Expires 04/30/2027)
                                                    Neutered dogs: $10.00 per dog
                                                    Unneutered dogs: $20.00 per dog
          Rabies shots must be up to date and cannot expire before 03/01/2027 (You must show proof of current vaccination)

                                            3 (Three) Year License (Expires 04/30/2029)
                                                  Neutered dogs: $25.00 per dog
                                                  Unneutered dogs: $50.00 per dog
          Rabies shots must be up to date and cannot expire before 03/01/2029 (You must show proof of current vaccination)

                                                                  Proof of Rabies Vaccination or Exemption
All applications require a copy of the rabies vaccination certificate showing it to be valid through the license period and attached to this application when filed. If your
Veterinarian is exempting your dog from this vaccination, New Jersey State Law 8:23A 4.3 requires that a duly licensed veterinarian certifies in writing that the dog is
incapable of being inoculated because of an infirmity, other physical condition, or regimen of therapy. We require the original exemption letter or the New Jersey
Department of Health (VPH-28) “CERTIFICATE OF EXEMPTION FROM RABIES VACCINATION FORM” showing it to be valid through the license period and attached to
this application. https://www.nj.gov/health/forms/vph-28.pdf
