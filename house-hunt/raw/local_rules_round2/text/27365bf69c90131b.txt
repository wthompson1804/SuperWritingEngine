Park & Ride Jitney Service
Springfield offers a Park & Ride Jitney Service from the Springfield Community Pool Parking Lot (44 Morrison Road) to the NJ Transit Short Hills Train Station.
Jitney Rider Form
The daily fee is $3.00 per ride (one way ticket) and monthly/quarterly is $50 per month. Monthly fees
must
be paid before the beginning of the new month or the 1st week.
Payments MUST be cash or check made to the order of “Township of Springfield”
and mailed (100 Mountain Avenue) or put in our drop box in front of town hall.
