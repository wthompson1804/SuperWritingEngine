Dog & Cat Licensing
Requirements
All dogs and cats must be licensed according to the following regulations:
By January 31st each year
When they are over the age of 7 months
Within 10 days of moving to Roxbury, dog licenses from other New Jersey municipalities are transferable with proof of the animal’s current licensure.
Cat License Application
Dog License Application
In order to license you will need proof of a current rabies shot. Current means the shot does not expire before November 1 of the licensing year. If your animal’s shot does expire before November 1, please have the animal revaccinated before attempting to license. There is a five animal limit per household. Licenses must be physically received by this department within a month to be charged that months fee, licenses mailed in one month but not received until the following month are subject to that months fee.
