Parking During Snowstorms
During snow storms when vehicles must be removed from the street for snow removal, the Parking Authority allows residents to park in the nearest municipal parking lot
(NO PARKING ON BOARD OF EDUCATION PROPERTIES AND POLICE DEPARTMENT LOT)
.
All vehicles must be removed once vehicles are allowed back on the streets. Please sign up for alerts at Nixle.com, enter zip code and follow prompts for sign up, this will allow you to be informed when vehicles are permitted back on the streets as well as other Borough alerts and advisory notices.
