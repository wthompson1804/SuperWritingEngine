Mobile Pay Parking
The Fort Lee Parking Authority is proud to announce its brand-new parking app- “PARK FORT LEE”. PARK FORT LEE allows you to pay for vehicle parking in seconds. It can be used for all street parking locations and parking lots throughout the Borough. The app is a completely cashless solution, reminds you of when your parking session is about to expire, and even lets you extend your parking time from anywhere! PARK FORT LEE is convenient, easy and secure. In addition to PARK FORT LEE, you may also use the PayByPhone app. For more information, please visit:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cCMs06nFP3s
PARK FORT LEE can be used on all street parking, as well as Fort Lee lots managed by the Fort Lee Parking Authority, including:
MBL Lot
Guntzer Street Lot
Gerome Street Lot
540 Main Street Lot
Center Avenue Lot
Warren Street Lot
In each area, participating meters are individually labeled with mobile phone parking instructions.
Benefits of Using PARK FORT LEE
It’s the fastest and easiest way to pay for parking. You’ll receive text alerts when your parking session is about to expire. You can extend time remotely within the allowable maximum time limit. You can view your parking transactions online or via the mobile app.
How to Register for PARK FORT LEE
Before using the service, you need to register in the PARK FORT LEE system.
To register: Download the
FREE
PARK FORT LEE  app from your device’s app store to sign up.
A $0.40 convenience fee will apply to each session along with the Fort Lee parking fee. You may always check your account balance in the PARK FORT LEE app.
Please note that standard parking rules apply for PARK FORT LEE parking. Parking beyond the maximum time permitted in any designated zone is strictly prohibited.
How to  PARK by App
Download the free PARK FORT LEE app from your mobile device’s app store.
When you arrive at a parking meter:
Open the PARK FORT LEE app
Enter the location and meter number (located on the meter decal)
Select the amount of time you wish to park
Click on "Park Now" to begin parking
How to Call to Park
Don’t have a smart phone? Simply to call to park. Dial the PayByPhone service number displayed on parking meters and signs. Existing users will be prompted to enter the 4 or 5 digit location number, and parking time required. New users will be guided through a registration process. Once you have heard conﬁrmation that your parking has started you may leave your vehicle. Parking details will be sent to the handheld units the Parking Enforcement Officers patrol with. Extend your parking at any time. Simply call the number you dialed in Step 1. Your account will be recognized and you will be given the option to extend your parking session or to start a new one.
