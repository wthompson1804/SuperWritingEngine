Pets & Associations
Issues pertaining to pets at properties belonging to an association such as but not limited to condos, private beaches (up to the high water mark), and other shared common areas/facilities, should be made to the designated contact person of that specific association.
Associations typically have rules and regulations that members are required to follow. These would trump basic city ordinances directed at loose pets while on the property administered by the association.
Incidents such as animal-on-animal bites or animal-on-people bites should be made directly to Animal Control/Milford Police Department for reporting and rabies verification.
Some associations also have more detailed rules such as specific dog breeds allowed on association property or rules pertaining to feeding feral cats.
It is important to understand your association's specific rules before adopting a pet.
