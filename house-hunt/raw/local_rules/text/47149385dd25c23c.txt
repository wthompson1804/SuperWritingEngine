Annual September 11th Remembrance Ceremony
Posted September 8, 2026
Mayor Albio Sires, the Board of Commissioners & The West New York Police Department invite you to the annual September 11th Remembrance Ceremony.
Friday, September 11 at 8:30AM
Donnelly Park (60th Street & Boulevard East)
As we come upon the 25th anniversary of the tragic events of September 11th, 2001, we extend our love and support to survivors, families of those who lost their lives, and heroes of that fateful day. We remember their bravery and sacrifices.
West New York will never forget.

Buena Vista Social Club Grammy & Tony Awards Winner David Oquendo Live at West New York Cultural Center
Posted August 25, 2026
Mayor Albio Sires and the Board of Commissioners are proud to invite residents to a special evening of live music featuring West New York’s own David Oquendo, in collaboration with WestNYArts!
Join us for
“David Oquendo: From West New York to Broadway and Back!”
as we celebrate the incredible musical journey of this talented local artist, guitarist, and educator.
David will be joined by Chacho Schartz on bass and Mauricio Herrera on percussion, with special musical contributions from David Oquendo’s longtime collaborator and Buena Vista Social Club associate music director & guitarist.
Sunday, August 30
7:00 PM
West New York Cultural Center 5712 Bergenline Avenue
FREE EVENT!
Bring your family and friends and enjoy an unforgettable night of music right here in West New York! We look forward to seeing you there!
