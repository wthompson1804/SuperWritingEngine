WHAT'S HAPPENING NOW
Welcome to the  Town of Hempstead's Parks and Recreation Department, one of the largest municipal parks systems in the nation. From our pristine beaches, parks, and golf courses, to swimming lessons, fitness classes, and sports clinics, our Parks Department offers a wide array of facilities and dozens of programs for children, adults, and senior citizens to enjoy.   Sign up to get notified of new program offerings and seasonal events that are coming your way using the "Notify Me" button on this page.
