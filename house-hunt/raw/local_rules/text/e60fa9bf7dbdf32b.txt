             ** Rules and Regulations **


1.   Written application to the Administration and approval by the
     Chief of Police must be given before any block party may be held
     which utilizes public thoroughfares. Submit a completed Block
     Party Permit Application at least 30 days prior to your proposed
     date.

2.   One member of the group should be named at Block Party
     Coordinator.

3.   The coordinator will be responsible for notifying, in writing, all
     residents living on the block of the date and time of the block
     party at least one week before the scheduled date. Block Party
     permits will not be issued for personal parties that do not include
     the other residents living on the block.

4.   All activity on the public roadway must be terminated at dusk.

5.   Barricades will be placed at the corners of the intersections to be
     blocked. The coordinator is responsible for erecting and
     dismantling the barricades. No major intersections or major
     streets may be blocked.

6.   No street shall be completely blocked by any block party. Street
     access must be available for persons wishing to use the public
     right-of-way for pedestrian or vehicular access to their property
     on the block and for emergency vehicles,

7.   The Hawthorne Police Department may order the party ended and
     the street opened should the above rules be violated, should a
     safety hazard be recognized, or in the event the party becomes
     unruly.
