Parking: Oceanside LIRR
Parking: Oceanside LIRR
Parking permits are required for the Oceanside parking district. These permits were created in order to prevent non-residents from parking in town-maintained commuter lots. The fee is $3 for a one-year registration. The license period is August 1 to July 31.
Please download, complete, and sign the Oceanside Special Parking District Application. A copy of your vehicle registration must accompany this application. If your current address is not listed on the vehicle registration, you will also be required to present proof of residency, such as a utility bill, tax bill, deed to the house, or a notarized lease agreement. Please make checks or money orders payable to Hempstead Town Clerk. Please enclose a stamped, self-addressed return envelope. Please mail completed, signed applications along with payment, copy of registration, and self-addressed, stamped return envelope to the Hempstead Town Clerk.
