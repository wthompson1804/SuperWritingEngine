How to Obtain a Temporary Placard
Forms available:
Police Records Division
https://www.state.nj.us/mvc/pdf/vehicles/SP-68.pdf
Call 609-292-6500 x 5061 for application form SP-68
Complete top portion, physician completes certification on bottom.
Bring completed form
AND $4 check payable to NJ MVC
to the police station, placard will be issued by the Police Chief
DO NOT SUBMIT TO MVC
, placards are issued by your local Police Department.
How to Obtain a Permanent Placard
Forms available:
Police Records Division
https://www.state.nj.us/mvc/pdf/vehicles/SP-41.pdf
Call 609-292-6500 ext. 5061 for application form SP-41
Complete front side, physician completes certification on the back side.
FOR FASTER SERVICE, the MVC STRONGLY recommends bringing the form in-person to any Motor Vehicles location (NO APPOINTMENT NECESSARY) or mail application
*
to:
Motor Vehicle Commission
Special Plate Unit
P.O. Box 015
Trenton, NJ 08666 to 0015
*Processing time up to 3 months.
