Resident Parking Stickers
Resident Parking Stickers are issued pursuant to
City of Milford Ordinance Sec. 14-12 (PDF)
.
Enforcement: Per City of Milford Ordinance Sec. 14-13: May 1st to September 30th
, no person shall park a motor vehicle in the parking areas at Gulf Beach or Walnut Beach marked and designated by appropriate signs as an unrestricted area without a Resident Parking Sticker, or $40/day parking charge, or $5/hour parking charge, or $250 seasonal parking sticker. Prices subject to change.
Resident Parking Stickers are available at the Tax Collector's Office.
Y
ou must present your current motor vehicle registration(s) and your taxes must be paid up to date
. Additional information is needed if your vehicle registration does not reflect a Milford address/tax town.
The vehicle registration is to verify the vehicle is currently registered to a Milford taxpayer. This cannot be determined from your tax bill. For example, your July 1, 2026 tax bill was based upon the vehicle you owned on one day October 1, 2025 (the date the Assessor's Grand List of taxable property was compiled). When looking at your tax bill it is only known that your car was registered October 1, 2025.

Reprint Vehicle Registration:
If you need a copy of your vehicle registration DMV allows you to reprint your registration at no cost. View the
Reprint Vehicle Registration Instruction (PDF)
, or visit the
CT DMV reprint registration portal website
.
Sticker Placement
: Per City Ordinance, please place the resident parking sticker on the lower right-hand (passenger side) corner of the rear windshield or passenger side rear bumper.
Important note regarding address changes:
If you have a Connecticut vehicle registration, driver's license, or ID card, Connecticut law requires residents to report address changes to the DMV within 48 hours of the change of address. More information is available on
DMV's website
or
visit the CT DMV address change portal website
.
Replacement Sticker Information:
The City is not responsible for Lost, Stolen, or Destroyed stickers. There will be a $25 charge for a replacement sticker if the original one is not returned. Replacement stickers shall be provided free of charge to any eligible person who transfers their registration to a new vehicle or has a rear windshield or rear bumper replaced and provides satisfactory proof thereof.
Please Note: Replacement Sticker Fee is payable by cash or check only.
