Water Front Trail & Park Master Plan (2009)
The Waterfront Trail and Park Master Plan was adopted by the Peekskill Common Council in August 2009 after extensive public input. It serves as a guide for future park improvements along the Hudson River shoreline from McGregory Brook to Lent's Cove. The Site Reconnaissance Report provides an overview of the existing conditions of the shoreline in 2009.
