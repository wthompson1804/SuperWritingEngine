Winter Street Parking
Winter Street Parking Regulations Repealed
The City of Middletown is pleased to announce that overnight parking on City streets will be permitted year-round, starting December 1st. Seasonal winter parking restrictions have been officially repealed.
Snow Alert System
To ensure safe and efficient snow removal during winter storms, the City will issue a
Snow Alert
when snow-clearing operations are anticipated. During a Snow Alert, street parking will be prohibited until the alert is lifted.**
Important Information
Towing & Fines: Vehicles not removed from the streets during a Snow Alert will be towed at the owner’s expense, and fines will begin at $100.
Lawn Parking Violations: Parking on lawns remains a violation of City Code. Beginning Spring 2024, the City’s Code Enforcement will begin actively enforcing this regulation.
Stay Informed
The City has set up multiple channels to keep residents informed about Snow Alerts and weather-related parking updates:
1. Weather Alert Hotline: Call 845-346-1414 for daily updates during the winter months.
2. City Website & Social Media: Snow Alerts and updates will be posted on the City’s website and official social media pages.
3. Radio Stations: Tune in to local stations for notifications.
4. Emergency Alert System: Sign up to receive notifications directly to your phone or email.
