Animal Control
As of July 1, 2023, all animal control services will be provided by the
Bergen County Department of Health
. Animal Control can be reached 24/7 at
201-229-4616
or through the Bergen County Public Safety Dispatch Center at 201-785-8505.
Please note: Animal Control is not a pest control service and will not remove healthy wildlife from any property. An Animal Control Officer (ACO) can remove wildlife from living quarters for a fee payable by the resident at the time of the service.
For more information on services provided,
visit the Bergen County Animal Control page.
For animal bites, please contact the Environmental team at 201-568-3450 ext. 517.
