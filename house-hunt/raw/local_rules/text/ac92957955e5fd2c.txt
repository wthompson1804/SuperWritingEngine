Dog & Cat Licensing
This is just a reminder that any dog or cat applications received after March 1 by the Board of Health, will incur a $10.00 late fee in addition to the license fee.  Any application that is not received by March 15
th
will be subject to a summons for $74 under the Borough Ordinance Code 129-2.
The Health Office is at 916 River Road, FL 2, Historic Borough Hall.
Licenses
All dogs and cats over the age of 6 months must be licensed. Renewals of dog and cat licenses are due by January 31 of each year. A late fee of $10 will be charged if you do not license by February 28th as well as a $74 summons for unlicensed pet.  Owners must show proof of rabies vaccination in order to receive a license.
All previous license renewals for your pets were mailed out by email before the end of the year. Please check your spam & junk mail folders for the renewal email.
Call the Board of Health @ 201- 943-1700 EXT. 6007 if you have any questions.
