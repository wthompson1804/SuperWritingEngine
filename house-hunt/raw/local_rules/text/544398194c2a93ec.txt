Public Space Use Permit
Click here to jump down to the "Regulations" section.
The Village of Larchmont allows for any storefront business to apply for a permit to use the sidewalk directly in front of their establishment for certain commercial activities. This program was previously known as the Sidewalk Café or Outdoor Dining program, which was expanded in 2020 to include retail businesses and the display of wares.
The 2026 permit is online. Please access the form here.
Prior to submitting an application, business owners must review the
Regulations for Use of Any Public Spaces
below.
Regulations for Use of Any Public Spaces
The rules, regulations and criteria set forth herein shall be deemed conditions of any permit issued hereunder. Failure to comply with, or violation of, any of these rules, regulations or criteria shall, upon determination of the Village Code Enforcement Officer, will result in the revocation and termination of the Permit without refund of any fees paid. Any party whose permit is so revoked and terminated may appeal the Code Enforcement Officer’s determination to the Village Administrator or designee.
The Applicant MUST be the owner or operator of the business using the requested public space. The completed application shall be submitted online. Paper copies, if requested, shall be submitted to the Village Clerk's Office. A permit shall be issued upon compliance with all conditions set forth herein, including, but not limited to, approval of location by the Code Enforcement Officer or designee. The Code Enforcement Officer, at their discretion, may determine that no use may occur in certain locations even if the other criteria set forth herein are met. Payment shall be submitted online. If a check payment is requested, it shall be submitted to the Village Clerk's Office.
Permits may be issued for sidewalk use directly in front of the business of the applicant ONLY. An expansion of the permitted area beyond the applicant’s frontage may be requested from the Code Enforcement Officer through the application process. The expansion process applies if the applicant wishes to expand into the areas of the adjacent storefront(s). The decision to grant or deny such requests will consider many factors, including but not limited to:
the stated need;
the potential to cause an undue hardship on other business owners;
the potential pedestrian safety impacts.
If the applicant wishes to be considered for an expansion, they must provide Adjacent Merchant Authorization as part of their permit application. Authorization is completed during the application process. It is incumbent on the applicant to ensure that they have received authorization from the adjacent merchant; failure to do so will result in application denial or permit revocation.
The Code Enforcement Officer may, in their discretion, determine a different location for the applicant to use that is not limited to being directly in front of the applicant’s storefront based on safety. The Village will attempt to provide a new space that is equal to the same square footage that the applicant requested, to the best extent possible.
Any permit granted for use of the public space does not confer any rights to Village property upon the Permit Holder and will be revocable at any time at will by the Village.
It is incumbent on Permit Holders to remove their items from the sidewalk in cases of inclement weather. In addition, the Code Enforcement Officer or designee is authorized to direct any Permit Holders to remove their items. This can include, but is not limited to, safety hazards, weather conditions, utility repairs, special events, or emergencies. Permit Holders that do not comply with this section will risk revocation of their permit.
Permits are valid from April 01, 2026 thru December 31, 2026, or by determination of the Code Enforcement Officer their designee, or the Village Administrator or their designee. Valid permits are required at all times in order for establishments to participate.
In order to protect the character of the downtown, Permit Holders who do not make use of requested sidewalk space for two consecutive weeks will forfeit the ability to use the public space with no permit fee refund. Exceptions are for weather conditions which render the space temporarily unusable or the Village has required the use of the space for Village purposes.
Permit Holders must supply the Village with the Village’s standard indemnity and hold harmless agreement (attached herewith), and an insurance certificate naming the Village as an additional insured in the amount of $1 million on all policies of insurance covering third party liability and workmen's compensation shall be required prior to the placement of any furnishings on Village right-of-way.
Permit Holders may use the permitted public space during the following days and times: Sunday through Wednesday, 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM; and Thursday through Saturday, 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM. Any music, whether live or recorded and regardless of amplification, requires a cabaret license from the Village Clerk's Office. Furnishings on public sidewalks would ideally be removed after closing but may be left against the building if properly secured so as to not create a hazard. No furnishings shall left on streets, parking spaces, or any space outside the permitted area or property of the permit holder.
If the Permit Holder places their furnishings in the permit area, or relocates their furnishings against the building, in an unsightly manner as determined by the Code Enforcement Officer, the Permit Holder shall comply with any order to remediate such condition. Non-compliance shall result in revocation of the permit. Appeals by the Permit Holder can be made to the Village Administrator.
Permit Holders may, with permission and subject to inspection and approval by the Code Enforcement Officer:
Use umbrellas and patio heaters.
Have lighting: Lighting should be for ambience only. Light temperature should be warm, not bright white. No floodlights, strobes or color-changing lights allowed. Exterior lighting shall be designed so as to minimize spillage to adjoining properties.
If you need electricity for lights, you may be able to connect to Village light pole outlets with Village permission.
SPECIFY if you will be connecting to a Village outlet, for what purpose and quantity of items to be connected.
If you cannot connect to light pole outlets because of your equipment or location, please show where you would run electric cables and how you would make them safe.
The Permit Holder is responsible for:
Securing all furnishings properly and attractively. No furnishings may be placed outside the permitted area.
Assuring cleanliness and removal of trash and debris arising from the use of the public space.
Assuring safe pedestrian movement along the sidewalk being used or adjacent to the area of use (four feet clearance between the area of sidewalk used and the curb).
Maintaining an attractive space. All materials used must be in good condition and cleaned regularly, all plants must be fresh, and pots should be decorative and heavy enough to withstand strong winds. Dead plant materials must be removed immediately.
The rules, regulations and criteria set forth herein shall be deemed conditions of any permit issued hereunder. Failure to comply with, or violation of, any of these rules, regulations or criteria shall, upon determination of the Village Code Enforcement Officer, result in the revocation and termination of the Permit without refund of any fees paid. Any party whose permit is so revoked and terminated may appeal the Code Enforcement Officer’s determination to the Village Administrator or designee.
