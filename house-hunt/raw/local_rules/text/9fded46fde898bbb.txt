Permit Parking
Permit parking is a convenient option that saves you money. Customers may purchase most permits on a semiannual or annual basis at a considerable discount from the published meter rates.
In certain locations, permits are restricted to area residents only. In this case, proof of residency is required for purchase, which may be a copy of a house deed, lease, or current utility bill in the resident/parker's name. Permits can be obtained for parking garages and surface lots.

Permit Waiting Lists
Certain parking facilities may be sold out and have established waiting lists. Waiting list requests are accepted online.  Register as a new user
here
and then submit a parking permit waiting list request.
Please be advised that priority on the waiting list for residential parking will be given to any resident with no alternative means of parking.
