Dog Licenses - How to apply
🐶
Hello Milford Dog Owner,
🎉
June is Dog Licensing Month!
🎉
❤️
Licensing helps keep your furry friend safe and ensures they get home if lost.
CURRENTLY licensed dogs, with up to date rabies, use this link to RENEW
:
Dog License Renewal
(renew on IQS site)
Connecticut law requires any dog six months of age or older to have a current rabies vaccination and license.
Your dog’s license must be renewed every year in June. This year the license is valid from July 1, 2026 - June 30, 2027.
📅
A late penalty of $1.00 per month begins to accrue on July 1, 2026.
💸
Avoid late fees – renew by June 30!
PLEASE NOTE THAT WE ARE NOT AUTHORIZED TO ISSUE NEW LICENSES UNTIL JUNE 1, 2026. WE WILL HOLD ANY APPLICATIONS FOR PROCESSING UNTIL THAT TIME.
