Accela Citizens Permitting Portal (ACA)
Effective November 5, 2024, the Village is pleased to announce the move to electronic building permitting for most building permit applications via the Accela Citizen Portal.  Please review the Accela - ACA FAQ's and see instructions below for registering for an account, connecting to your property, applying and paying for a building permit online and reviewing permits on parcels in the Village.
Start here to being the process and review our FAQ's and helpful information below.
Please note - You will need to create an account to view property records.
