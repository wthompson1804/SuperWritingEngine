Frequently Asked Questions
Parking Permit
5
All Content
What proof of residency do I need to apply for a parking permit?
Your vehicle registration and driver’s license must list a valid Town of Harrison address. Additional documents may be required, such as a tax bill, lease, utility bill, or payroll stub.
Parking Permit
What happens if I receive a summons for not displaying a permit?
All summonses for no permit must be resolved in court, even if you later obtain a permit. If your permit is improperly displayed, you may receive a summons and must appear in court if you wish to contest it.
Parking Permit
Can I use my parking permit for a different vehicle?
No. Permits are issued for the specific vehicle listed on your application. If you sell or transfer your vehicle, you must remove and return the permit before applying for a new one.
Parking Permit
What if I live in Harrison but my vehicle is not registered here?
You may apply for a Special Circumstance Resident Parking Permit. Issuance is subject to approval by the Police Department. Applications are accepted Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–4:00 PM.
Parking Permit
How can I stay informed about town emergencies and updates?
We recommend registering with the
Town of Harrison Emergency Notification Service
and following the Harrison Police on X @harrisonpolice for alerts on snow removal, road closures, and other important notifications.
