Street Sweeping
The City of Bridgeport is kicking off the spring season with a renewed commitment to cleaner streets through the Clean Sweep Project. The initiative, led by the City’s Public Facilities Department's Roadway Division, will focus on clearing debris and improving conditions along major roadways throughout the city.
Work will begin with a systematic sweep of all main roads, enhancing safety, appearance, and the overall quality of life for residents.
To support the effort, alternative-side parking regulations will be strictly enforced during scheduled cleaning periods.
