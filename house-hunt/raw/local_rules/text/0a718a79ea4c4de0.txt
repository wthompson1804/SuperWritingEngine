Parking Meters and Enforcement
Parking Enforcement Hours Update
***Downtown parking meters are enforced Monday through Saturday, from 8:00am - 8:00pm.***
Welcome to our New Location at
255 John Street, Bridgeport, CT, 06604.
Handicapped parking and building entry access
is available from Broad Street only. Upon entry, you will be escorted to the Parking Enforcement's office.
Parking Meters
The City of Bridgeport has enlisted the help of electronic parking meters from IPS Group. Please see directions on how to use meters at
BridgeportParking.com
.
Parking meters are enforced Monday through Saturday between
8:00 AM and 8:00 PM
. If you do not pay for parking, you risk receiving a ticket.
Parking Enforcement inquiries and payments
All Parking Enforcement inquiries and payments are done in person at:
Parking Violations Bureau
255 John Street
Bridgeport CT, 06604
Phone:
203-581-5287
Hours of Operation:
8:00 AM to 3:30 PM, Monday - Friday
Payments or appeal
In-person payments are accepted
by check, cash, or debit card. Large amounts of loose change (coins) will not be accepted unless properly counted and in coin rolls.
All appeals or payments must be made within fourteen (14) calendar days from the issue date.
Online Inquiries
For an appeal or payment, use the following site:
Pay My Parking Ticket
By Mail
For an appeal, send the ticket with a brief explanation of the circumstances.
For a payment, send the ticket with a check or money order to the above listed address. Make check payable to City of Bridgeport.
Do Not Send Cash.
For vehicles Booted, Towed, or needing a DMV Release, the only accepted payment is Cash.
