Recreation Board
The Board shall report to the
Superintendent of Recreation
and shall perform the following duties:
Conduct regular meetings once a month and report to the City Manager the names of inactive members to be replaced by the Mayor and Council.
Review and comment on the objectives and goals of the Department of Recreation.
Review and comment on the recreational and cultural program submitted by the
Superintendent of Recreation
.
Review and comment on the annual budget proposal prepared by the
Superintendent of Recreation
.
Entertain public proposals and suggestions and articulate same to the
Superintendent of Recreation
.
