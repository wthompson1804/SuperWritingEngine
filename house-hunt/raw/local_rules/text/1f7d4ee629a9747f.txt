Police Department
Overview
The Plainfield Police Department is committed to providing exemplary police service to Plainfield residents and guests. We are a Guardian model based police agency looking to ensure the health and welfare of all. We are dedicated to serving the public with Honor, Accountability and Transparency.

Mission
The Mission of the Plainfield Police Department is to safeguard the community through ethical policing, collaboration, and technological innovation. We strive to build trust and advance equitable public safety by working alongside residents, respecting the rights of all, and embracing accountability.

Police Department Recruitment Plan
The goal of the Plainfield Police Department Recruitment Plan is to attract qualified individuals to pursue a career with the Plainfield Police Department. The objective is to achieve an overall racial and gender composition of the Department in comparison to the service population of the City through the department's recruiting activities.
