White Plains Building Department Forms List
Instructions
Access the needed form from the list and then fill in or print the blank form as needed.
The forms are in PDF format and can be viewed, filled, and printed with Adobe Reader. Note that electronically filled forms cannot be saved. Print the completed form before leaving the active form.
Complete the appropriate application form, get it notarized, and bring it to the Building Department.
Applications are accepted between 9 a.m. and 1 p.m. on normal business days.
If the form does not open, make sure your Adobe Reader is updated by visiting the
Adobe resource
to download the most up-to-date PDF viewer.
