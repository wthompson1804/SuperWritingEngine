Zoning Permits
** Please refer to
NEW Zoning Regulations
, effective 2/19/2024.**
Work that requires a Zoning Permit includes, but is not limited to:
Accessory structures, such as sheds, swimming pools, HVAC equipment
Interior or exterior modifications to your home, including the construction of decks, additions, & garages
Use of your home for a private business or "home occupation"
Commercial tenant fit-ups or changes, signage, and expansions
Certain work within the flood hazard zone
For
driveway permits
, please start with this
application form
and submit the form to
DPW
.
Click below on Zoning Permit application form(s):
Single-Family or Two-Family projects
Multi-Family, Mixed Use or Commercial projects
All projects with excavation, grading or ground disturbance
All projects within the Flood Hazard Zone Overlay
You must provide sufficient detailed and accurate information to submit an application for Zoning Permit. Incomplete applications will not be accepted.
Applicants should fill out only the top portions on the form.  Applicant
AND
property owner must
sign
the application.  Zoning Staff will fill out the rest of the form during review of your project.
In addition to the application form, you will need to submit
plans that adequately depict your project
.  At a minimum, you must provide
THREE (3) COPIES
of:
☐ Site plan indicating the location of the proposed work relative to the property lines. This plan must be drawn at a standard scale and be legible.
☐ Plans (architectural, construction, or floor) indicating the current and proposed conditions. This plan must be dimensionally accurate and legible.
Depending on the nature of your project, you may need to provide further detailed plans and/or a land survey.  Please note, properties in a
flood zone
require additional documentation.
Submitting your application:
Hard copies
of plan sets and your completed application form must be either
dropped off
at the P&Z office at
Room 129
City Hall, between 8:30AM and 4:00PM Monday through Friday or
mailed
to: Planning & Zoning, Rm. 129, City of Norwalk, PO Box 5125, Norwalk, CT 06856-5125.
We will need three (3) paper copies of your plans, four (4) for commercial projects, and one (1) copy of the completed application form.
The
application fee
will be collected when the application is submitted, when applicable, and the zoning fee will be collected when the application is approved.  We accept payment by credit or debit card only.
AFTER THE FACT PERMIT – DOUBLE THE FEE LISTED!
ZONING PERMIT APPLICATIONS WITHIN A FLOOD ZONE ARE SUBJECT TO ADDITIONAL $250 FEE
Your Zoning Permit will be internally circulated to receive Conservation/Wetlands review and then you will be contacted by phone and advised you may pick up your Zoning/Conservation approvals. You will then need to visit the
Building Department
to begin Building permit review.
Additional materials may be needed prior to the issuance of your
Building Permit
.
Please refer to the Building Permit procedure below:
Projects Involving Residential (1 & 2 Family Residences) Properties
Projects Involving Commercial Tenant Fit-Ups, New Commercial Construction, or Residences with 3+ units
Does my project require a Zoning Permit?
An approved Zoning Permit is required in advance of applying for a Building Permit. Some activities, such as the construction of a small shed, are exempt from Building Permits, but still require a Zoning Permit. To learn more about the fees associated with a Zoning Permit, click
here
Does my project require a Zoning Permit?
.
Many home improvements such as re-paving, window, siding and roofing replacement, and cabinet replacement do not need zoning approval, but may need approval from other agencies. In these instances, the Zoning Office may still need to review the plans to confirm they are exempt from Zoning permitting.
