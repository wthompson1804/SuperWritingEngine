Welcome to the City of Clifton!
On behalf of the Mayor, City Council, and City Manager, we are glad that you have chosen the City of Clifton as your new home!
Now that you’ve arrived, we hope you will take advantage of all the amenities our city has to offer, from basic city services to our 39 beautiful parks, public libraries, and many fun activities for residents of all ages.
The City of Clifton also utilizes a mass communication system known as “Rave Alert” (formerly Swiftreach) that we strongly encourage all residents to sign up for [Rave's Smart911 Profile]. This allows us to use phone calls [and SMS] to send out emergency notifications and important announcements to residents. You can sign up for Rave Alert Smart911
here
.
Below you will find more helpful links as well as a City Hall department directory. We hope this information helps you get settled into your new community.
Note: All existing Swift911 accounts have been migrated to Rave Alert Smart911.
