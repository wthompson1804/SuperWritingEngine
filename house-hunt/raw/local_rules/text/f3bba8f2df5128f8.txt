Animal Control
Milford Animal Control is a Division of the Milford Police Department that services the City of Milford and the Town of Orange. Milford Animal Control is responsible for enforcing State Laws and City Ordinances regarding animals. We service the public by investigating animal bites and dog complaints, and humanely capturing stray/roaming dogs. We also enforce leash laws, license laws, and rabies vaccination laws on dogs and cats.
All visitors should enter through Silver Sands State Park in order to reach our facility at 1 Silver Sands Parkway
All dogs and cats (regardless if they are indoor or outdoor) are required to have an up to date rabies vaccine administered by a trained professional per State Law.
