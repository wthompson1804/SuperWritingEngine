Animal Control
Residents should call 911 or Bridgeport Police Dispatch at
203-576-7671
to report an animal complaint at any time of day.
For
emergencies also,
call 911 dispatch at the above number.
Please, do
not
call the shelter with an
emergency
!
If you are interested in adopting an animal from our shelter, our adoptable animals are available to view on
Facebook
and
Petfinder
.
If a potential adopter is very interested in a pet, they may submit a completed application with proof of home ownership or notarized letter/lease via
animal.shelter@bridgeportct.gov
or fax. Staff will review and pre-approve the application pending a ‘meet-and-greet’.
Animal Control related questions that are Not urgent animal complaints may contact
203-576-7727
;
Press
1 to speak to an ACO/Staff Member or leave a message. This number should be used for general inquiries only, not for emergencies.
Police reports regarding animals (such as dog bites) can be requested at the Records Division
203-581-5270
.
