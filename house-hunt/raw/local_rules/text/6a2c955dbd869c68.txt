Animal Carcass Removal
Overview
The Sanitation Bureau will remove small dead animals, that are located on City streets or sidewalks. The City will not come on to a homeowner's private property to remove a dead animal.
Contact Information
To report a dead animal in the roadway, contact the Bureau at 914-235-4654
