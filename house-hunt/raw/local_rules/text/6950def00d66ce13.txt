To keep updated on North Bergen news, sign up for the weekly online newsletter. Every Monday you’ll get an email filled with links to information about upcoming and recent events. Stay informed about local news and ensure you know what activities are coming to your neighborhood. To sign up simply go to the bottom of the township web page at www.northbergen.org and enter your email address where it says, “Stay in touch with your township.” You won’t receive anything other than weekly updates filled with town information and events. #northbergen
