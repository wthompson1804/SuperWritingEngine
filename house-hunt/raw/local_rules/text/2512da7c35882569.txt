The NBPA owns and manages several parking facilities, mostly in the downtown area, that provide convenient hourly, daily and monthly parking for New Brunswick's many destinations and businesses.
We take great pride in providing clean, safe and affordable facilities. Every facility is cleaned on a daily basis, security personnel patrol facilities regularly, and parking rates are moderately set to cover our operating, debt service and capital reserve requirements.
Vehicle storage is prohibited. If you plan on leaving your vehicle for longer than 48 hours, please call Parking Services at 732-667-8100.
