Animal Redemptions
Any impounded animal found roaming can be picked up during our kennel hours.
To redeem an impounded animal, an owner/caretaker must provide proof that the animal is up to date with its rabies vaccine per Connecticut State Law.
A fee of $15, per impounded animal is due upon picking up your pet.
An additional $15 per animal will be charged for each day after notification of the impounded animal.
Any pet left for multiple days after notification can result in a fine for abandonment and the owner can face a felony charge for animal cruelty.
Animal Control only accepts
cash (no bills larger than $20), bank checks, and money orders
. We
do not
accept personal checks or debit/credit cards.
