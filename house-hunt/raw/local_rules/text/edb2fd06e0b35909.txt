Q
: If I have a handicap parking spot, am I able to also park in a regular parking spot?
A
: No, you can only park in your designated handicap parking space. If you park in a different spot without a parking permit you will be in violation and will be subject to a summons.

Q
: If I live in a certain zone, can I only park in that zone or can I park anywhere in North Bergen?
A
: With a residential permit, you may park anywhere in North Bergen, except where it is signed for Zone Specific permits or it is a metered parking area. You must have the Zone Specific permit to park on those signed streets. Be sure to follow the streets signs and feed the meters if parked at one.
Q
: Do I need a permit with a handicap placard?
A
: Yes, you need a residential permit to park even if you have a handicap placard.

Q
: Do I have to be a resident to apply for a parking space?
A
: Yes, only North Bergen residents can apply for a parking space. Your driver's license and vehicle registration must reflect this when you apply. North Bergen residents can apply for a parking space in one of our municipal lots either in person, at our office or over the phone.
Q
:What do I do if I want to contest a ticket?
A
: If you would like to dispute a summons, you may contact the Court at
(201) 392-2088
to schedule an appearance.
Q
: If I have a commercial vehicle, where can I park in North Bergen?
A
: Commercial vehicles are not permitted to park in North Bergen unless they are rendering a service or are in a metered area patronizing our business districts. Resident owned commercial vehicles must be parked off street, in your garage or driveway, or you can check with our office for available space in one of our municipal parking lots.
Contractor commercial vehicles may park on a residential street, for up to 8 hours, if they place cones in the front and back of the vehicle to indicate that they are rendering a service. Contractors should contact our office for available permits if they are working on longer term projects.

Q
:  Is a person with a wheelchair symbol license plate/placard required to deposit money when parking at a metered space?
A
:  Yes. When parking at a metered space, a driver with a disability placard/license plate must put the appropriate amount of money in the meter. If the vehicle will occupy the space for
more than the maximum amount of time allotted on the meter,
the driver must fill the meter to the
maximum amount allowed
. This will permit the vehicle to occupy the space for up to 24 hours without having to come back and refill the meter. A municipality may require the vehicle to vacate the space
after 24 hours.
The 24 hour limit is set forth in N.J.S.A. 39: 4-207
Q
: How long do the residential parking permits last?
A
: If the residential parking permit is purchased at the beginning of the renewal period, it will last 3 years. If purchased after the renewal period has begun, you will get the remainder of the time left.
