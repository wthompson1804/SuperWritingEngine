Animal Control Services
The Borough of Hawthorne contracts with Tyco Animal Control Services. Call Tyco at 201-652-4554 - all inquires should be made to this line, which is answered by a machine.
Dead Animals on Public Property
When finding a dead animal on public property, call Tyco at 201-652-4554 and please leave an accurate location
including the town
. Dead animals are picked up by Tyco Monday through Friday only. On weekends, call Hawthorne Police at 973-427-1800 and someone will be dispatched to remove the dead animal.
Dead Animals on Private Property
Residents are responsible for any fee to remove a dead animal or animals from private property. To have the animal removed, you can call Tyco at 201-652-4554 or another company that provides this service.
Do not call the Police
to remove a dead animal from private property.
Nuisance Wildlife
As with dead animal removal on private property, Tyco Animal Control Services charges a fee for trapping wildlife on private property. Call Tyco at 201-652-4554 and leave a message. Tyco will contact you regarding your wildlife nuisance.
Owned Animals
Call Tyco at 201-652-4554 and leave a message and Tyco will return your call and attempt to resolve your issue.
Lost Pet
Call 201-652-4554 and leave a message and Tyco will return your call and attempt to help you find your pet. All stray dogs or cats will be returned to their owners at the Police Headquarters.
