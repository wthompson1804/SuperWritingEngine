Parking Permit Fees
Please note:
Proof of residence must be shown when purchasing parking permits.
Parking Permits may be obtained from the Mount Vernon Parking Bureau, located at 3 East Sidney Avenue, Mount Vernon.
The Office is open from 8:30 am to 4 pm Monday to Friday.
The telephone number is 914-665-2323.
