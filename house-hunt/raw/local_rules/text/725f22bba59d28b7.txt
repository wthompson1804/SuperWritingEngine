Community Events
The Borough of Hawthorne offers a wide range community events to bring residents together for entertainment and remembrance, including a Memorial Day Parade, an annual 9-11 Memorial Service at our 9-11 Memorial on Goffle Road, a Veterans Day Service, a Hawthorne Day celebration with food and entertainment, an annual Christmas Tree lighting ceremony at the Municipal Building, and a slate of summer concerts and movies at the Boro Bandshell during the summer months. For more information, see the links  in the menu to the left, or contact the Mayor's Office at 973-427-1168.
