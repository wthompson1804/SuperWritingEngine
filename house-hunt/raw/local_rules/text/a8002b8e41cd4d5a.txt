Requirements:

 1.       All work, (i.e. erosion and sediment control, sediment management, subsurface
          connections, etc.) will be performed in accordance with all specifications and
          requirements of DPW, and all necessary permits will be secured before any work is
          started.
 2.       If the drain system is not accepted as a municipal public improvement by the Stormwater
          Management Officer (SMO), the drain system and connection shall remain private and
          shall be maintained by the property owner(s).
 3.       All conditions affecting the City’s MS-4 shall be subject to the approval and inspection
          by the SMO. The applicant must notify DPW at least two (2) business days prior to
          commencing work and at least two (2) business days prior to final restoration.
 4.       Any connection shall not be removed without the written consent of the SMO.
 5.       If it is determined by the SMO that this connection is a detriment to the municipal storm
          sewer system, this connection may be removed without notice to the property owner(s).

 Indemnification:
 The applicant/property owner shall indemnify and save the City harmless from any loss, damage
 or expense, including reasonable attorney’s fees and costs, claims or suits arising out of and in
 connection with the installation and connection to the City’s MS4. In addition, the SMO makes
 no guarantee that its MS4 will not become surcharged or otherwise overburdened and that water
 from the MS4 will not back-up through the connection onto the owner’s property. By making a
 connection, the applicant/owner assumes all of the risk and liability that may arise from it.

 This authorization and agreement is binding on the present owner(s) of record, all heirs, assigns
 and successors.
