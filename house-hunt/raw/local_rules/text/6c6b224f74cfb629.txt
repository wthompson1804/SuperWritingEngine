Becoming a member of one of our  advisory boards lets residents volunteer in city decision-making, bringing local insight to committees and commissions that guide planning, services, and civic life.

HACPAC
The Hackensack Performing Arts Center showcases theater, music, and visual arts, bringing artists and neighbors together through performances, exhibits, and community events.
Recreation Center
Hackensack’s M&M Recreation Center offers youth sports, fitness, and community programs, with courts, multipurpose rooms, and spaces for seniors, events, and year-round activities.
Johnson Public Library
The Johnson Public Library serves Hackensack with books, digital resources, classes, and programs for all ages, supporting learning, job search help, and lifelong community connection.
