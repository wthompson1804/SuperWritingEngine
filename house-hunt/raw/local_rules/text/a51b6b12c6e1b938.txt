Alternate Side Parking Suspensions
Alternate Side Parking (ASP)
  regulations allow for
street
    cleaning
. The City of New York suspends ASP rules on specific legal and religious holidays. The City
  also
  suspends ASP rules due to weather and emergencies. Check
NYC 311
or
Notify NYC
for emergency suspension information.

*Major Legal Holiday:
On major legal holidays, you can park at a No Stopping, No Standing, or No Parking sign
  if the rule is not normally in effect seven days a week (for example, No Standing Anytime). Otherwise, you cannot.
  Parking meter regulations are also suspended on major legal holidays.
On all other holidays, only street cleaning rules are suspended. All other regulations remain in effect.

Emergency Suspensions
NYC may suspend ASP regulations because of inclement weather or emergencies. The decision is sometimes made late in
  the day since weather patterns can change fast.
NYC restores ASP regulations after a snowfall. This way plows can begin removing snow and ice from curbside lanes.
  Plowing helps clear parking lanes and catch basins, reducing the chance of flooding. It also helps return street
  cleaning operations back to normal.
Temporary Suspensions
NYC may suspend ASP regulations in parts of the city to post new regulations. This happens when the
Department of Sanitation
(DSNY) changes street cleaning
  rules.
  Suspending regulations for a short time allows NYC DOT to update signs. These suspensions do not affect parking
  rules
  other than ASP rules.
DSNY provides a weeklong grace period after new signage is posted. During this time crews will clean streets
  according to the new schedule. They will not issue summonses.
Enforcement of the new rules begin after the one-week grace period.
