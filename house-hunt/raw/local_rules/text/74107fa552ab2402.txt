Mental Health, Substance Abuse and Wellness Guide
Recent times have put into clear focus the importance of safeguarding the health of ourselves and our loved ones. Indeed, with health at the forefront of Americans’ minds, we must remember a holistic approach to ensuring one’s well-being – including a prioritization of mental health in congruence with physical health.
With this commitment to mental health in mind, Supervisor John Ferretti and the Hempstead Town Board are pleased to provide a Mental Health and Wellness Resource Guide to residents. This informative piece contains the contact information for a host of crisis hotlines, outpatient psychiatric facilities, and substance abuse treatment support organizations. Anyone who is experiencing difficulties pertinent to the resources detailed in this pamphlet is encouraged to consider utilizing the support infrastructure available across Long Island.

FreeRehabCenters.org
This site provides a list of free, low-cost, sliding scale, and low-income assisted rehabilitation centers.
Addictions.com
This site provides information for people at any stage of their recovery journey, including information on different types of therapy/recovery options, warning signs, how to talk to a family member, definitions of different types of treatment, and a local treatment center directory.
Rehab.com
This site provides information about a variety of rehab and therapy services and resources, and may assist people who are early in their search and want a side-by-side view of local options. The site also provides reviews and Rehab Score metrics for local programs.

- A directory that helps individuals struggling with opioid addiction find and connect with methadone treatment centers in New York.
