OVERVIEW
New York State requires that all dogs over the age of 4 months be licensed.  In order to be licensed, the dog must have its current rabies vaccination certificate from the veterinarian.  The name of the owner must be on all health documents.  If the owner is under 18 years old, the parent/guardian will be considered the owner and their name must be on all health documents.
