To All Landscaper I Snow plowing Applicants:

The following documents are required for completing your landscaping permit for the current year.
Please bring with you photocopies of the following:

   ~   Certificate of insurance showing proof of workman's compensation/disability coverage or waiver
       from the NYS Workmen's Compensation Board
   ~   Certificate ofliability insurance listing the Town of Haverstraw as an additional insured
   ~   Vehicle registrations
   ~   Vehicle insurance cards
   ~   Drivers licenses of all employees operating your company vehicles
   ~   List of chemicals and fertilizers used & photocopy of each label
   ~   $50.00 fee in the form of cash, check, or money order

The following documents are required for completing your snow plowing permit for the current year.
Please bring with you photocopies of the following:

   ~   Certificate of insurance showing proof of workman's compensation/disability coverage or waiver
       from the NYS Workmen's Compensation Board
   ~   Certificate ofliability insurance listing the Town of Haverstraw as an additional insured
   ~   Vehicle registrations
   ~   Vehicle insurance cards
   ~   Drivers licenses of all employees operating your company vehicles
   ~   List of all customers names & addresses
   ~   $50.00 fee in the form of cash, check, or money order

All copies MUST be clearly legible or your paperwork will be returned to you.
License year is from January 1st- December 31st.
Feel free to contact my office if you have any additional questions.
