PARKING TICKETS
If you receive a City of Milford parking ticket
you can pay for it
online
,
in person
or by
mail
.
Please note that:
1. Failure to pay
will
result in late fees.
2. The Traffic Division must
receive your ticket payment or appeal within 9 days of issuance
to avoid late fees.
3. No vehicle may be parked or left disabled within any snow alert area or snow emergency area so designated and announced by commercial broadcast and press release.
To Pay Parking Tickets Online
Visit
www.milfordct.rmcpay.com
Have your ticket number, license plate number, or your VIN number ready to locate your ticket.
**Note:
If your parking ticket is
orange
. Please allow up to 5 business days for your ticket to be accessible online.
To Pay Parking Tickets in Person
Bring the parking ticket to Police HQ with payment in the form of cash, check, or money order only.
Credit and Debit cards cannot be processed in person
.
To Pay Parking Tickets by Mail
Do
not
mail cash, remit by check or money order.
*To ensure credit for payment, note the registration number (license plate #) or ticket number on your check's memo line. *
Checks/Money Orders for parking tickets can be made out to:
"Milford Police Department Traffic Division"
Checks can be sent to:
Milford Police Department
Attn: Traffic Division
430 Boston Post Road
Milford, CT 06460
If you believe a parking ticket was issued in error, you can file an
appeal
by completing the online form or by submitting a completed "Parking Ticket Appeal Form”
**THE WEBSITE AND FORM BELOW ARE FOR PARKING TICKETS ONLY**
To appeal online visit
https://milfordct.rmcpay.com/
IF your ticket is orange, please allow up to 5 business days for your ticket to be accessible online.
OR
Print form linked below and follow instructions to submit an appeal for a parking ticket
Parking Ticket Appeal Form (PDF)
To submit the form:
Mail it to: Milford Police Department c/o Traffic Division, 430 Boston Post Road Milford, CT 06460
Email it to
Traffic@milfordct.gov
Dropping it off in person at Police HQ

BEACH STICKERS
Ch 14 Sec. 14-12
Beach and other restricted parking areas — Restricted and unrestricted areas designated; parking sticker required for restricted area.
[Ord. of 4-4-2005]
(a)
The parking area adjacent to Gulf Beach or Walnut Beach or any other area designated by the Milford Police Department may be divided into a restricted area and an unrestricted area to be clearly marked and designated by appropriate signs
. No person shall park a vehicle in any restricted area unless
such vehicle shall have visibly displayed on the lower right-hand (passenger side) corner of the rear windshield or passenger side rear bumper,
a sticker for the current year certifying that the owner is a resident or taxpayer of the City.
Notwithstanding the foregoing, the board of Police Commissioners may designate up to eight parking spaces within the restricted area at Gulf Beach which do not require a sticker but which are subject to a thirty-minute time limit restriction.
How To Obtain a Sticker
Available starting January 1
st
of the current calendar year
Milford Resident/Taxpayer
City of Milford Annual Resident Parking stickers are available at the Milford Tax Office located at the Parson's Government Center, 70 West River Street Milford, CT.
Call 203-783-3280 or visit the
Tax Office
website for more information.
Non-Resident (Seasonal)
Season Passes are available for $250.00 at the Milford Recreation Department (70 West River Street). Season Passes are only valid for parking at Gulf Beach and Walnut Beach. Call for more information: 203-783-3280.
The purchaser will be instructed to tape the tag to their windshield. Officers will
not
honor "portable" stickers obviously designed to move from vehicle to vehicle in violation of the terms of issue.
