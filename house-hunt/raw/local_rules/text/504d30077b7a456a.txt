Street Sweeping
Beginning in May, town roadways are swept on a regular basis. Street cleaning not only appeals to the eyes, but it also pleases Mother Nature. Ridding our roadways of litter and debris protects our precious waterways. The process prevents contaminants from entering the storm drains and emptying into South Shore waters. Street sweeping efforts conclude in October.
