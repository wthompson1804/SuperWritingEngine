Construction PERMITS
The mission of the Construction Department is to ensure adequate construction and maintenance of buildings and structures throughout the Township and to protect the health, safety, and welfare of the residents and business owners. This is accomplished by issuing permits for residential, commercial, and industrial construction, performing various inspections throughout the project, and issuing final approval and occupancy certificates. The Department conducts inspections to determine that all work is completed per the State Uniform Construction Code and all applicable prior approvals. The State of New Jersey licenses all Uniform Construction Code inspectors. The Department is self-supporting through fees generated from permits and certificates.
To see the various Construction and Zoning Department forms, please go to the Township Forms page of this website.
