Dear Neighbors:

The City of White Plains believes in building connections and creating a vibrant,
inclusive space for all - and this year's programming reflects just that.
Please take a moment to explore this Guide, mark your calendars, and invite a
neighbor to join you in the many exciting programs and events planned for the
upcoming fall and winter seasons.

We have programs for residents of all ages, abilities, and interests, from toddler
classes to festive celebrations, we've got something to appeal to everyone.
Whether you're looking to learn something new, get active, or simply connect with neighbors, our
calendar is full of opportunities to engage and enjoy.

I am also excited to let you know of our plans for a proposed brand new Inter/Multi-Generational
Community Center. The Center will be a centralized, year-round indoor facility that will serve residents
of all ages. This facility will provide flexible, accessible space for recreation, education, and community
programming and will serve as a welcoming, central hub promoting connection, wellness, and lifelong
engagement for children, teens, adults, and seniors alike. Thank you to all who shared their insight and
opinions to create this space. Your participation towards the needs of our community has been
invaluable.

As I prepare to conclude my time as Mayor, I want to take a moment to express my heartfelt thanks to the
people of White Plains. It has been the honor of a lifetime to serve this remarkable community, and I am
proud of the progress we've made together. One of the greatest joys of this role has been seeing the
ways in which our seasonal programs bring people together - across generations, cultures, and
neighborhoods. I encourage everyone to take part in this year's fall and winter events. Let's continue to
build a stronger, kinder, more connected White Plains.

With gratitude and excitement,




Thomas M. Roach, Mayor

Youth Bureau Before and After School Connection Programs
The Before School Program provides before school care on site at five elementary schools in
White Plains, beginning at 7:00am until the start of classroom instruction. The programs provide a
friendly, safe and enriching quality program complete with breakfast and activities to prepare
students for the school day.
The After School Program provides quality after school child care from school dismissal until 6pm
at all five elementary schools, Eastview 6th Grade Academy and Highlands Middle School.
Programs include: Snacks, Homework Help, Recreation, Art, S.T.E.A.M, Dance, Games, Nature,
Gardening and many other special activities.

                                    BEAUTIFICATION FOUNDATION
White Plains Beautification Foundation has enhanced our parks and other public areas with flowers, shrubs, trees,
street clocks, a carillon, fountains, a blooming basket, a flagpole, a gazebo and a summerhouse for 6 decades.
WPBF is an all-volunteer, not-for-profit organization supported by the city's Departments of Public Works and
Recreation & Parks, and engages with local nurseries to create and maintain more than 65 gardens and parklets. Its
Adopt-a-Park program allows individuals, businesses and other organizations to support the mission.
In the fall, WPBF plants thousands of daffodil bulbs around the city. Early bloomers, those daffodils burst forth
each spring, followed by tulips, cherry blossoms and ornamental fig blossoms. It keeps the city filled with cheerful
blossoming plants and shrubbery that bring joy and a park-like vibe to our urban setting.
WPBF welcomes volunteers and would love to hear from you. Send an email to info@wpbf.org to receive the
annual newsletter and announcements of upcoming activities. Visit the website at wpbf.org.

BALDWIN FARM                     PARK FACILITIES
This 18.5 acre Specialty Park is located in the Woodcrest Heights neighborhood on Hall Avenue.
Baldwin Farm is devoted to the community gardens, which along with 40 garden plats located at
the New York Hospital property off Bryant Avenue; provide residents who might not have their own
land an opportunity to garden.

BARK DOG PARK
This pet friendly park is located on the Main St. extension off of Brockway Pl. Opposite the rear of
Delfino Park. A new pet friendly site is now located in Battle Hill Park, west of the condominiums on
Battle Ave. Dogs with permits are welcome to run around and exercise at our Dog Park. This park
features an area exclusively for small or young dogs. All dogs are required to have a current license
tag and vaccinations.

BATTLE HILL PARK
This 3.68 acre park is located to the west of the Battle Hill Condominiums on Battle Avenue and
McKinley Avenue. Open field space and the outdoor basketball court is the perfect location for
individuals to engage in casual athletic activities. Dog park area available with permit. Newly
renovated playground.

BATTLE HILL POLLINATOR HABITAT
This 1 .47-acre located on Route 119 between Robertson Ave. and Chatterton Ave. Opened to the
public in October 2023. This passive open area is the first of its kind in Westchester County. The
naturalized area boasts an impressive array of native plants, most notably: redbud, constellation
dogwood, paper birch, serviceberry, oakleaf hydrangea, highbush blueberry, blue milkweed,
butterfly weed, coneflower, New York aster and many more. It also includes a porous pathway
made from 100% organic material: decomposed granite, limestone and crushed stone, three
birdhouses and five benches for peaceful friendly space that keeps our pollinator community
diverse, healthy and beautiful for all to appreciate.
BATTLE OF WHITE PLAINS PARK
This 1.31 acre park is located at the corner of Whitney Street and Battle Avenue at a high point
overlooking the City. This park is the historic site of the Battle of White Plains, designed and built in
1976 as part of the National Bicentennial Projects. Interpretive historical signage is located
throughout the park. Picnic tables have wheelchair access and accessible wide pathways.

BRYANT-MAMARONECK PARK
This 8.93 acre circular trail way park is located on the southeast corner of Bryant and Mamaroneck
Avenue. This half mile rugged looped trail is perfect for hiking and exploring nature. Seasonal Story
walks are presented in the park, for more info see p.7. You may enter and exit the park from
Mamaroneck Avenue. Meter parking is available at
the City owned Shapham Place lot across Mamaroneck Avenue.
CHATTERTON
This 40 acre park is located at the corner of Chatterton Avenue and Harmon Street. The modern
fenced play areas are well shaded and provide a variety of playground activities. The playground is
accessible and disability friendly.

DELFINO PARK                      PARK FACILITIES
This 17.89 acre park is our largest multiuse park and is located at 110 Lake Street and is the location
of Ebersole Ice rink. This beautiful flagship park offers many exiting recreational options. Ball fields,
Basketball Court, Playgrounds, Picnic area, Platform Tennis and Pickleball Courts are available at
this location. Spring, Summer and Fall bring softball to Delfino Park. The park also features a fun
accessible pay area for children of all ages and abilities. In November of 2024, the We-Go-Swing®
opened to the public, an inclusive swing for all with no need to transfer from a mobility device. The
park also features a fun accessible play area for children of all ages and abilities. Bathrooms are
accessible.
DRUSS PARK
 This 1.99 acre park is located at the intersect of South Lexington and Prospect Avenue opposite St.
Bernard’s Church. The Park naturally separated into three terraces divided by gentle slopes. Each
terrace has a flat plateau perfect for leisure opportunities. There are two accessible playgrounds
for 2-5 yrs old and 6-12 yrs olds. The picnic tables and pathways are accessible.
GARDELLA PARK
This 7.5 acre park is located in the Ferris Avenue and Church Street/Park Avenue neighborhoods
with access from Ferris Avenue and from the parking lot of Church Street School. Gardella pool is a
great way for residents to splash away the summer. This park is the location of our competitive
outdoor 18 & under and open basketball leagues and the Gardella Festival held annually the second
Sunday of August. Permits are available for group outings. The playground and pool are accessible.
GILLIE PARK
This 11.24 acre park is located at the corner of Mamaroneck Avenue and Gedney Way. Registration
for all recreation programs is available in the Administration building which is also the location of
all staff offices. Additionally, this busy park is known best for its outstanding tennis courts, softball
fields and home to the White Plains Little League and the “Field of Dreams”. Pre-school programs
are abundant in the Gillie Recreation room.
JACK HARRINGTON GREENWAY
This 19 acre linear park train portion begins at Gedney Way. The trail between Gedney Way and
Ridgeway is 0.8 miles long. The trail from Ridgeway to the Scarsdale border is 0.5 miles long. The
Greenway from Gedney Way south to the Scarsdale line is accessible from Gedney Way and
Ridgeway. The Greenway provides a long path for individuals to enjoy walking in a natural setting in
a protected area.
KITTRELL PARK
This 1.67 acre specialty park is located in Fisher Hill on a multi-level site at the intersection of Fisher
Avenue and Irving Place. Kittrell pool renovated in 2015 features zero depth entry, lap lanes and
expanded swim area. Also the park features basketball courts, outdoor fitness center and
accessible playground. The park features outdoor fitness center and accessible playground.
LIBERTY PARK
This waterfront 2 acre park is located on Lake St. on the White Plains Harrison border. Residents
can enjoy passive recreation, picnicking, hiking, kayaking and fishing in the 22 acre Silver Lake. A
peaceful environment tucked away from the busy down town Liberty Park lends itself to quiet,
contemplative relaxation. Named in honor of city residents who lost their lives on September 11,
2001, this is the site of our permanent monument.

                                PARK FACILITIES
MATTISON PARK
This .13 acre park is located in Fisher Hill at the intersection of Quinby Ave. and Lynton Pl. This
park serves as a gathering place for children who enjoy the play areas, court and grassed areas for
unstructured play.
MITCHELL PLACE TOT LOT
This .13 acre playground is in the Core Area/Central Business District across from the Community
Center on Mitchell Place. This fenced playground was designed especially for young children ages
Pre K - 5 years. The park also features a shade canopy over its’ two picnic tables.
JACOB PURDY PARK
This 1.37 acre park is located on Park Ave. south of its intersection with Kirby Terrance.
It is situated at the top of Purdy Hill on a part of the original 132 acre farm purchased by Jacob
Purdy in 1730. In winter, the site commands an excellent view of White Plains and its surroundings
to the south and west. The park was developed by the City of White Plains to accommodate the
relocation of the historic Jacob Purdy House. The house is used for historical programming and as
headquarters of the White Plains Historical Society.
RENAISSANCE PLAZA
This urban passive. 36 acre park is located at the key downtown intersection of Mamaroneck
Avenue and Main Street. The Park features 3 large fountains with decorative lighting, music and
water features, and seating on benches, surrounded by shade trees, ground cover and shrubs.
Additionally, this landmark location is the site of numerous special events, concerts and
ceremonies, most notably our New Year’s Eve Celebration.
RIDGEWAY NATURE TRAIL
This 0.6 mile long trail and the surrounding open space area abut Ridgeway School on Ridgeway.
On the north it is bordered by a small dead end street, Seeger Drive. The Park is utilized for
educational purposes. It also serves as a community resource by providing a 0.5 walking trail to
the Scarsdale border. There are several wooden bridges over wet areas.

TIBBITS PARK
This 4.52 acre park composed of seven large median traffic islands is located along North Broadway,
running from approximately Lake Street south to Armory Place. Colorful annual and perennial
plantings installed under the auspices of the White Plains Beautification Foundation grace this park.
In spring this park blooms with cherry trees. Passive area with benches are scattered throughout
the park. Two display fountains, three commemorative statues, a gazebo and decorative clock also
add to the beauty of these islands. The accessible entrance is located on Main St.

TURNURE PARK
This 4.05 acre park is located on a sloping site diagonally opposite Eastview School bordered by
Main St, Canfield Ave. and Lake St. A picture perfect park in a densely populated area of the city.
A accessible themed playground for both toddler and school aged children is available in this
park. Turnure Park is a spectacle of beauty in the spring with over 100 Japanese Cherry Trees in
bloom. This park is also the location of our free outdoor BOCCE courts, open year round.
Pathways ae accessible.
