Parks and Recreation
From beautiful parks and nature trails to classes and activities for all ages, Teaneck has it all! The Recreation Department provides wonderful services such as seasonal classes, childcare, after-school programs, events in the park, camps, and accessible transportation for residents. The Teaneck Public Library, lead by Shinae Hyun, hosts a wealth of resources including literature, computers, community events, a supply of museum passes and hot-spots, and clubs and programs for all ages. Between these amazing departments and the many organizations in town that run youth sports or put together events like the Teaneck Film Festival, there is no shortage of things to do!
