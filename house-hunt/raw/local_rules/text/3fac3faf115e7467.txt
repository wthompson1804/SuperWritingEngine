Renewing Dog Licenses
Renew online
You may now renew your dog license online!  Dog licenses must be renewed every year.  You will need the tag number and a method of payment (Credit card or banking information).  There is a $5.00 late fee for all renewals received after the expiration date.
In order to be eligible, the following requirements must be met and already on record with the City Clerk's Office:
The most recent rabies information (within 3 years) must be on file with the City Clerk's office.
If applicable, the spayed/neutered status
must not have changed s
ince the last renewal.
If either of these items need to be updated, you can forward the documentation via email to the
City Clerk's Office
.  Once this information is processed, you will be notified via email that you can now begin the online renewal process.
