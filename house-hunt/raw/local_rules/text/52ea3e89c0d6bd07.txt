Snow Removal
The Department of Harrison Public Works is responsible for snow removal and to better serve our community when snow and ice hit, we provide information and updates.
It is very important to the Harrison Public Works Department to ensure that our streets are safe when they are covered with snow and ice.
Snow Procedures
Snow Procedures (PDF)
PUBLIC SERVICE NOTICE
Effective immediately, there will be No Parking while road is snow covered on both sides of Angelo Cifelli Dr, between Frank E Rodgers Blvd South and South 3rd St.
This parking restriction is to help keep the streets plowed and accessible to emergency vehicles, as well as pedestrian and vehicular traffic. The Harrison Police Department will be strictly enforcing this parking restriction.  Vehicles in violation will be ticketed and towed at the owner’s expense there are no exceptions.  Residents must wait until snow is completely removed from curb to curb before parking.
Also, with the Winter Season upon us, snow removal information can be found on the town website or by calling the DPW at 973 268 2468.
Please check often as cars will be towed if not moved for snow removal.
