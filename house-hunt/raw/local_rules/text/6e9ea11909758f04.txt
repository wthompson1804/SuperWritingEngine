Cleaning
We
sweep the streets
, empty
litter baskets
, tow
derelict vehicles
, and remove
graffiti
to keep NYC clean, safe, and healthy. But we can’t do it alone.
New Yorkers need to do their part. Follow Alternate Side Parking (ASP) rules, keep trash off the street, and clean up after pets. Be our partner in keeping our City clean and report the
misuse of public litter baskets
,
abandoned vehicles
,
illegal dumping
, and when business and property owners
fail to keep their sidewalks clean
.
