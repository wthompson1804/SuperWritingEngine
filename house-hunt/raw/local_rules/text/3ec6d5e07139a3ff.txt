Get A Permit or License
Welcome to the Plainfield Center for permits, licenses, and applications. These vary and can range from getting a pet license to a construction permit, and everything in between! As a business person, community representative, homeowner or commercial property owner seeking a permit, license, and/or application for your project or event, you can find applications and contact information to get started here. Information pertaining to the permit, license, and/or application process and payment must be obtained directly from the respective City Division as indicated below
