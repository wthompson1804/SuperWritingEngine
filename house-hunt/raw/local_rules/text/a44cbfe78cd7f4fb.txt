Street Sweeping
Guidelines & Regulations
The following streets have alternate side street sweeping regulations at this time:
Edgewater Place: There is no parking on the east side of the street on Mondays from 10 a.m. - noon.
Edgewater Place: There is no parking on the west side of the street on Friday from 10 a.m. - noon.
Undercliff
    Avenue Between Dempsey Avenue and Garden Place: There is no parking on
    the east side of the street on Tuesdays from 10 a.m. - noon.
Undercliff
    Avenue Between Dempsey Avenue and Garden Place: There is no parking on
    the west side of the street on Thursdays from 10 a.m. - noon.
The street sweeper is out on a daily basis (weather permitting).
Consequences
Any vehicles parking on either side of the street between the above times could be issued a summons.
