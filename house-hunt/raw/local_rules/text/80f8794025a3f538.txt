2026 SWIM LESSONS
Red Cross certified swim lessons for ages 3-18 in Freedom Lake. With an emphasis on drowning prevention and water safety, courses are available for a variety of ages and skill levels.
Registration opens online to LaGrange residents on Tuesday, 3/10/26, 9am, and to eligible non-residents* on Tuesday, 3/24/26, 9am, at www.lagrangeparksandrec.com.
Session 1:  7/6/26 – 7/17/26
Preschool 11 am or 12 pm
Level 1 11 am, 12pm or 1 pm
Level 2  11 am or 1 pm
Level 3 12pm or 1 pm
Level 4,5, and 6 1pm
Session 2:  7/20/26-7/31/26
Preschool 11 am or 12 pm
Level 1 11 am or 1 pm
Level 2  12 pm or 1 pm
Level 3 11 am, 12 pm or 1 pm
Level 4,5, and 6 1 pm
Session 3:  8/3/26-8/14/26
Preschool 11 am
Level 1 11 am or  12pm
Level 2  12 pm
Level 3 12pm or 1 pm
Level 4,5, and 6 11 am or 1 pm
With Freedom Lake Season Pass: $75 / $90 Non-Resident
No Freedom Lake Season Pass: $105 / $120 Non-Resident
Fees INCLUDES lake usage during weekdays of lesson session. No weekends.
Please note:
If it is raining, classes will still meet under the pavilion. If there is a storm, classes may be cancelled with no makeup or refund.
You may only register for one session at a time due to level testing.
Pre-swim evaluation may be required.
Refunds for all programs and rentals will be processed upon
written
request in accordance with the following policy unless stated otherwise:
No refunds within 6 weeks of start date due to, but not limited to, inclement weather, acts of God, flooding, dismissal for behavior issues or non-usage.
50% refund up to 6 weeks prior to start date.
NO REFUNDS FOR LAGRANGE DAY CAMP
unless we are able to fill the spot.
A doctor note may be required prior to end of program for illness/accident.
Eligible non-residents include residents from the following border TOWNS:
Beekman, East Fishkill, Pleasant Valley, Poughkeepsie, Union Vale, and Wappinger. Proof of residency through a property tax or utility bill attached to residence is required.
