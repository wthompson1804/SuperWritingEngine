Ammann Park
Section Menu
Ammann Park
is located near the intersection of Teaneck Road and Fort Lee Road. It is 5.3 Acres and features playground equipment, a 60' Softball field, two tennis courts, walking/bike paths, a basketball court (seasonally illuminated for evening use), and sprinklers.
Organized leagues require permits for court or field use, permits have preference.
