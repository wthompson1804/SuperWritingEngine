On-Street Handicap Parking
Residents must have a valid handicap placard if they wish to be considered for use of On-Street handicap parking spaces.  The following questions and answers should satisfy any queries about obtaining a placard or a handicap parking space on a street within the city of Bridgeport.
How do I apply for a handicap placard?
Please visit the
CTDMV
website and carefully read the qualifications, restrictions and costs of applying for a handicap placard.* There is also additional information about replacing a placard as well.
*Please Note:
There are separate applications for temporary and permanent handicap spaces.
CT DMV
CT DMV site to request an a accessibility parking parmit

How do I apply for residential On-Street Handicap space in Bridgeport?
Handicap Parking Sign-Installation Request
Please fill out our application above.
Print and fill out with a pen
Drop off, email, or mail application to:
City of Bridgeport
Department of Social Services
999 Broad Street
Bridgeport, CT 06604
Attn: ADA Coordinator
Please submit a current driver's license and the original handicap placard for the vehicle being used.
If you have a driveway on the property, a notarized statement from the property owner/landlord is required stating any restrictions preventing you from parking in the driveway.
Looking to remove a handicap parking sign? See form below:
Handicap Parking Sign-Removal Request
After the application for on-street parking is received by the City, staff will look at the address and determine the following:
Your application will be forward to the Bridgeport Police Department who in turn will notify the City’s Traffic Engineer Department. They will conduct an on-site visit to determine if it is appropriate and location.
The Traffic Engineer Department will make a recommendation to the Bridgeport Police Commissioner.
Once the Police Commissioners make a decision you will be sent a letter informing you whether your request has been approved or denied.
Please note:
The process can take 3-5 months.
Will this spot be exclusive to my vehicle?
No.  As stated on the application, the space will be available to any vehicles that have a valid handicap placard on a first come first serve bases.
Please note:
City Ordinance 10.30.10
, anyone with a valid handicap parking permit can utilize the space that you have requested. The space cannot be reserved for any specific individual.
