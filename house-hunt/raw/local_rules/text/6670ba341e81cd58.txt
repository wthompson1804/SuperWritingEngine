Animal Control Services
Clifton Health Department is dedicated to delivering exceptional public health services to enhance the wellbeing of residents, including animals. Our Animal Control Officers (ACOs) are compassionate animal welfare professionals who are all thoroughly trained and certified by the NJ Department of Health and Senior Services and the Police Training Commission in consultation with the New Jersey Animal Control Officers Association. ACOs are authorized to enforce local and regional laws concerning animal care and treatment and are committed to educating the public about animal control safety.
ACOs may issue warnings or citations for violations of city ordinances, such as failing to license a dog (§203-2), allowing a dog to run at large (§203-10), or failing to pick up and dispose of dog feces in a sanitary manner (§203-11B1, 2). They also enforce ordinances regarding the unsupervised feeding of free-roaming cats (§203-70) and feeding of wild waterfowl (§203-48).
Officers respond to calls about stray dogs, stray cats, and injured animals (both domestic and wildlife). When necessary, they capture animals and attempt to locate their owners. If no owner is found, the animals are brought to the Clifton Animal Shelter at 2 Dog Pound Road behind City Hall. At the shelter, animals are cared for and held for seven days before becoming eligible for adoption. The primary goal is to reunite pets with their owners.
Clifton Health Department offers free rabies clinics for dogs twice a year (spring and fall) and rabies clinics for cats at a nominal fee of $5 once per year.
Clifton Animal Shelter accepts stray domestic animals from Clifton, Wallington, and Little Falls. If you reside in these areas and can no longer care for your pet, please contact Friends of the Shelter at 973-470-5936 to schedule an appointment to surrender your pet.
ACOs also respond to reports of deceased domestic animals (dogs and cats) on public or private property to scan for microchips, check for collars with licenses or IDs, and match lost pet reports to provide closure to owners.
ACOs do not handle deceased wildlife. For wildlife found on roadways, please contact the Department of Public Works at 973-470-2237 during regular business hours. For after-hours, holiday, or weekend assistance, call 973-470-5911.
Wildlife calls are limited to animals found inside living quarters (not detached garages or attics). Please note that nuisance wildlife trapping (e.g., groundhogs, skunks, or raccoons) is no longer conducted, as the New Jersey Department of Fish and Wildlife restricts release areas.
Clifton Animal Shelter does not trap outdoor cats. If you are interested in Trap-Neuter-Return (TNR) programs, contact Friends of the Shelter at 973-470-5936 for information.
As of February 1, 2019, all reports of animal abuse or cruelty must be investigated by the police department. If you suspect an animal is being abused, please contact the police dispatch at 973-470-5911.
If you believe your pet has ingested a poisonous substance, contact your veterinarian or the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center at 888-426-4435.
