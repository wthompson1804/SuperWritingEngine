Between December 1st and April 15th, alternate side-of-the-street parking rules are in effect. Parking regulations are indicated on street signs. A helpful "Parking Rule of Thumb" to use is that if there is no sign authorizing parking, then parking overnight on a city street is not permitted between December 1st and April 15th.
