Parking
When you are out for a night on the town, browsing our local stores, or just stopping in to run a few errands, knowing where to park can make or break your trip. Not only are there spots up and down almost every street, with no parking meters, but there are also numerous parking lots scattered around the area. On the map you can check out where free public lots are downtown, so your visit can be stress-free from start to finish.
