Cheescote Park
Location:
1/4 mile off of Willow Grove Road in the unincorporated area of the Town of Haverstraw.  The park entrance is the first left after the Palisades Interstate Parkway underpass.
Facilities Available:
317 acres of parkland with picnic areas, hiking trails, camping grounds and a 6 acre lake for fishing.  A permit is needed for over-night camping.
Park Dates:
The park is open to the general public daily from April 1 thru October 31st, weather permitting, from  9:00AM till dusk.
Future Attractions:
The Town is in the process of getting grants to install trailheads and make hiking and paving improvements.
Thiells Mt. Ivy Ballfields
Location:
Thiells Mt. Ivy Ballfields, Thiells, NY
Location:
From the Palisades Parkway North:
Exit 13 - stay left at exit ramp and make left turn onto Thiells/Mt.Ivy Rd.
Travel about 1 1/2 miles and ball fields will be on your right side.
From the Palisades South:
Exit 13 - At light turn left onto 202 East
Make a Left at next light onto Thiells/Mt. Ivy Road
Travel about 1 1/2 miles and ball fields will be on your right side.
Facilities Available:
Two baseball and/or softball fields with sixty foot bases and backstops. Fields could be used for various sport teams.   Any questions please contact Town Hall at 845-429-2200.
Also available is a nature walking or jogging path for the publics pleasure along the Mineceongo creek.   The Town of Haverstraw also stocks the Mineceongo creek each year with Trout usually late March or early   Fall for anyone interested in fishing.
Park Dates:
The ball fields are open to the general public daily all year round from dawn till dusk.
