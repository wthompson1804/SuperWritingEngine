Snow Emergency Routes
When 3 or more inches of snow have fallen in the City of Mount Vernon, 'Snow Emergency Routes' become active and vehicles parked on the routes have to be moved, otherwise they risk being ticketed and then towed/impounded (tow fees are seperate from the ticket fees).
