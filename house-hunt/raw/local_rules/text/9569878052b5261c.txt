ONLINE REGISTRATION!
The Town of Hempstead Department of Parks & Recreation has made it quicker and more convenient for residents to sign up for town-run sports and other recreation programs throughout the year. Among the many new and exciting features is an option to create a family profile that allows parents and guardians to easily view and track their children’s upcoming events. You can also maintain calendars, manage reservations and payments, and much more.
