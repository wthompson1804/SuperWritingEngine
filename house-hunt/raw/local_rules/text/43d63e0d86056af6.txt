Parking: Elmont
Parking: Elmont
Parking permits are required for the Elmont parking district. These permits were created in order to prevent non-residents from parking in certain restricted areas. There is
no fee
for a one-year parking permit sticker or for guest passes for visitors. For family gatherings, parking waivers can be obtained from the
Office of the Town Board.
The license period is January 1 to December 31.
Please download, complete, and sign the Elmont Special Parking District Application. Your vehicle registration or a copy must accompany this application. If your current address is not listed on the vehicle registration, you will also be required to present proof of residency, such as a utility bill, tax bill, deed to the house, or a notarized lease agreement. Your registration will be returned with your parking sticker. Please enclose a stamped, self-addressed return envelope. Please mail completed, signed applications along with a copy of registration, and a self-addressed, stamped return envelope to the Hempstead Town Clerk.
If you have any questions regarding Elmont Parking District permits,
email us
or call
516-812-3513
.
