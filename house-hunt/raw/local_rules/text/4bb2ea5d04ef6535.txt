This form is for use in applying to the City of Orange Township for zoning permit approval and/or zoning information on a particular
property. All applications must be accompanied by a certiﬁed check in the amount of $100.00, payable to the “City of Orange Township”,
as well as a completed application checklist (attached). Applicants who are tenants should obtain zoning, building and/or land use board
approvals prior to entering into a lease. Submission of an application to the City does not constitute approval of any permit; and, the City reserves
the right to accept or reject any application as determined appropriate in accordance with applicable law.
