Legal Advertisement – NOTICE OF PENDING BOND ORDINANCE AND SUMMARY
Posted November 13, 2026
PUBLISHED: NOVEMBER 13 | ARCHIVE DATE: NOVEMBER 24 –
The bond ordinance, the summary terms of which are included herein, was introduced and passed upon first reading at a meeting of the governing body of the Town of West New York, in the County of Hudson, State of New Jersey, on November 12, 2026. It will be further considered for final passage, after public hearing thereon, at a meeting of the governing body to be held at the Municipal Building, 428 60th Street, West New York, New Jersey on November 23, 2026 at 6:30 p.m. Instructions regarding attendance and participation in the meeting will be posted on the Town’s website: http://www.westnewyorknj.org/ . During the week prior to and up to and including the date of such meeting copies of the full ordinance will be available at no cost and during regular business hours, at the Clerk’s office for the members of the general public who shall request the same. The summary of the terms of such bond ordinance follows:
Title: ORDINANCE #25/26
AN ORDINANCE OF THE TOWN OF WEST NEW YORK, IN THE COUNTY OF HUDSON, NEW JERSEY, PROVIDING FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF A RECREATION CENTER AND APPROPRIATING $35,300,000 THEREFOR, AND PROVIDING FOR THE ISSUANCE OF $12,067,446 IN BONDS OR NOTES OF THE TOWN TO FINANCE THE SAME
Purpose(s): The construction of a recreation center on Madison Street in the Town, and including all work and materials necessary therefor or incidental thereto.
Appropriation: $35,300,000
Bonds/Notes Authorized: $12,067,446
Grants (if any) Appropriated: $8,732,554 in grants are appropriated in the bond ordinance, plus $9,500,000 from purchase agreements with developers and $5,000,000 from a Shared Service Agreement with West New York Board of Education are also appropriated in the bond ordinance.
Section 20 Costs: $2,300,000
Useful Life: 40 years
AYES – Commissioners Arroyo, Barrera, Diaz, Parkinson, and Mayor Sires
NAYS – None
ABSENT
–
None
Dated: November 12, 2026
Adelinny Plaza, RMC
Town Clerk
