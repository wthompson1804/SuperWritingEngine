Transportation, Mobility and Parking
The City of Norwalk's Transportation, Mobility, and Parking (TMP) Department is committed to making it easier, safer, and more convenient for people to move throughout our city. Through transportation planning, engineering, traffic operations, and parking management, we work every day to improve mobility for everyone—whether you're walking, biking, driving, taking transit, or visiting one of Norwalk's many destinations. By combining community input with data-driven solutions, TMP is building a more connected, accessible, and sustainable transportation network that supports economic growth and enhances quality of life for all.
