Parking
Overview
The City of New Rochelle recognizes that parking is a key component to both the revitalization and growth of New Rochelle. An efficiently run parking operation is necessary to maintaining a vibrant retail community. To that end, the City has positioned the parking operation as a part of the Department of Development.
Goal
It is the goal of the City's parking operation to meet the needs of those individuals seeking commuter parking for mass transit, parking for employees of local businesses as well as their customers, and for those that have residential parking needs.
Operations
The parking operation of the City of New Rochelle oversees the operation of parking garages, parking lots, and on-street (metered) parking.  The enforcement of parking regulations is handled by the
Police Department
.
