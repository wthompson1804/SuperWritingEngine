Parades/Processions/Special Events Permit
PERMIT APPLICATION FOR PARADES/PROCESSIONS/SPECIAL EVENTS
The following must be completed at the time of application:
Parades must start after 5 P.M. during the summer time.
It is your responsibility to obtain a copy of the event ordinance from the town clerk.   You and your organization are responsible for compliance with all of its requirements.
If parade/procession involves other towns, please attach Event Permit approval from each town.
A check made payable to Town of West New York for $ 250.00, if Organization is
NOT
a Non-Profit.
To be exempt from fee, a Non-profit Organization must present their 501c (3) status.
The Event Rules and Regulations form
MUST
be notarized.
In the event you might need to hire an officer for security purposes you need to Jobs4blue at 1-877-425-8330
A copy of your insurance documentation
MUST
be submitted at time of application.  Liability Coverage is $1,000,000.00 per occurrence.  Certificate holder should include:

877-425-8330
