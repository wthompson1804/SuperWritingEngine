Resident Parking Sticker Online Request
Only Available Through City Squared Portal
Resident parking stickers may be requested online through the
City Squared On-Line Permitting Program (Link).
Alternatively, requests may continue to be submitted in person, by mail, or by drop-box.
Please review the
Resident Parking Sticker Online Request Instructions (PDF)
. This document will guide you through creating a City Squared profile, submitting your online sticker request, and tracking the status of your request.
Also, review the
Resident Sticker Common Eligibility scenarios (PDF)
to ensure you have all the required documentation readily available when submitting your request.
You will get an email confirmation your request was submitted. Then, the Tax Collector's Office will review your submission and issue your requested resident parking sticker(s) by USPS mail at their earliest convenience. Please track your submission through your City Squared dashboard.
The City of Milford is not responsible for lost, stolen, or destroyed stickers. There will be a $25 per sticker replacement fee. Replacement stickers shall be provided free of charge to any eligible person who transfers their registration to a new vehicle or has a rear windshield or rear bumper replaced and provides satisfactory proof thereof.
