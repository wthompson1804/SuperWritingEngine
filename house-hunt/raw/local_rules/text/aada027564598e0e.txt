Recreation
The Larchmont Recreation Department facilitates Village-wide events, issues permits for use of athletic facilities and manages the Flint Park Summer Day Camp. Together with the Department of Public Works, the Recreation Department also oversees the Village's nine parks and the conservation area located at the Larchmont reservoir. Active and passive recreation facilities include: ballfields, tennis courts, paddle courts, basketball courts, walking paths, nature trails, and playgrounds.
Permits are required for the use of the
athletic fields
and
tennis/paddle courts
, as well as group use of certain
parks
.
The Recreation Department is also instrumental in the production of many notable events. Please visit the
Events
page for more information on our annual community events.
