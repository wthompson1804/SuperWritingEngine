Monthly contract parking is available at the following parking facilities:
Lower Church Street Deck ($200 per month)
Morris Street Deck ($200 per month)
New Street Deck ($200 per month)
Gateway Deck ($210 per month)
NBPAC Deck ($210 per month)
Paterson Street Deck ($210 per month)
Plum Street Deck ($210 per month)
Wellness Plaza Deck ($210 per month)
If you are an individual
-in order to secure parking at one of these locations, you may log into
https://njnbpa.mgzephire.com/parkerportal/
and then visit our office at 106 Somerset Street, Sixth Floor to pick up your credentials. (make sure you bring your license and vehicle registration). Your access card will be activated after pickup and may take a few hours to be active.
If you are a merchant (business)
-in order to secure parking at one of these locations, you may log into
https://njnbpa.mgzephire.com/groupportal/
and then visit our office at 106 Somerset Street, Sixth Floor to pick up your credentials. (make sure you bring your license and vehicle registration). Your access card will be activated after pickup and may take a few hours to be active.
If you are an NBPAC parker
In order to secure parking at NBPAC location, you may log into
https://www.tocite.net/newbrunswickparkingauthority/portal
and click on
Purchase NBPAC permits
and then click on
Register
. Once you have entered the required information, you will receive an email to confirm your email. Login in back again and complete the required information and proceed to purchase the permit.
We currently offer a $10 reduction in parking for New Brunswick residents. Two forms of proof of residency must be shown when signing up in the office (this includes a utility bill with your name on it, lease, driver's license, and/or deed).
Vehicle storage is prohibited. If you plan on leaving your car for longer than 48 hours, please call Parking Services at 732-667-8100.
*If you're a Middlesex County College student, make sure to bring a copy of your schedule.
For deck addresses, go to our
Parking Locator page
.
If you have any questions, you can call 732-545-3118 and we'll gladly answer your questions.
