Enforcement of Alternate-Side Parking (Street Sweeping) Regulations Resumes on Monday March 16.
Posted
March 11, 2026
Mayor Albio Sires and the Board of Commissioners would like to alert all residents that, beginning Monday, March 16, 2026, the West New York Parking Services Utility will resume enforcement of alternate-side parking (street sweeping) regulations.
Residents are kindly asked to move their vehicles during posted sweeping hours to avoid parking tickets.
Please note: All parking enforcement regulations remain in effect throughout the Town.
