Candlewood Town Park Rates, Rules & Boat Stickers
With 11 acres and 200 yards of beach-front, put your toes in the sand, swim, relax and enjoy Candlewood Town Park. The Park offers benches, picnic tables, a concession stand, restrooms, outdoor shower, playgrounds and a boating dock (separate entrance). Candlewood Town Park opens for season Saturday, May 23rd
Hours: Monday-Friday: 11:00AM- 7:00pm, Saturday-Sunday: 10:00AM-7:00PM
No Alcohol Allowed in park.  Gas grills only. Proper swim attire required to enter water
Address: 36 Hayestown Road
Town Park Rules
Boat Ramp access for Danbury Residents ONLY, must show proof of residency.  Season and Daily passes must be purchased at Boat Ramp or Danbury Park & Rec Office.
