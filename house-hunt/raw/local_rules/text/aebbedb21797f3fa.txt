2026 FREEDOM LAKE ROCKERS SWIM TEAM
For boys and girls Ages 6-18:
Beginners to advanced swimmers. (10 year old and under swimmers must be able to swim 25 yards (1 lap in deep water lanes with stamina). Team practices are weekdays from 4:00-4:30pm (10 & under) and 4:30-5:30pm (11 & older), starting Friday, June 26, 2026, through August 7, 2026. There are Home and Away Meets with other local Town Recreation Teams. New team members are always welcome.
Registration opens online on Tuesday, 3/10/26, 9am, for residents and Tuesday, 3/24/26, 9am, for non-residents through the Town of
LaGrange Parks And Recreation Website
until the first meet.  $70 per Town Resident Swimmer OR $90 per Non-Town Resident Swimmer. There is a $10 discount for Freedom Lake Pass Holders Swimmer.
Fees do NOT cover lake usage before/after program.

MEET THE COACHES
HEAD  COACH:  Tom Bubel
Tom grew up in the Hudson Valley and graduated from Marist college, where he swam. For several years he swam on a local Masters Team. He resides in Hyde Park with his wife, Jill and 2 daughters. He was a lifeguard, waterfront director, and Red Cross WSI swim instructor for several years here at Freedom Lake. He currently is the in-house ARC trainer for Waterfront Lifeguarding, CPR, First Aid and other courses. He started the Freedom Lake Rockers Swim Team in 1989 and has been the coach here at Freedom for over 35 years. He is the director of the Hudson Valley Lake League of Recreation Parks Swim Teams. He has also coached Empire State Swim League and US Swimming Teams (Millbrook & Hudson Valley Dolphins). He is currently the Head Coach of Our Lady of Lourdes High School Girl’s Swim Team. He is passionate about swimming and helping others to learn and enjoy the benefits of swimming both competitively and for fitness.
ASSISTANT COACH: Anya Fridman
This is Anya’s first year working at Freedom Lake. Anya is an ARC Swim Instructor at Freedom Lake this year. She swam for the Lords High School swim team and was the captain of the girl’s team. This Fall she will be attending Sacred Heart University.
ASSISTANT COACH:  Annie Sobolewski
Annie grew up in the Hudson Valley and was a member of the Freedom Lake Swim Team.  Annie is currently a Lifeguard and ARC Swim Instructor here at Freedom Lake. This summer will be her 7th season working at Freedom Lake.

CHAMPIONSHIPS
Championships will be 8/7/27 at Freedom Lake this year with 4 teams in the league participating. All families are asked to donate two (2)  items for our food sale at the meet. There will be a sign up on line families will be notified by an email invitation when it is ready to go (around 7/25/27). Not all swimmers qualify for Champs but it would be nice if all team members come to support those swimming. Also there are many duties the day of Champs that both team members and adults can help with. Please plan to come and help out the team that day. SWIM MEET DIRECTIONS
Beekman:
Take Route 55 East towards Pawling. Go Straight at Noxon Road light and at the next light make a right onto Beekman Road. Make the first left onto Beekman Recreation Road. Follow this road until you see baseball fields on your right and the lake. Make a right into the entrance of the park and park in the parking lot closest to the lake. (For GPS:  29 Recreation Road, Poughquag, NY)
Pawling:
Take Route 55 towards Pawling (about 10 miles). After the intersection of Route 55 and 292, make first left onto Old Route 55.  Follow Old Route 55 for about 2.5 miles. Make  left onto Lakeside Drive and continue about 1 mile. You will pass Edward Murrow Park on left, but continue until you come to Lakeside Park on left. Park in parking lot to the left. (For GPS:  2 Lakeside Drive Pawling, NY)
Red Wing Park:
Take Route 82 South toward Hopewell Junction.  Red Wing Park is approximately 2½ Miles past the intersection of Route 82 and the Taconic State Parkway.  Take a right onto Old Farm Road just past the Foot Specialist sign; (there is a Dr. office on the corner in a low building). There is a small sign that says Red Wing Park.  Follow Old Farm Road around a curve, as you start down the hill there is a chain link fence enclosure on the right at the entrance to the Park.  Turn right at end of fence and follow the road into the park and tell the person at the booth you are with the Freedom Park Swim Team and are here for the meet.
Stanford:
Take Route 82 North at the Troop K State Police Barracks (a left turn about 1 mile East of the intersection of Route 44 and Taconic State Parkway.)  Follow Route 82 North to Stanford about 7 miles.  As you come into Stanford there is a white church on your right, then Route 82 makes a slight turn to the right and there is a road that veers off to the left at a yellow building (Deli) on the left and a Big tree in front of you to the left side of this road.  Make this left turn onto Church Lane. Follow this road until it T’s with Creamery Road and make a left onto Creamery Road.  Stanford Town Park parking lot is on the right about 0.2 mile. (For GPS:  40 Creamery Road, Stanfordville, NY)
