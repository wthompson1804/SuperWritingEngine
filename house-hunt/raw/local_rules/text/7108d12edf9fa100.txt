Danbury Alert
Danbury ALERT is the City of Danbury’s new mass notification system powered by the Everbridge platform. Sign-up to receive time-sensitive emergency and community alerts. You can select multiple delivery methods such as email, text, phone, the Everbridge mobile app, and more! Messages will be sent to wherever you specify on a priority basis to ensure you receive the vital information you need.

Arts & Culture
Danbury is home to a vibrant and growing arts and cultural community that reflects the diversity, creativity, and history of our city. From public art and galleries to theaters, museums, music venues, cultural organizations, and community events, there are countless opportunities to explore, create, and connect.

Whether you're looking for your next family outing, searching for local artists, or hoping to become involved in the arts, Danbury offers something for everyone.
