Welcome
Welcome to the official website of the Borough of Bogota, New Jersey. We’re excited to introduce our new online home—designed to better serve our residents, businesses, and visitors with easier access to information, services, and community updates.
​
Here you’ll find the latest news, municipal resources, department information, meeting notices, and important announcements, all in one convenient place. Our goal is to make it simple to stay informed, get things done, and stay connected to everything happening in Bogota.
As a proud community with a rich history and a strong sense of hometown spirit, Bogota continues to grow while honoring the values that make our borough a great place to live, work, and visit. We invite you to explore the site and check back often as we continue to add new features and content.
Thank you for being part of the Bogota community.

A Message from Mayor Daniele Fede
It is an honor to serve as your Mayor and lead our fine and giving community. I will continue our efforts to make strides to make a healthy, safe and vibrant place for our residents. I welcome all residents and community groups to join in these efforts. Feel free to call or email me with ideas, projects and concerns as you have done in my past two years of my Mayorship. Your contributions make our community an inclusive and diverse place to live.
I hope that you can navigate this website quickly, and if there are any services that you require or think should be included on the website, press the contact button below and I will make sure that it is addressed.
​
The Mayor and Council will continue to use and explore grant opportunities, improve our aging infrastructure which is a constant challenge, and find ways to complete the open-space projects. Veterans’ Park improvements are now complete. The recreation center is now complete and planning is in place for the tennis, pickleball and basketball courts in Olsen Park. The athletic complex is a testament to our commitment to the wellbeing of our community. Community walking groups are enjoying the track.
We will continue our sustainable practices and I am proud of our Tree City USA designation for over 20 years as well as our Sustainable Jersey bronze certification. I am very proud of the services that the Police Department provides for our public safety, the Department of Public Works and all the services that it provides, including keeping our roads clear, removing leaves and taking care of Borough buildings. We cherish our first responders who train, volunteer and help with our commitment to public safety.
I look forward to hearing from any volunteers that wish to serve on boards, committees or special projects. Kindly provide a copy of your resume or letter of interest to the Clerk, and we will see if there are any openings to fit your interests.
I hope to see you at one of the Mayor’s Wellness Campaign events and at our community events. Bogota has made history in electing its first female mayor, and I have worked tirelessly to govern and support as many events as I can. I appreciate the support that has been given to me by the community.
