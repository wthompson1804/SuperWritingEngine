Road Repair / Snow Removal
County Roads
The following roads in the Borough Of Edgewater are county roads:
River Road
Gorge Road
Snow removal on these streets is performed by the Borough Of Edgewater. Road repairs are completed by the county Department of Public Works (DPW).
Complaints
If there are any problems you can contact the Edgewater Police Department at 201-943-2200, Edgewater DPW at 201-943-1700, ext. 5000 or the Bergen County DPW through the County Police at 201-646-2700 with your complaint.
Highways
Snow removal on the Highways is performed by the State Department Of Transportation (DOT) with assistance by the Borough Of Edgewater. Road repair is completed by the State DOT.
Complaints
Any problems or questions please contact the Edgewater Police Department at 201-943-2200, Edgewater DPW at 201-943-1700, ext. 5000 or the State DOT at 973-770-5000.
Streets
All other streets in the Borough are maintained by the Edgewater Department of Public works. Any problems or questions please feel free to call the DPW at 201-943-1700, ext. 5000.
Emergencies
All emergencies should be reported to the Edgewater Police Department at 201-943-2200.
