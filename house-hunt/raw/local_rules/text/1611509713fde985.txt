Parking: Oceanside Terrace
Parking permits are required for the Oceanside Terrace Parking District. Parking permits may be issued only to residents who live near Mount Sinai South Nassau Hospital in Oceanside on designated streets as specified below. The requirement for parking permits was created in order to prevent non-residents from parking in certain restricted areas. There is
no fee
for a one-year parking permit sticker or four (4) guest passes for visitors. For family gatherings, parking waivers can be obtained from the
Office of the Town Board.
The license period is January 1 to December 31. The permit requirements took effect January 2022.
Residents may download an
Oceanside Terrace Parking District
Application. Please complete and sign the application, and send it along with a copy of your vehicle registration. If your current address is not listed on the vehicle registration, you will also be required to present proof of residency, such as a utility bill, tax bill, deed to the house, or a notarized lease agreement. Please mail completed, signed applications along with vehicle registration. If you have any questions regarding the
Oceanside Terrace Parking District
permits,
email us
or call
516-812-3513
