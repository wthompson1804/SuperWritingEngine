Valet Parking
A valet parking service conducted
entirely
on private property does not require a permit.
Use of City of White Plains streets, sidewalks, or rights-of-way for the operation of valet parking services is restricted by Chapter 4-27 of the White Plains Municipal Code and requires a
Valet Operating Permit
issued by the Department of Parking. Valet Operating Permits will only be issued where on-street loading areas have been approved by the Common Council under recommendation of the Transportation Commission.
See the Department of Parking's
Valet Rules and Regulations (PDF)
for complete details and fees.
Valet Operating Permit Applications (PDF)
should be submitted to the Department of Parking.
Please contact the Department of Parking at (914) 422-1232 for more information.
