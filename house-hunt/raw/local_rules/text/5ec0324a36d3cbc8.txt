Dog Day Festival
Welcome to the very first Trumbull Dog Day Festival presented by Choice Pet and in support of Trumbull Animal Group.
Join us on Saturday, October 3, 2026, at Unity Park for an unforgettable day celebrating our favorite four-legged family members!
Designed for dogs and the people who love them, this inaugural community event brings together local pet vendors, live demonstrations, fun giveaways, and plenty of tail-wagging activities. Whether your pup is dressing up for our parade, sampling gourmet canine treats, or just sniffing out new friends at the park, there is something for every dog and human to enjoy.
All dogs must be kept on a leash.
Saturday, October 3, 2026
11:00 AM - 2:00 PM
Unity Park Pavilion
White Plains Road Trumbull, CT 06611
Event is Rain or Shine.
Free Admission and Parking.
Check back soon for the schedule of the day's events.
Admission is free but donations are encouraged.
100% of proceeds go to the Trumbull Animal Group.
