Supervisor's Message -
Welcome to the Town of Haverstraw web site!
The Town Board and I hope that the    information provided to you will assist you and inform you about Town   Government and    the services that we render and also discover the opportunities and   amenities that the Town of Haverstraw    offers to our residents.
Our website will keep you updated on the many recreational   and cultural    activities that have been scheduled.
I am proud to be the Supervisor of the Town of Haverstraw. Haverstraw is   rich    in its history, its culture and its people. It is a great place to live   and learn. Our future is bright    and there are many things happening in Haverstraw which include the   County Waterfront Park, development    of a town park in the unincorporated area, youth and senior activities   and much more.
Please feel free to contact me at the Haverstraw Town Hall with any   questions or comments you may have. 845-429-2200 or fax 845-429-4701.
Howard T. Phillips Jr.
Supervisor

Licenses are issued Monday through Friday 
			between 9:00 a.m. and 4:30 p.m. -
Appointment necessary.
The Town Clerk's office issues various permit 
			and Licenses in the following areas.
Marriage License
Fee $40.00. Cash, check or money order
Both parties must be over the age of 18 
				years
If any of the parties is under the age of 
				18 he/she must bring both parents
Both applicants must come in person to 
				apply
Each applicant must bring two forms of ID
1) Original Birth Certificate (no Copies)
WITH PARENTS NAMES ON BIRTH CERTIFICATE
AND
2) One form of valid picture ID (Driver's 
			License, Non-Driver's License, Passport or Military ID)
Any previously married person must provide a 
			certified copy of divorce papers or death certificate for all 
			previous marriages, whichever is applicable.
There is a 24 hour waiting period before a 
			license can be used and the license is good for 60 days. If license 
			is not used within the time period it will expire, and the license 
			has to then be mailed or brought back to the Town Clerk's Office.
Any 
			documents not in English must be translated into English by a 
			Certified Translator
Death & Birth Certificate
If a person has died or was born at home in 
			the Town of Haverstraw, a certificate should be on file in this 
			office.
Copies can be obtained by the next of kin for 
			$10.00 a copy along with a picture ID and proof of next of kin (ex: 
			Birth Certificate and/or Death Certificate)
Dog Licenses
All dogs in the Town of Haverstraw over 
				the age of 4 months old must be licensed
If the dog is spayed / neutered the fee 
				is $5.00 (you must show proof)
If the dog is unspayed / unneutered the 
				fee is $13.00
Proof of the rabies vaccination must be 
				provided with expiration date
License must be renewed once a year
If dog license tag is lost you can apply 
				for a replacement tag in the amount $3.00
Conservation Licenses
You must have the required hunter 
				education courses certificate (not required for fishing)
You must provide a NYS drivers license or 
				non driver ID
If you are under the age of 18, a parent 
				must come with you
The fee varies depending on the type of 
				license
Parking Permits
You can obtain a parking permit for the 
				Mt-Ivy Park & Ride at 202 & Rt 45 at a fee of:
-
Resident living in the Town of Haverstraw $50.00 
			per year
-
Non-Resident $60.00 per year
-
5 consecutive days for $5.00
Landscaping and Snow Plowing Licenses
Application fee is $50.00 per license 
				cash, check or money order accecpted
Form must be completely filled out along 
				with all attached information requested
Carters Permit & License
Application fee is $50.00 and License fee 
				is $100.00 per truck cash, check or money order accepted
Application must be completely filled out 
				along with all attached information requested
After permit is received, it is then 
				given to the Sanitation Commission for approval
If approved by the Sanitation Commission 
				you will be notified
