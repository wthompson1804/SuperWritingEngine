Park City Communities (Housing Authority)
The Park city communities (Housing Authority): Responsible for managing nearly 2,600 public housing units in Bridgeport.  Park City Communities consists of five members; four are appointed by the Mayor and confirmed by the City Council while one member is elected by the Park City Communities residents.
Previously known as the Bridgeport Housing Authority
Five members appointed by Mayor
Any vacancy shall be filled for remainder of term
Meet first Monday of the month
Members serve until a successor is appointed and has qualified.
