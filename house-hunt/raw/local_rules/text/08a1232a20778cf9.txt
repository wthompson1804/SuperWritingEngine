City Clerk
The City Clerk houses vital records for those who were born, died or obtained their marriage license within the city limits of White Plains. To request such records, go to the Clerk's
Vital Records
page
The City Clerk also issues a number of Special Licenses, including an Auction and Auctioneer's License, Window Cleaning License, Solicitation / Peddler License, Secondhand Dealer License. For a full list of Special Licenses and to access the applications for these licenses see
Special Licenses
.
