Temporary Tent  Permit
WHEN REQUIRED:
A Temporary Tent Permit (Building Permit) is required for the erection of a structure, enclosure or shelter, with or without side walls or drops, constructed of fabric or pliable material supported in any manner except by air or the contents it protects, per Connecticut State Building Code.
WHO CAN APPLY:
Pursuant to section
29-263
of the Connecticut General Statutes, application for a permit shall be made by the owner of the property or by an authorized agent.
TEMPORAY TENT PERMIT APPLICATION PROCEDURES:
Visit the
Park City Portal
and
create an account
to
apply for a Tent Permit
. For all Tent Permits you will also need to apply for a
Zoning Plan
. All documents must be submitted on PDF format.
PROVIDE THE FOLLOWING:
Required documents to be submitted with tent permit application:
Copy of Workers’ Compensation Insurance Certificate for tent erection company or if Sole Proprietor a signed and notarized
affidavit
is required.
If agent – Provide a letter, on company letterhead, signed by the licensed holder - indicating the agents name as the authorized representative, type of permit and address of the work. Agent must provide photo identification and a copy of the contractor's license.
Site plan or plot plan showing locations of existing structures and tent on the property
Certificate of Flame Retardant for tent membrane
Copies of all applicable pre-approvals from Federal Government, State of Connecticut and or City of Bridgeport
If tents are erected on City property, a letter authorization from the property owner is required.
SPECIAL NOTE:
Per
City Ordinance 15.08.020
- No Permits can be issued by the Building Department if there are delinquent Property Tax and/or WPCA Sewer User Fees.
