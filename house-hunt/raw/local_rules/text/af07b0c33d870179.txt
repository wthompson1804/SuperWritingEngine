RESIDENT PERMIT RULES AND REGULATIONS
When applying for a resident permit, you must have the following:
A valid NJ driver's license. **(Note)
Permanent vehicle registration.
Insurance card for each vehicle.
All three (3) must have the address of the street for which you are applying. The Division of Parking will not accept any other forms of ID.
**(Note) If you change your address for DMV online, you must PRINT the letter with the change of address, Rahway Division of Parking WILL NOT accept it on your phone.
TWO HOUR, FOUR HOUR AND 24-HOUR RESIDENT PERMITS ARE ONLY FOR THE STREET YOU LIVE ON. THIS DOES NOT ALLOW YOU TO PARK ON ANY OTHER TWO-HOUR TIME LIMIT STREET.
If a resident disposes of their car or moves off the street, the permit must be removed & returned to the Division of Parking.
PERMITS ARE NON-TRANSFERABLE
.
Resident permits must be placed on the *lower rear driver's side window. The resident permit does not guarantee parking space but will exempt you from the two-hour parking limit on your street. The permit does not exempt the residents from any other parking rules.
* Motorcycles should place the permit on the left front fork of the tire.
This permit does not exempt your vehicle from the street sweeper, meters, snow removal or any other parking rule. This permit is only for your street.
The penalty for violation of this ordinance shall be a fine of not exceeding $500.00 or imprisonment for a period of thirty days, and revocation of the parking permit privilege.
