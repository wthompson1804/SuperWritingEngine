Film Production Inquiries
White Plains is Television & Film Friendly!
We are in the ZONE here in White Plains!
For crews looking for alternatives to filming in NYC, White Plains is within the boundaries set by NYC based worker’s unions, guilds, and organizations for film production.
We welcome ALL productions
- from feature film to photography to student filmed projects.  Our streamlined process addresses all aspects of your shoot, and all city departments apply their expertise ensuring that the actual filming goes smoothly.
White Plains'  building inventory features many different styles of architecture including, Brutalist buildings that play as Washington, D.C, Glass high rises, and an eclectic Main Street. These are complimented by a mix of housing stock comprised of various architectural styles such as Victorian, Tudor, Colonial, Dutch and Modern homes.  The city boasts traditional suburban tree lined neighborhoods and urban multifamily buildings.  Additional film friendly locations in White Plains include two translations, City Hall, hotels, shopping malls, interesting schools, parks, ice rink, library, and roads that can read as rural.  The city can accommodate large vehicle footprints as well.
