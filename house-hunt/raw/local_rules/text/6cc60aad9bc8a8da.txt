Overview
At 722 acres, Tarrywile Park is the largest municipally owned park in Connecticut and one of Danbury’s most important natural, recreational, and historic resources. The park offers more than 21 miles of multi-use trails winding through forests, open fields and meadows, with ponds, a lake, picnic areas, scenic overlooks, and diverse wildlife habitat throughout the landscape. Visitors can enjoy hiking, biking, equestrian trail use, nature observation, photography, and other forms of passive recreation throughout the year.
A Park with a Rich History
Tarrywile’s history reaches back to the agricultural estates that once shaped this landscape. In 1985, Danbury residents voted in a historic referendum to protect more than 535 acres from development. Additional land acquisitions have expanded the park to approximately 722 acres.
Among Tarrywile’s most recognizable historic landmarks is Hearthstone Castle, a distinctive late-19th-century stone structure set high within the park landscape. Although the Castle cannot be entered, it remains an important part of Tarrywile’s heritage and Danbury’s history.
Today, the nine-member volunteer Tarrywile Park Authority manages this remarkable public resource for the City of Danbury, balancing public access and recreation with the protection of the park’s natural landscapes, historic resources, and environmental character for future generations.
Community Programs and Environmental Initiatives
The Park Authority continues to expand opportunities for community engagement, forest stewardship, and workforce development. Recent programs and projects include:
Invasive Vines Cutting Day
, bringing volunteers together to remove invasive woody vines and protect trees and native vegetation, and enhance the beauty of the park landscape.
Arbor Day Celebrations
, promoting forest health and safety, tree planting, environmental awareness, and community involvement.
Danbury’s first Monarch Butterfly Migration Runway
, created by repurposing a former cattle feeding trough into habitat supporting monarch butterflies and other pollinators.
OVERwinter Initiative
, focused on seasonal land management and improving habitat for overwintering wildlife.
Project Last Seed: A Water Chestnut Seed Bank Reduction Initiative
, addressing invasive water chestnut and its persistent seed bank in Parks Pond.
Tree Ambassador Program
, providing hands-on opportunities to learn about tree care, forest health, horticulture, and landscape management, with proposed scholarships in Horticulture and Landscape Design supporting future education and career pathways.
Volunteers Make a Difference
Hundreds of volunteers have participated in Tarrywile programs, events, and improvement projects. Community members, students, businesses, civic organizations, and youth groups have contributed their time and skills. Their involvement extends well beyond routine maintenance, helping to protect natural resources, and strengthen connections between Tarrywile and the community.
Investing in Tarrywile
Grants and public investments help the Tarrywile Park Authority protect and improve the park for future generations. Funding from local, state, and federal programs supports improvements to park facilities and infrastructure, recreational opportunities, historic resources, landscapes, and natural areas. These investments include support from the Connecticut Department of Energy and Environmental Protection (DEEP) Urban Green an Community Garden Grant Program, the Connecticut Recreational Trails Program Grant, the American Rescue Plan Act (ARPA), and the Local Capital Improvement Program (LoCIP).
Friends of Tarrywile Park, Inc. is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization that raises private and public funds to support the preservation, enhancement, and long-term stewardship of Tarrywile Park.
Tarrywile Mansion
The historic Tarrywile Mansion provides a distinctive setting for weddings, showers, celebrations, business meetings, retreats, and community gatherings. Surrounded by gardens, an orchard, and mature specimen trees, the Mansion combines the character of a historic property with the natural beauty of the park.
Tarrywile Park is where nature, history, recreation, and community come together, offering something to discover in every season.
