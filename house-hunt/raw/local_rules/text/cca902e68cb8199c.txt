NEW COURTRESERVE USERS:
Follow these steps to create your CourtReserve account and sign up for a membership with the Village of Larchmont.
Go to our CourtReserve site:
Larchmont Courts through CourtReserve
Click the “Create Account” button.
Create a username and password for your account.
Enter your personal information. *Please only fill out the Primary Household member’s information. Once your account has been created and approved, you can add your Family Members.*
Once your account is created, you’ll be redirected to the Memberships section.
Choose the membership type that best suits you and/or your family. *We now offer combined permits!* This option lets you purchase a discounted permit for both the Fall/Winter Paddle season and the upcoming Spring/Summer Tennis & Pickleball season.
Follow the on-screen instructions to complete your membership sign-up and payment. *Please allow up to two business days for your account to be approved.*
- Resident Memberships: Email recreation@larchmontny.gov with the subject line PROOF OF RES with supporting documents.
Once your membership is approved, you’ll receive an email and can start booking courts!
EXISTING USERS:
If you’ve used CourtReserve before, you don’t need to create a new account. Instead:

Larchmont Courts through CourtReserve

Sign in with your existing CourtReserve username and password.
When prompted, select “Join Organization” (or “Join Club”) to connect to the Village of Larchmont.
Review and update your personal information. *Please only fill out the Primary Household member’s information. Once your account has been approved, you can add your Family Members.*

Follow the on-screen instructions to complete your membership sign-up and payment. *Please allow up to two business days for your account to be approved.*

Paddle Permits:
The Village has three (3) paddle tennis courts at Flint Park. A Paddle Permit is required to play on these courts and can be purchased online only.
Court Rules
Enforcement: Those failing to produce a permit upon request will be escorted off the facilities.
Permits: Play is limited to permit holders and their guests. Guests are subject to all tennis rules.
Court Assignments: Assignments are on a first-come, first-served basis, unless reservations have been made.
Playing Time: Limited to one hour when others are waiting to play. Time starts when any player arrives on the court.
Proper Etiquette: Is to be observed at all times.
Proper Attire: Shirts and non-scuff tennis shoes must be worn.
No loud, threatening or obscene language.
No violent, obstructive or threatening behavior.
No disruption of play on other courts for ball retrieval or otherwise.
Ball throwing machines are only allowed in the afternoons during off-peak times. In addition, to reduce disturbances to others, there must be a free court in between players with ball throwing machines and other players using the courts.
Snow Clearing and Ice Removal: Are strictly prohibited except by Village-appointed workers.
Items with wheels are not allowed on the court, including skateboards, roller-blades, and bicycles.
Clinics and Instruction: At certain times some courts may be used by instructional clinics, teams and other groups. Only those clinics and lessons that are approved by the Village of Larchmont are allowed on the court.
Play is prohibited when surface is wet.
