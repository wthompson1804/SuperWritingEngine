Street Sweeping
When you see our municipal street sweepers moving through your neighborhood, they are doing much more than just keeping our community looking its best. Street sweeping is a vital municipal service that actively protects our local environment, provides storm water protection, flood prevention and safeguards our infrastructure, saving tax payer dollars.
How you can help:
Our sweepers can only do their job if they can reach the curb line. During our active seasonal sweeping period from
April 1st to December 31st
,
please comply with all
Alternate Side Parking
regulations and move your vehicles on scheduled sweeping days.
Your cooperation ensures our trucks have total access to the debris—keeping our City safe, clean, and sustainable.
Even a single vehicle parked on the restricted side of the street will completely disrupt the sweepers path and prevent effective cleaning. Vehicles must remain clear from the restricted side for the entire duration of the posted hours. Please do not move your vehicle back onto the street as soon as you see the sweepers pass. Sweepers may plan to return for a second time once they run through their route. Vehicles that remain parked on the restricted side during posted hours may be subject to fines and/or towing.
Suspended Alternate Side of Street Parking
Alternate side of street parking restrictions are suspended for the period of January 1 through March 31 on those streets and portion of streets indicated as being subject to winter suspension of alternate side of street sweeping parking regulations in
Schedule XIII
of the
City Code
.
