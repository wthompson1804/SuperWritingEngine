Snow Removal Operations
Plowing usually occurs when there is a snowfall in excess of 1 inches on the road. There is no parking on any village roadways between December 1st through March 31st whenever 2 or more inches of snow falls, until 24 hours after the snow has stopped falling or when plowing has been fully completed, whichever comes first. Vehicle owners may face a fine of up to $100. Residents' cooperation is urged during snow emergencies to ensure roadways are fully cleared.
