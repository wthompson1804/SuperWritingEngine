Parking Permits
Commuter Parking Permits are available to village residents only. A copy of a valid driver license and vehicle registration must be provided and match the license plate and Mineola address listed on the application. The permit must be affixed to the vehicle as required. Commuter permit holders may only park in designated commuter parking permit areas. For a list of these areas, please contact the Village Clerk's Department.
