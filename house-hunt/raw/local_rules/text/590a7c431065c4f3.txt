Park Pass
Park Pass
Overview:
With the New Rochelle Park Pass, individuals enjoy free parking at
Hudson Park
,
Five Islands Park
, and
Davenport Park
.
The senior Park Passport is also required for those seniors who wish to participate in the activities at the
Hugh A. Doyle Senior Center
.
Glen Island Park is a Westchester County park. For more information, please call the County Department of Parks, Recreation & Conservation at 914-231-4575.
Requirements:
To obtain a Park Pass, photo identification is required, along with two permanent items that include name and address, i.e. driver’s license, vehicle registration, utility bill, tax statement, lease, or rent receipt.
Park Pass Fees:
1 YEAR Resident
Park Pass (ages 16 to 59) - $40.00
***For an additional $20 you may add one adult family member who resides in the same household. Proof of address required.***
3 YEAR
Resident Park Pass (ages 16 to 59) - $100.00
***For an additional $50 you may add one adult family member who resides in the same household. Proof of address is required.***
Non-Resident Park Pass(ages 16 to 59) - $90.00
***For an additional $40 you may add one adult family member who resides in the same household. Proof of address required.***
Resident Senior (ages 60 and up) valid for 5 years - $50.00
Non-Resident Senior Park Pass (ages 60 and up) - $75.00
10% Discount For All Veterans
Daily Parking Fees (Non-Park Pass Holders):
Resident Rate: $5.00
Non-Resident Rate: $10.00
To Obtain a Park Pass:
In Person:
Passes may be purchased at the Parks and Recreation Office in City Hall, Monday through Friday, 8:30 am - 4:30 p.m.
Each person applying for a pass must show proof of residence and age, and have their photo taken.
By Email:
Email ID, proof of residence and a clear photo of yourself to
info@newrochelleny.gov
Once sent, you will receive an email to make your payment online. Passes will be mailed out once payment has been received.
Renewals
Pass renewals can be done in person, mail or email:
Parks and Recreation Department
515 North Ave.
New Rochelle, NY 10801
or by emailing info@newrochelleny.gov
