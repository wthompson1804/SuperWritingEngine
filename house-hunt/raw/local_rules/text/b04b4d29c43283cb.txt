Abendroth Park Master Plan
The Village of Port Chester has initiated a comprehensive master planning process to establish a long-term vision for the future of Abendroth Park—one of our community's most valued recreational assets. Recognizing the park's importance to current residents and its growing role within the Village, the Board of Trustees retained Barton & Loguidice to conduct a thorough evaluation of the park, including existing
conditions, environmental characteristics, accessibility, recreation needs, and opportunities for future investment. The planning effort has emphasized extensive public participation, incorporating online surveys, interactive mapping, community events, and Board of Trustees presentations to ensure the master plan reflects the priorities and aspirations of the people who use the park every day.
Village staff, elected officials, recreation personnel, and residents have worked collaboratively throughout this process to identify opportunities that will strengthen Abendroth Park for generations to come. Rather than focusing on isolated projects, the master plan establishes a flexible, phased framework that will guide future decision-making, engineering, grant funding, and capital investment as resources become available. This planning effort is particularly significant as Abendroth Park sits immediately adjacent to the future Westchester Crossing development, where nearly 1,000 new residents are anticipated to live. As Port Chester continues to grow, Abendroth Park is expected to become an increasingly important destination for recreation, community gatherings, wellness, and neighborhood connectivity, making thoughtful, long-term planning essential to ensuring the park continues to serve both today's residents and tomorrow's community.
