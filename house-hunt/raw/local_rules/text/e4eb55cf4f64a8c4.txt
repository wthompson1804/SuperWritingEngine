PUBLIC SAFETY NOTICE
Posted June 17, 2026
IMPORTANT TRAFFIC & PARKING INFORMATION TO PROTECT YOU
Mayor Albio Sires and the Board of Commissioners want to make residents aware of temporary parking and sanitation schedule changes to help everyone plan ahead.
Street Sweeper service will be suspended from Monday, June 29 through Monday, July 6.
Metered parking will be suspended from Friday, July 3 through Sunday, July 5.
THERE WILL BE NO VISITOR PASSES PERMITTED AT THAT TIME AND ONLY RESIDENTS WILL BE ALLOWED TO PARK IN TOWN
Please plan accordingly and allow extra travel time as increased traffic and temporary road impacts may occur during this period. Thank you for your cooperation as we work together to keep our community safe and moving.
Stay informed and enjoy the holiday weekend responsibly.
