Payment Instructions – Step by Step
There are several convenient ways to pay for parking:
Pay with Quarters
The kiosk accepts
quarters only
for cash payments. Insert the appropriate amount based on how long you plan to park.
Pay by Credit Card
Credit card payments are accepted at the kiosk. You can tap to pay or insert your card’s chip and follow the on-screen instructions.
Scan the QR Code at the Kiosk
Scan the
QR code displayed on the new parking kiosk
with your mobile device. You will be directed to the appropriate parking zone, where you can select the amount of parking time needed and proceed with payment.
Pay Using the ParkMobile App
Download the ParkMobile App on your mobile device and enter the appropriate
Zone Number
for the parking lot:
Lot A – Zone #61409
Lot B – Zone #61410
Lot D – Zone #61412
Lot G – Zone #61411
Once you enter the correct
Zone Number
, you do not need to remember or enter a parking space number. Enter your
vehicle’s license plate number
and follow the prompts to complete your payment.
Important:
Please make sure you select the correct parking zone and enter your license plate information accurately before completing your transaction.
Non-Profit Organizations Parking Permit: $25.00 per month
Local Business Parking Permit: $50.00 per month
New Senior Parking Permit Decal (65+): $10.00 (renewals are exempt)
Seniors will need to bring their driver's license and car registration. For first-time applicants, there is an initial $10 fee; upon renewal, there is no charge unless the placard is lost. These permits allow parking only in municipal lots, not on the street.

Handicap Parking Request
The Town of Dover provides a process for residents who require a designated handicap parking space.
Applicants must follow the steps outlined below to ensure their request is properly reviewed.
How to Apply:
Submit a written request to the Administration Office
Requests may be submitted to:
Email:
esantiago@dover.nj.us
In Person:
Administration Office-37 N Sussex Street, Dover, NJ
Required Documentation:
A letter from the applicant explaining the reason for the request
A letter from the owner of the house if you are a renter
A copy of the applicant’s valid handicap placard or license plate registration
Supporting medical paperwork from your physician
Review Process:
The Administration Office will review all submitted materials
The application will be evaluated based on eligibility and the specific street location requested
Applicants will be notified once a determination has been made regarding approval or denial
