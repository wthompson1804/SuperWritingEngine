Demolition Permits
WHEN REQUIRED:
A Demolition Permit is required to demolish any building, structure, or part thereof, per the Connecticut State Building Code. Demolition permits are valid for 30 days from date of issue.
WHO CAN APPLY:
Any Connecticut Licensed Demolition Contractor, or authorized agent of the license holder may apply for a demolition permit.
DEMOLITION PERMIT APPLICATION PROCEDURES:
Visit the
Park City Portal
and
create an account
to
apply for a Demolition Permit
. All documents must be submitted in PDF format.
PROVIDE THE FOLLOWING:
Required Documents to be submitted for a Demolition Permit
SPECIAL NOTES:
Upon demolition completion, provide a letter from the dump location and dump tickets to the Building Official to close out the demolition permit.
