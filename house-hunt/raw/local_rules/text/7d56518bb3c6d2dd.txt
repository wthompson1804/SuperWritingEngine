Parking: Bellerose Terrace
Parking: Bellerose Terrace Restricted Parking
Parking permits are required for the Bellerose Terrace Restricted Parking District. Parking permits may be issued only to local residents on designated streets as specified below. The parking permit requirement was created to prevent non-residents from parking in certain restricted areas. There is
no fee
for a one-year parking permit sticker or four guest passes for visitors. Parking waivers can be obtained from the Office of the Town Board for family gatherings. The license period is January 1 to December 31. The permit requirements took effect in July 2022.
Residents may download a
Bellerose Terrace Restricted Parking District
Application. Please complete and sign the application, and send it along with a copy of your vehicle registration. If your current address is not listed on the vehicle registration, you will also be required to present proof of residency, such as a utility bill, tax bill, deed to the house, or a notarized lease agreement. Please mail the completed, signed application along with vehicle registration. If you have any questions regarding the
Bellerose Terrace Restricted Parking District
permits,
email us
or call
516-812-3513
.
