Tickets and Towing
Juror Parking for the Middlesex County Court House is located in the Morris Street Deck. Parking is free at these facility when you have your parking deck ticket validated by the Jury Office.
Jurors with oversize vehicles should utilize the Morris Street Deck located on New Street near the George Street intersection.
Please remember to bring your parking deck ticket with you for validation. When exiting the facility you may drive to the exit and the machines will be able to process the validation. If you have questions please contact the NBPA at (732)545-3118 prior to arrival.
Directions
From Livingston Avenue
- Proceed North on Livingston Ave. to New St. (Millenium Bank on left). Turn right onto New St. Proceed 1/2 blocks and enter the Morris St. Deck on the right.
From Rt. 18 North
- Take the New St. exit. On New St. go through 2 traffic lights. After 2nd light enter the Morris St. Deck on the left.
From Rt. 18 South
- Take the New St. exit. On New St. go through 2 traffic lights. After 2nd light enter the Morris St. Deck on the left.
From Rt. 27 North into New Brunswick
- Rt. 27 becomes French St. Make right turn on George Street follow to make a right onto New Street and quick left into the Morris Street Deck.
From Rt. 27 South
- Travel over the bridge from Highland Park into New Brunswick onto route 18 South. Make right turn onto New Street. On New St. go through 2 traffic lights. After 2nd light enter the Morris St. Deck on the left.
From Easton Avenue
- Travel South on Easton Ave. into New Brunswick to end of street. Turn left onto Albany St. and make right onto George Street. Follow to New Street to make a right onto New Street and quick left into the Morris Street Deck
