Licenses & Permits
View the licenses, permits, and certificates the Town Clerk's Office issues to residents of the Town of Hempstead. You may download and print a form or obtain a form at the Town Clerk's Office during normal business hours. For further information, please
email us
or call 516-812-3025.

Auction Sales

Auctioneer License
Find information for the Auctioneer License.
Bingo License
Guidelines for the issuance of Bingo licenses are mandated by the New York State Racing and Wagering Board.
Block Party
There is no fee to obtain a block party permit and this type of activity is not covered by town ordinance. The permit specifically allows for the closing of a street for a specific amount of time.
Dog License
Guidelines for the issuance of Dog licenses are mandated by New York State.
Games of Chance
The guidelines for the issuance of a Games of Chance license are mandated by the New York State Racing and Wagering Board.
Going Out of Business Sale
Guidelines for the issuance of Going Out of Business Sales licenses are mandated by New York State.
Hunting & Fishing License
NYS hunting and fishing permits are available at Town Hall.
Limousine or Livery Owner
The issuance of medallions is to regulate passenger-carrying vehicles and to protect the public from unlicensed and uninsured operators.
Limousine / Taxicab Livery Driver
The issuance of Limousine and Taxi Driver Licenses is to regulate passenger-carrying vehicles and to protect the public from unlicensed and uninsured operators.
Parade Permit
There is no fee for a parade permit in the Town of Hempstead, however, Town Board approval is required.
Parking: Baldwin
Baldwin Special District Parking Permits are required for Baldwin Resident-Only parking spaces in the Baldwin Special Parking District.
Parking: Bellerose Terrace
Parking: East Meadow
Parking permits are required for the East Meadow Special Parking District.
Parking: Elmont
Parking permits are required for the Elmont parking district.
Parking: Oceanside Terrace
Parking: Oceanside LIRR
Parking permits are required for the Oceanside parking district.
Pawn Broker
NYS Law requires all Pawn Brokers to be licensed by the local government.
Peddling & Soliciting Permit
The licensing of peddlers and peddler vehicles offers the public a measure of protection from unlicensed and unscrupulous vendors.
Private Carter
Learn more about the private carter license.
Resident Commuter Parking Permit
Resident Commuter Parking Permits are required for all Hempstead Town-maintained parking spaces in the Baldwin, Bellmore, Merrick, Seaford, and Wantagh LIRR commuter lots.
Second Hand Dealer
The issuance of a second hand dealer license was created at the request of the Nassau County Police Department to aid in the investigation and recovery of stolen property.
Special Event Permit
Find more information about the special event permit.
Taxicab Owner
The issuance of medallions is to regulate passenger-carrying vehicles and to protect the public from unlicensed and uninsured operators.
Tow Car Driver
The regulation of car tow drivers is designed to protect the public from unlicensed and uninsured operators.
Tow Car Owner
The regulation of tow car owners is designed to protect the public from unlicensed and uninsured operators.
