Animal Control
Overview
The
Norwalk Department of Police Services
oversees the city's Animal Control Program. The city employs a dog warden, assistance dog warden, and a kennel keeper, who handle the majority of calls for animal complaints throughout the city, as well as maintain the operation of the city's dog pound on Smith Street.
The Animal Control Division enforces local laws pertaining to the keeping of dogs, cats, and other animals.
For emergencies after hours, please call the
Police Department
at (203) 854-3000.
