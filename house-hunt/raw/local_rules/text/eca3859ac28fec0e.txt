Snow Emergency Information
When a significant snowfall is expected, the City of Rahway Department of Public Works is asking everyone for their cooperation by following the guidelines listed below:
Try to stay indoors, don’t drive unless absolutely necessary.
If you live on a snow emergency street, you must remove your vehicle to provide access for emergency vehicles.
Please make every effort to utilize off-street parking whenever possible.
Do not park cars within 20 feet of an intersection. Parking too close to an intersection can restrict emergency vehicle access.
Do not blow or shovel snow into the street. Shovel driveway aprons, walkways, and sidewalks once plow trucks are completed with their work.
Do not double park vehicles.
Keep fire hydrants and catch basins in your neighborhood clear of leaves and snow.
Thank you for your cooperation and patience.
