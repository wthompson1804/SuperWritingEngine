Business districts are designed to balance the parking needs of the residents, merchants and visitors who patronize those establishments.  The Parking Authority has various means to accommodate the needs of each:
Residents
Permit parking regulations will become effective at all metered spaces once meter hours have expired. This measure helps create additional parking for residents and their visitors. As a bonus to our resident permit holders, the new ordinance regulating parking meters also allows for residents to park in a metered space with their permit, during certain times prior to meter hours expiring, at no cost. Be sure to check the signage for applicable times before leaving your vehicle.
Merchants
Monthly permits
are available to business owners and their employees to allow parking in the residential areas at a cost less than daily meter usage.  Leaving metered spaces available for your customers can boost your business.
Visitors
Metered parking spaces are now in effect
Monday through Saturday from 9am to 12 midnight
, unless posted otherwise.  Metered parking is designed to facilitate a short term parking solution for the constant flow of visitors and shoppers visiting our local businesses and restaurants. A 3-hour parking limit helps enhance the availability of parking for visitors, shoppers, and residents.  Metered parking rate: $0.25 for 20 minutes, the ParkVantage app provides a convenient method for extending time on your meter without having to physically return to your vehicle. If you need to stay longer, you may want to consider purchasing a daily permit to park in the residential areas and avoid the need for moving your vehicle.
Industrial Business District (Dell Avenue) Regulations:
The industrial business district, located on Dell Avenue between 40th and 49th Streets, has special parking needs due to the variety of businesses and high volume of heavy trucking in the area.  Regulations along Dell Avenue help balance the needs of the residents, business owners, their employees  vendors, visitors and commercial vehicles servicing those companies. Special regulations are in effect 24/7 and only affect the streets listed below between Tonnelle Avenue and Dell Avenues:
Dell Avenue
Parking is permitted in any of the available parking stalls along Dell Avenue with a business permit between 6am and 6pm.  Vehicles without a permit will have a
3 hour limit daily.
From 6pm to 6am, commercial vehicles are permitted to park in available stalls after purchasing the applicable commercial permit from the pay station located on 43rd Street off of Dell
Avenue.
