Welcome!
As the 32nd Mayor of the City of Danbury, I'd like to welcome you to the City of Danbury! Danbury is a thriving, diverse city in Western Connecticut that focuses on preserving and providing a superior quality of life for our residents and taxpayers through efficient and effective municipal services.
To learn more about Danbury, I encourage you to explore our website, download the myDanbury App in the App Store, or call my Office at 203-797-4511.
Mayor Roberto L. Alves
