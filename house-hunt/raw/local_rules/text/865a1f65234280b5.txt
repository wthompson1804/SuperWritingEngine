            Shadyside Resident Parking Permit Application
        Please fill out a separate application for each registered vehicle. You must attach a copy of your
        driver’s license and vehicle registration to this application in order to receive your parking
        decal. If your driver’s license does not show an Edgewater address, you must present proof of
        being a resident. Permits must be affixed to the interior driver’s side window with the permit
        number clearly visible. Please ensure your permit is always properly displayed. Parking permits
        are non-transferrable and misuse of a permit will result in revocation of your permit. Resident
        parking permits must be renewed every two years.
