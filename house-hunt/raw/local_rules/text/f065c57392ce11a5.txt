Animal Control
Effective January 1, 2026, Animal Control Solutions, LLC. will provide Animal Control Services to the City of Rahway. Residents can report lost animals or make general inquiries by calling
908-722-1271
.
General inquiries about lost pets.
Animal Cruelty concerns should be reported immediately to the Rahway Police Department.
Animal Control is not responsible for marine mammals or dead deer.
Animal Control does not pick up rodents (i.e., rats, mice, voles or moles).
Canadian geese will not be picked up unless in need of veterinary care.
Deer Carcass Removal
To report a dead deer in a local roadway, please call (732) 827-2200. You will be asked to provide the location/road name, mile marker, distinctive landmarks, etc. Please be as exact as possible for location.
State Roads: For state owned roads please call (609) 588-6211.
If the animal is on private property, the property owner is responsible for arranging to get the carcass to the side of road before removal.
