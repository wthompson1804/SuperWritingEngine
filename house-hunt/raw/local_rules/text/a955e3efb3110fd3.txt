Overnight Parking
Overnight parking in the Downtown area requires an Overnight Parking Permit.  Permits are available through the City Clerk's Office, Monday through Friday from 8:30 AM-4:15 PM.  Each permit is $120 for the year and is prorated by $10 each month.
Overnight parking begins at 8 PM and ends at 7 AM and is only available in designated lots (See Map Below).
There are also nine (9) 72-hour lots that allow overnight parking without a permit.
