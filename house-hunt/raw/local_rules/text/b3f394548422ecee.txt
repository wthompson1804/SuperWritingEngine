Snow Removal
PLEASE wait to do your final clean-up after the streets are cleared, which is typically six (6) to eight (8) hours after the storm is over.
Sidewalks
As per Township of Teaneck Code, Section 32-6, residents, property owners, and managers must remove snow and ice from public sidewalks in a timely manner. Commercial property owners/tenants must remove such within 12 hours after snowfall stops. Residential property owners/tenants have up to 24 hours after snowfall stops to remove snow and ice. In addition, walkways to the entrances of any properties should be cleared in a timely fashion to allow emergency personnel and mail delivery carriers safe and clear access to the premises.Residents with properties contiguous to a fire hydrant must remove all snow and ice within an area of three feet of such a fire hydrant.
Parking During a Snow Emergency
By
Ordinance Section 36-10.1
: Parking of any vehicle on any portion of a public street is prohibited whenever snow is falling and the accumulation is such that it covers the public streets in the Township and plowing is required,
except
where designated (see list below). The term “snow” includes sleet, hail and/or ice.
Before a storm begins, the main streets may be pre-treated with an environmentally safe solution that reduces the freezing temperature at which snow freezes.
During the storm, snow plows will concentrate on keeping two lanes clear on every street.
After a storm, plows will return to widen the streets curb line to curb line.
For additional off-street parking, residents are encouraged to use any of the municipal parking lots. Beginning six hours before snow fall is predicted to start and until 24 hours after snow fall has stopped, all hourly limits and meter fees in Township lots are waived.
Said parking prohibition shall remain in effect after the snow has ceased and until the streets in the Township have been plowed sufficiently and to the extent that parking on them will not interfere with the normal flow of traffic and the snow removal process.
Regulatory and warning signs in accordance with N.J.S.A. 39:4-198 shall be erected and maintained at all street entrances for motor vehicles to the Township. Signs for exempt streets shall be posted as required.
Any unoccupied vehicle parked or standing in violation of this section shall be deemed a nuisance and a menace to the safe and proper regulation of traffic, and any police officer may issue a summons and provide for the removal of such vehicle. The owner of such vehicle shall pay the prevailing cost of the removal and storage which may result from such removal before regaining possession of such vehicle.
Handicapped parking spaces shall be exempt from the provisions of this section.
Any person convicted of violating §
36-10.1
hereof shall be subject to a fine.
