How do I pay Fees in Accela ACA?
All fees assessed in Accela can be found in Chapter 151 of the Village of Port Chester Code.
Village of Port Chester Fee Schedule
Base Fees are assessed and charged at the time of permit application submittal.
Please be sure when completing your application online whether your application is based on Residential or Commercial terms as base fees are non-refundable.
Once your application is submitted successfully, Building Department staff will follow up electronically with an invoice for additional fees that may be charged such as Cost of Construction fees.
Paying Online:
To pay online by credit card or e-check, you must have an Accela ACA account that is linked to the record.
NOTE - There is a 3.2% Convenience Fee per transaction (minimum of $2) that is directly passed onto the credit card processor for all credit card transactions and is non-refundable.    Below are the fees for e-check verification:
eCheck Transaction Tiers Fees
$0.00 to $50,000.00 $2.00 w/Verification Per Transaction
$50,000.01 to $75,000.00 $3.25 w/Verification Per Transaction
$75,000.01 to $100,000.00 $6.25 w/Verification Per Transaction
$100,000.01 to $150,000.00 $10.25 w/Verification Per Transaction
$150,000.00 + $250,000.00 $15.25 w/Verification Per Transaction
Paying in Person:
You can now pay by credit card in person.  Convenience fee terms and conditions apply.

How Do I Check Status on My Building Permit Application in Accela ACA?
To view the processing status for a permit, you must be logged in with an account linked to the permit. If your account is not linked to the permit, or you cannot view the permit details, please send an email request the Building Department via:
/FormCenter/Accela-ACA-Building-8/Accela-ACA-Building-62
Accela - FAQ
How Do I Search for Information in Accela ACA?
Searches may be completed by record number, address, or parcel information without logging in; however, If you are looking to view your project details, download or upload files, pay fees, and more, you must be logged in to your account. If you have not yet created a user account, see "How Do I Register for an Account".
Property Records Database
The Village has digitized Building Permit records in Accela since February of 2021,  All Building Permit related records should be in Accela as of that date.  There is an ongoing backfile scanning project in place to digitize records prior to February 2021.  A Freedom of Information (FOIL) Request will need to be completed and submitted to the Clerk for files prior to February, 2021.
