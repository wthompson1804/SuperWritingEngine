Smoking is prohibited at all Borough and School-owned facilities.

Borough Field Requests
Requests for use of Borough fields must be made in writing to the Board of Recreation including all relevant information, including a roster for any team proposed to use the field. Approval must be granted at a Board of Recreation meeting before the proposed event, and so the request must be received prior to a scheduled meeting. Approval will be granted with the condition that a certificate of insurance is provided to the Borough. Use is for Borough residents only. Contact the Board Secretary / Director at 973-427-5555, ext. 309 for more information.
Contact the Passaic County Parks Department at 973-881-4832 for use of Goffle Brook Park or the Diamond Bridge fields.
