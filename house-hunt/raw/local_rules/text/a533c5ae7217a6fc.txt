Englewood Parks & Recreation office is located at the Gatehouse in the front of Mackay Park, our beautiful 28.6 acres park.
Our office builds, monitors and promotes seasonal activities for our community by working in partnership with our various City Departments, Englewood Board of Education, Houses of Worship, Local Businesses, Community Leaders, and Coaches.
Mission & Vision Statement
Our mission is to provide quality recreation opportunities to residents of the community through creative programs and safe, clean facilities in the most efficient way possible. Through this mission, it is our purpose to provide lifelong leisure experiences that will assist in contributing to a positive way of life.
