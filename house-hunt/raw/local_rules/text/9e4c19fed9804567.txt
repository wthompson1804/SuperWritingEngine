Animal Shelter
Ready for Sunny Days & New Friends?
The Town of Hempstead Animal Shelter is home to playful pups and laid-back cats waiting for a fresh start. Browse available pets below before visiting the shelter.
Animal  Control
Animal Control officers respond to reports of loose or stray dogs and injured or sick cats.   To request Animal Control services, contact the Town of Hempstead Animal Shelter at 516-785-5220.  After hours, please contact the Department of Public Safety at 516-538-1900.
Please contact the NYS Department of Environmental Conservation for information about controlling nuisance wildlife (ie. raccoons, Canada geese, bats).
Adoption Information
Bring the love of a dog or cat into your home. All adopted pets will be spayed or neutered, will receive required inoculations, and will be microchipped. These are included in adoption fees.
The adoption fee for dogs and cats is $25.
While no appointment is required, we strongly urge visitors to call ahead to ensure that we can properly allocate resources to your family and ensure quality time with the animals. Please come in and visit us, and find your new forever family member!
The Adoption Process
Please complete the Adoption Application and provide two
non-related
personal references and a veterinary reference if you have previously owned pets.
Consult with one of our adoption staff members prior to meeting animals for a history of the animal.
All household members must meet the animals you are interested in to ensure a correct match.
If you have a dog, a dog-to-dog "meet and greet" is required. Your dog
MUST
be up-to-date on their rabies vaccine unless a letter can be provided by your veterinarian stating it is not recommended.
If you have a cat, you must provide a history of veterinary care.
Please Note
All of our animals are spayed/neutered, medically checked, current with age-appropriate vaccinations, behaviorally evaluated, and provided with a microchip before adoption.
Incomplete applications or applicants who have not completed the adoption process cannot be considered or approved.
We
DO NOT HOLD
animals.
Adoption fees are waived year-round for seniors, veterans, physically challenged residents, volunteer firefighters, and auxiliary police officers. Adoption fees may also be waived during various special events.
Depending on the dog or cat's availability, some pets may not be ready for immediate release. We are an "open admission" shelter and receive animals 24/7. Please check back often!
When Finalizing an Adoption
You must provide a photo ID with proof of current address.
Only cash or personal checks are accepted forms of payment.
If you are adopting a dog, please bring your own leash and collar.
If you are adopting a cat, please bring your own carrier.
