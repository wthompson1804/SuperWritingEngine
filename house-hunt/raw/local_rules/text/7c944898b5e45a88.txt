FRIDAY, MAY 01 ***
*** CLICK HERE TO ACCESS THE PERMIT PORTAL ***
There are a variety of parking options within the Village of Larchmont. Each may have a specific regulation, fee, or requirement. Please review each option carefully in finding the parking solution to meet your needs:
Shoppers Parking
Convenient short-term parking geared to the needs of patrons of Larchmont's Post Road and Palmer Avenue Business Districts is readily available. Payment for parking is fast and easy, and can be completed at a paystation (credit card, coins or bills) or through the Passport mobile parking app. Visit
Shoppers Parking
for more information.
Permit Parking
Permit parking is available for residents, business owners/employees, and commuters (resident/non-resident) in any one of the Village's nine municipal lots. Generally, parking permits are required to park overnight, by day, or 24/7; however, regulations in individual lots may vary (check signage on-location). Temporary parking permits are also available for sale for short-term parking needs (up to two weeks). Visit
Permit Parking
for more information.
Non-Metered Street Parking
Parking on any street that does not otherwise have a posted regulation is limited to four hours between 8AM and 6PM.
Overnight on-street parking is prohibited between 2AM and 6AM. This regulation is Village-wide, 365 days per year, as posted on all roads leading into the Village. By request, temporary overnight on-street parking may be permitted. Please contact the Police Department at
(914) 834-1000
for instructions.
Other Parking
The Village also provides
Parking Permits for Persons with Disabilities
. Please print and fill the application, and submit it to the Village Clerk's Office.
In addition, the Village has
Electric Vehicle Charging Stations
.
