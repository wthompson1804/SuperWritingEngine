Parks & Public Property
The Parks & Public Property Department manages and operates 12 Town Parks, 3 Fields, and The Town Pool.
For more information on each park, please visit the respective section from the drop down menu or call us at 201-295-5178.
Our 3 Fields are:
Centennial Field at 11 Port Imperial Boulevard
Little League at 5300-14 Broadway Avenue
Miller Stadium at 5808 – 5908 Jackson Street
Our Town Pool:
West New York Swim Club  6600 Anthony M. Defino Way

Annual September 11th Remembrance Ceremony
Posted September 8, 2026
Mayor Albio Sires, the Board of Commissioners & The West New York Police Department invite you to the annual September 11th Remembrance Ceremony.
Friday, September 11 at 8:30AM
Donnelly Park (60th Street & Boulevard East)
As we come upon the 25th anniversary of the tragic events of September 11th, 2001, we extend our love and support to survivors, families of those who lost their lives, and heroes of that fateful day. We remember their bravery and sacrifices.
West New York will never forget.

Celebrate and Empower the Incredible Mothers in Our Community
Posted May 7, 2026
Mayor Albio Sires and the Board of Commissioners invite you to celebrate and empower the incredible mothers in our community at a special two-day event at the West New York Cultural Center.
Join us on May 15 & 16 at 4:30 PM for inspiring workshops designed to support, uplift, and motivate.
Friday’s “Me First” session focuses on self-care and finding balance, while Saturday’s “From Fear to Business” workshop offers guidance on turning your ideas into a thriving future.
Come learn practical tools, connect with your community, invest in yourself, and take the next step toward empowerment. We look forward to seeing you there!
The WNY Town Hall will close early on Wednesday December 24.
