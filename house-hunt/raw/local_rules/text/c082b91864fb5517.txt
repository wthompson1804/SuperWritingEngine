Playtime in the Park (Outdoors)
Join Miss Patti for music, movement, and fun in the sun! Each class features a playful theme, live singing, instruments, bubbles, and plenty of joy. Caregivers and children are invited to sing, dance, and play together in this fun-filled outdoor music series.
This class is for ages 6 months 4 years old.
Please bring a blanket to each class. No refunds.
