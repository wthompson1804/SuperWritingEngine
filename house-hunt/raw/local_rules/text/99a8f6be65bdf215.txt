                            SNOW PROCEDURES

Snow Notice

Important notice to all residents and commercial properties:

•    The Department of Public Works (“DPW”) seeks your cooperation
     during snowfall events, to help keep the streets plowed and accessible
     for emergency vehicles, as well as pedestrian and vehicular traffic.

•    During snow events, we ask all vehicles be removed from snow
     emergency routes.

     Harrison Avenue (Passaic Ave. to Schuyler Ave.) and Frank E.
     Rodgers Blvd. (North & South) (Bergen Street to Scott Mobus
     Place).

     This will help the DPW plow/remove snow curb to curb. Vehicles on
     the snow emergency streets will be subject to tickets and removal.

Disposing of Snow

Snow removal. Please check your street for snow removal signs and also
check our website for updated removal schedule or call 973-268-2296 (DPW).
