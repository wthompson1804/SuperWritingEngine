Traffic Bureau
The Traffic Bureau oversees the enforcement of moving and parking laws and crash investigations within the Borough.  The unit manages handicap-parking, street permit related concerns and conducts educational initiatives to promote safe driving while supporting safer street planning.  This division often partners with engineering and municipal government on traffic safety improvements and community awareness campaigns.
