                      There is no longer a $5 fee for a Resident Parking sticker. No more than one
                      vehicle per licensed driver shall be issued a sticker. Your license & registration
                      must match your home address. Resident parking stickers may be obtained at the
                      Hackensack Police Department, located at 225 State Street. Applications are also
                      available on the City’s website at https://www.hackensack.org/traffic-bureau/
                      under the “Files and Links” section, titled “Residential Parking Applications.”
                      As the ordinance has been adopted and appropriate signage installed, any vehicle
                      that does not properly display a resident parking sticker will be subject to ticketing
                      and/or towing.

                      Should you have any questions please contact the Hackensack Police Dept. Traffic
                      Division at 201-646-7737 or the City Manager’s office 201-646-3901.
