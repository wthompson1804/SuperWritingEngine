Parking Overview
Park Norwalk delivers efficient, customer focused parking services that support Norwalk’s mobility, economic vitality, and community connectivity. As a financially independent enterprise, Park Norwalk funds all operating costs, repairs, technology upgrades, and new facilities exclusively through parking revenue - with no impact on the City’s General Fund.
Park Norwalk manages more than 4,200 on  and off street spaces, including:
4 parking garages
10 surface lots
2,000+ metered or time restricted spaces
Every improvement - from garage upgrades to new technology - is paid for by the people who use the system, not by Norwalk taxpayers.

Parking Operations
Park Norwalk’s refreshed identity emphasizes our staff as community ambassadors, prioritizing positive public engagement over punitive oversight. While turnover rules remain essential for supporting local businesses, our team leads with service: friendly conversations, directions, and proactive outreach.
In 2025, enforcement staff also assisted with maintaining 12.3 miles of new sidewalk, contributing to safer and more walkable streets citywide.
Enforcement
Park Norwalk’s enforcement approach prioritizes education, assistance, and voluntary compliance, reserving citations for situations that require them to maintain safety and fairness. Investments in clearer, more intuitive signage have reduced confusion and improved compliance.
Maintenance
Park Norwalk’s maintenance crews provide round‑the‑clock support across all facilities. Their responsibilities include:
Installing and maintaining on‑street regulatory signage
Managing and collecting from pay stations
Installing bike racks
Sweeping, striping, power washing, and pothole repairs
Daily cleaning of lots, garages, stations, common areas, and restrooms
These efforts ensure that Norwalk’s parking facilities remain clean, safe, and welcoming.
