Windsor at Mariners offers a variety of spacious and well-designed studio, one-, two-, and three-bedroom apartments, as well as townhomes with attached garages. Each home features high-end finishes, including granite countertops, wood flooring, and in-unit washers and dryers. Expansive windows frame stunning views of the Hudson River and New York City skyline, creating a living experience that is as visually impressive as it is comfortable.

Whether you’re looking for a cozy studio or a sprawling waterfront townhome, you’ll find the perfect space to fit your lifestyle. Explore our available floorplans and secure your new home today.
