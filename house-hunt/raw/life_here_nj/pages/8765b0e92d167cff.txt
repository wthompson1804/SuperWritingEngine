 Rahway Events
 Some of the many events that happen through the year in Rahway.
 Shop Local 
 Step into the warmth of Rahway’s locally owned shops and eateries this season. Explore local deals and events, and celebrate community and holiday sparkle starting November 27.
 Viva Rahway 
 Main Street transforms into a lively piazza with Italian food tasting from local restaurants, a classic car show, and Italian-inspired art alley, live DJ, games, giveaways, and more.
 Local Rahway Events 
 There is always something happening in Rahway! From Trivia Nights, to Comedy Shows to Yoga and Art Classes, our local businesses have something special to offer all ages!
 Business Spotlight
 Rahway’s Special Improvement District (SID) is pleased to highlight local businesses that help shape our local identity. Recognizing and supporting the rich, diverse make up of our neighborhood entrepreneurs, helps create a sustainable and healthy local economy. Research proves that small businesses are the backbone of any community. Statistically, main street businesses generate a greater investment back into the local neighborhoods that they serve. Rahway is committed to championing those investing in our city.
