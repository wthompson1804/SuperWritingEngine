 14th Annual Newark River Day & Scavenger Hunt 
 Saturday, June 20, 2026 
 11:00 AM 
 3:00 PM 
 Google Calendar 
 ICS 
 The 14th Annual Newark River Day & Scavenger Hunt in Newark is scheduled for Saturday, June 20, 2026, from 11:00 AM to 3:00 PM, celebrating the Passaic River waterfront. The event typically features community activities at Riverfront Park, often near the "Orange Sticks" on Raymond Blvd, fostering a connection to the city's river. 
 Location: Newark Riverfront Park (usually at the "Orange Sticks" near Raymond Blvd).
 Activities: Past events have featured live music, dancing, fitness classes, boat tours, vendors, and local food. 
 This event is part of a larger, ongoing effort by the Newark Riverfront Revival to revitalize the area and provide public access to the waterfront for recreation.
