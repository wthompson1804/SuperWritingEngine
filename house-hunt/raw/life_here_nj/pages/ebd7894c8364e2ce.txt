 Donnelly Memorial Park
 Donnelly Memorial Park 
 54th to 60th John F. Kennedy Boulevard East
 Hours
 Open 24 Hours a day
 Features
 -Dog Park
 -Playgrounds
 -Lady of Justice Fountain ( 9/11 Monument)
 -Flower Box Fountain
 -Game Tables including chessboards
 -The Pavilion / Birdhouse
 -Thomas Donnelly Monument
 -Christopher Columbus Monument
 Donnelly Memorial Park is a great park with magnificent views of New York. There are plenty of benches and tables to sit at and overall, the park is clean. It has a kid’s playground as well as a dog park. The park is fenced in to ensure safety for children. Drink fountains are available throughout, including one for dogs. Large trees provide shaded areas on warm summer days. You will love the NY skyline from this angle. It feels different than the sight below the cliff. The colors and the reflection of the sun from the skyscrapers look amazing around sunset.
