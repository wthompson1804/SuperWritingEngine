 Honor A Legacy
 Originally opened in 1929 as one of five famed “Wonder Theatres,” Loew’s Jersey Theatre has been a beloved landmark within Jersey City. From its golden age as a grand movie palace to its legendary live performances, nearly a century of history lives within its walls.

 Getting 
 Here. 
 Easily reached from throughout the tri-state area, Loew’s Jersey Theatre sits directly across from the Journal Square Transportation Center, offering seamless access via PATH trains from Hoboken, Manhattan, and Newark, along with NJ Transit Light Rail and regional bus service.
 Nearby major roadways—including I-95, I-78, and Routes 1 & 9—provide added driving convenience.
