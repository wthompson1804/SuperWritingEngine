 The Highland Park Farmers' Market reopens March 6. It runs every Friday until Dec. 11. Opening day starts at 11 a.m. and wraps up at 4 p.m.
 Vendors will set up shop downtown, right next to 212 Raritan Ave. Hours shift with the seasons — shorter when it's cold, longer when the sun sticks around.
 According to Patch , March, November, and December hours run from 11 a.m. to 4 p.m. From April through October the market stays open an extra hour, closing at 5 p.m. instead.
 Rain or shine, the market happens every Friday for 40 straight weeks. Shoppers can count on it without worrying about cancellations or surprise closures.
 The Raritan Avenue spot sits right in the heart of town, making it easy for people to stop by. It's the same location all season long, so nobody has to guess where to go.
 For updates, check the Highland Park Farmers Market on Facebook, Instagram, or its website. Main Street Highland Park also posts on social media and keeps its site current with fresh information.
 March kicks off the 2026 season. Shoppers can browse vendor stalls from spring through fall, with the final market day landing in December.
 That Friday routine makes planning easy. People know when to shop each week. Peak months offer that extra hour, giving visitors more time to wander through stalls and pick up what they need.
 Downtown Highland Park plays host for all 40 weeks, keeping things simple and predictable for everyone who shows up.
