 Bayonne Farmers Market
 Bayonne Farmers Market 
 (201) 240-9112
 bayonnefarmersmarket@gmail.com 
 Available Tuesdays from 2:00pm-7:00pm from June to November
 Located at West 23rd Street and Broadway Bayonne, NJ 07002
 Guests can acquire produce vouchers from the State of New Jersey which can be used to buy fruits and vegetables from the farmer’s market.
 Guests can also become a part of the Community Supported Agriculture Program which allows city residents to have direct access to high-quality, fresh produce grown nearby by regional farmers. When you become a member of CSA, you’re purchasing a “share” of vegetables. Customized personal, half and full shares are available and will be delivered weekly, to the market for pick-up.
 Bayonne Farmers Market is helping to foster a sense of community by bringing farmers and local businesses together right in our neighborhood. Our goal is to offer a wonderful variety of fresh and wholesome foods to our community.
