 Welcome to The City by the Sea. 
 Pier Village isn't just a day at the beach it's Long Branch's year-round destination for upscale dining, boutique shopping, boardwalk fun, and a luxury hotel where the ocean is always just outside your door.

 Pier Village Holiday Food Drive
 The Gift of Giving
 Pier Village Holiday Food Drive | November 1 – December 13, 2026 
 This holiday season, Pier Village invites our community to embrace the spirit of giving through our annual Holiday Food Drive benefiting Fulfill Food Bank of Monmouth & Ocean Counties .
 The holidays are a time for gathering, gratitude, and generosity. Together, we have an opportunity to extend that spirit beyond our own celebrations and help ensure local families have access to essential food items throughout the season.
 Last year, the generosity of our community resulted in an extraordinary 900 pounds of donated food . This year, we hope to build on that impact with the continued support of our residents, visitors, and local businesses.
 Share the Spirit of the Season
 We welcome donations of nonperishable food items, including:
 Pantry Essentials: Pasta, rice, oatmeal, cereal, and dried beans
 Canned Goods: Vegetables, fruit, beans, soup, and chili
 Protein & Spreads: Peanut butter, canned tuna, and canned chicken
 Breakfast & Snacks: Applesauce, granola bars, and whole-grain crackers
 Cooking Essentials: Shelf-stable milk, broth, and boxed meals
 Please ensure all donations are unopened, in their original packaging, and within their best-by dates.
 Participating Donation Locations
 Make giving part of your next visit to Pier Village. Donations may be dropped off at the following participating locations:
 Piazza on the Pier 
 Studio Pilates 
 The Baked Bear 
 Pier Pins 
 Turning Point 
 Wave Resort 
 A Little Kindness Goes a Long Way
 Every contribution, no matter the size, helps make a meaningful difference for families in our community. As we celebrate the season, let’s come together to share not only the joy of the holidays, but also the generosity and compassion that make our community special.
 Give generously. Share the warmth. Make the season brighter.
