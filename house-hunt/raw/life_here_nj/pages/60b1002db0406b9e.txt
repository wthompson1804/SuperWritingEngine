 Looking for nightlife in Elizabeth? The city has many notable bars and lounges where visitors and locals alike can enjoy a little nightlife.
 From sports bars to Cuban music lounges, you can find it all in Elizabeth. View our listings below to learn more about lounges, bars, and clubs in Elizabeth, NJ.

 Trendy Lounges in Elizabeth, NJ
 Dolce Lounge 
 Dolce Lounge has a laid-back environment where you can listen to live music while enjoying delicious food and cocktails. They have an eclectic menu with an abundance of American, Italian, and Latin American options. You can enjoy a hearty steak, savory pasta dish, or crisp empanadas—or all three—depending on your mood. The menu is heavy on meat and seafood, with a limited number of vegetarian options such as pizza, caprese sandwiches, and salads. Dolce Lounge also has an excellent bar where you can order many different kinds of cocktails. After dinner, head out to the spacious back room which transforms to a nightclub for live music and dancing.
 Chupito's Lounge 
 Chupito’s Lounge is a prime location to experience Latin fusion cuisine, and culture. The restaurant serves favorites like picada, empanadas, and an array of seafood options. Finish off your meal with tres leches and the chocolate brownie. The lounge is known for its delicious, refreshing cocktails prepared by expert mixologists. Regular events include top sports viewing and DJs, where you can enjoy drinks, live music, and dancing late into the evening.
 Top-Rated Sports Bars in Elizabeth, NJ
 The Lobby Sports Bar 
 The Lobby Sports Bar is an excellent destination for sports fans and music lovers alike. This unique venue is a restaurant, lounge, and sports bar all in one. You won’t miss a second of the action as The Lobby has 30 HD televisions as well as patio with a 20-foot outdoor screen for sports games and visual effects. For music fans, The Lobby regularly hosts Latino performers and well-known DJs. Dance lovers will delight in the free bachata and salsa classes offered every Wednesday night.
 The restaurant features American and Latin American cuisine, with favorites like tacos, empanadas, and burgers. From the extensive drink menu, popular options include mojitos, margaritas, and frozen cocktails. The Lobby hosts happy hour every Monday through Friday and is a great place to unwind after work. Going out on a Friday or Saturday night? Be sure to take note of the upscale and stylish dress code—no casual wear after 8 p.m. on the weekend.
 Barcode 
 If you want a single location to eat, watch the game, have a few drinks, and dance all night, Barcode is the spot for you. Barcode is the only nightlife venue in the tri-state area with an indoor, heated line, so party-goers can wait on line comfortably away from the rain, snow, and cold.
 The combo sports bar, restaurant, and nightclub features plenty of TVs to watch the game on, along with a massive 20-by-8-foot LED screen. You can order a full meal while you root for your team, then play your own game of pool. If you haven’t had your fill of fun, stick around for when the lights go low and the music turns up as the establishment transitions to club mode. The venue opens up to a patio with extra bars for those nights when a celebrity DJ takes control of the turntables or an of-the-moment rap act pumps up the crowd for a club performance.
