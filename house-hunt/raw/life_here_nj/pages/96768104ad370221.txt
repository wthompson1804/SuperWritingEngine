 Dog Park Rules
🐾 🐾 🐾 🐾 🐾
 🦴 All users are hereby advised that the dog park is an unsupervised recreational area of the Plainsboro park system. 🦴 
 Open sunrise to sunset. 
 All users are further advised that the use of this facility is at their own risk. 
 Children under 8 years of age are prohibited inside the dog park. 
 Users must properly secure access gates upon entering and leaving.
 No more than 20 dogs are allowed in the dog park at one time. 
 Dogs must be over the age of 6 months, and must be healthy, vaccinated, legally licensed, and wearing dog tags. 
 Not permitted: 
 Aggressive dogs
 Dogs under 6 months
 Female dogs in heat
 Sick dogs
 No other types of animals are permitted within dog park.
 No food or glass containers are permitted within the fenced area. No smoking within the fenced area.
 Owners are permitted a maximum of 2 dogs per adult each visit. 
 Owners under the age of 16 must be accompanied by an adult 18 years or older while inside fenced area.
 Dogs must be leashed until after entering and prior to leaving the fenced area of the dog park. 
 Adults must supervise and maintain control over dogs at all times. 
 Owners are responsible for cleaning up after their dogs and properly disposing of waste. 
 Digging is not permitted and owners are responsible for filling holes created by their dogs.
 Regulations are enforceable by any police officer, animal control officer, park ranger, or other code enforcement official. Violators are subject to summonses, fines, or removal from park.
 Emergencies should be reported to the police department at 911. Non-emergency incidents should be reported to the police department at 609-799-2333.
 Owners are responsible for compliance with all other park rules as governed under Chapter 25 of the Township Code.
