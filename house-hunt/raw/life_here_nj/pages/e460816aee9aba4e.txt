 Welcome to Elizabeth, New Jersey
 Share 
 Experience Elizabeth, New Jersey and embark on one-of-a-kind adventures.
 Founded in 1664, Elizabeth, formally known as Elizabethtown, was the first capital of New Jersey.
 Welcome world, to the center of it all! Being that we are minutes from Newark Liberty International Airport and driving distance to New York City, Philadelphia, Atlantic City and the best beaches in New Jersey, there is something for everyone! 
 Our nationally recognized brand name hotels are waiting for your arrival. From family-friendly activities to exceptional restaurants , we'll keep you busy with plenty of unforgettable things to do ! We have trending nightlife that is sure to keep you awake and dancing all night. 
 Step into history as Elizabeth celebrates America’s 250th ! As one of New Jersey's oldest cities, Elizabeth invites visitors to explore its rich Revolutionary War heritage, historic landmarks, and the remarkable stories that helped shape our nation. Through engaging tours, cultural experiences, and special commemorative events, discover the people, places, and moments that connect Elizabeth's storied past to America's present. Celebrate 250 years of history, heritage, and discovery in Elizabeth! 
 Experience Elizabeth...she'll surprise you!
 Keep an eye out for: Filming locations from ' The Sopranos ' can be found across Elizabeth!
