 Gotta Match!
 Ongoing
 The exhibit explores the once-common culture of smoking through artifacts that reflect Trenton’s commercial and manufacturing history. Featured items include matchbooks advertising Trenton businesses, ashtrays promoting local companies or manufactured in Trenton, and cigars produced by Trenton cigar makers.

 About the Museum
 In the heart of Trenton’s historic Cadwalader Park, Trenton City Museum at Ellarslie Mansion houses a fine collection of art and artifacts related to Trenton’s historical, industrial, and cultural past and present. In addition to changing and permanent exhibitions, we host a range of community events, performances, workshops, and classes.
