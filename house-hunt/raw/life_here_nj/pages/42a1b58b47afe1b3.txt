 Main Street Highland Park (MSHP) is a volunteer-based non-profit community development organization that manages the downtown Business Improvement District (BID) for the benefit of merchants, businesses, and residents of Highland Park. As a dedicated advocate for the downtown, MSHP's programs and projects improve the image of downtown Highland Park, focusing on its commercial assets, visual appeal, safety, and desirability as a destination for business, shopping, dining, fun, community, arts, and sustainability.

 DESIGN COMMITTEE 
 Our Design Team works to make aesthetic improvements to downtown. The committee administers the sign and awning grant program, manages downtown lighting and other decorative projects, and oversees the maintenance of downtown planters.
 PROMOTIONS COMMITTEE 
 Our Promotions Team enhances the image and promotes the district's unique social, economic, and cultural character. This committee works on the downtown's annual festivals and events designed to get people into Downtown Highland Park.
 ARTS SUBCOMMITTEE 
 Our Arts team helps Main Street coordinate with the Highland Park Art Collective and our artist community at large. This committee promotes and enables civic art projects and serves as a Main Street's link to the surrounding Artistic events and happenings.
 ORGANIZATIONAL COMMITTEE 
 Our Org Team builds a strong base of funding and network of resources and volunteers, board members, and committee members to sustain Main Street's mission. The committee fund-raises, recruits volunteers, and runs financial management.
 ECONOMIC RESTRUCTURING COMMITTEE 
 Our ER Team works to support, diversify, and grow the business district. This committee attracts new businesses, hosts business networking events, does outreach to local businesses, does outreach to local businesses, and helps sharpen the competitiveness of business enterprises.
