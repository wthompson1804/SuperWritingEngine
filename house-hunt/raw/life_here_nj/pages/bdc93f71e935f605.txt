Coffee Shops to Work Remotely From in South Jersey 
-->
 Just because you’re not in the office, doesn’t mean you can’t have a coffee break. Pack up your laptop, order your favorite caffeinated drink (and maybe a pastry?) and get to work at these South Jersey coffee shops. 
 By Zena 
 Moorestown 
 Lots of tables in a European-style cafe, where you can order breakfast and lunch, coffee drinks and out of this world pastries. 
 Jersey Java & Tea 
 Haddonfield 
 Plenty of coffee options, plus some baked goods and quiches to keep you full through your work session. And a sprawling patio for nice days. 
 Nuanced Cafe 
 Camden 
 Choose between tables and stools or a couch to enjoy your coffee, tea or smoothie and get to work. 
 Common Grounds 
 Oaklyn 
 A small space filled with plants that’s a good spot for weekday working. 
 Brown Dog Cafe 
 Audubon 
 Seating choices from tables and chairs to the coffee bar to a plush couch that adds bright colors to the room. The menu features classic coffees, signature syrups, breakfast sandwiches and more. 
 Four Green Cats 
 Mt. Laurel 
 An eclectic collection of vintage chairs, couches, tables and decor make for a cozy place for a work session, with a full breakfast and lunch menu and lots of tasty desserts. 
 Evermore Coffee Roasters 
 Burlington 
 Hang a right at the hanging motorcycle and pull up a chair in this industrial space that has just enough cozy elements to make you feel right at home. Browse the menu for your next coffee order or set up for breakfast or lunch. 
 Magic Brain Cafe 
 Cape May 
 Bright colors, surprise nooks and cozy corners are packed in this magic spot that features a menu of blended beverages, coffee classics and pastries to fuel your next project. 
 GNM Coffee Shop 
 Barnegat 
 For those who don’t like being stuck inside from 9 to 5, stop by GNM to soak in the sun with walls of windows as you sip on a smoothie, cold brew, kombucha and coffee drinks or order from the sandwich menu. 
 High Grounds Coffee Roasters 
 Glassboro 
 This Rowan go-to spot has the vibes and the coffee flights. Specialty lattes, matcha, overnight oats, smoothies, muffins, beignets and more will have you indulging while you work away.
