 Food, Music, Drink.
 Hat City Kitchen is a 3 Star Restaurant nestled in one of the oldest buildings in Orange, NJ, host to live music and bands from national to local favorites.
 Creosoul Comfort Food
 HCK offers American Comfort foods with an influence from the south and New Orleans. By focusing on a simple menu, Hat City Kitchen created an artisanal process with broad appeal to families, foodies and critical Epicureans alike.
 Menu
 New American cuisine with New Orleans influence, from meatloaf and shrimp & grits to burgers, wings and a wide drink menu.
 Live Music
 Entertainment several nights a week, open mic jams, blues jams, bands and community events.
 The Valley
 All profits from Hat City Kitchen support the revitalization of the Valley neighborhood.
