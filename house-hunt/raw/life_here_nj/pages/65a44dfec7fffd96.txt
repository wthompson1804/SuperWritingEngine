 Easton Farmers’ Market
 Established in 1752, the Easton Farmers’ Market is the country’s oldest continuous open-air market. The market provides fresh, high quality, local produce and food products directly from regional growers to the consumer. EFM strives to support local agriculture and preserve Northampton County’s agricultural heritage, while promoting the urban-rural connection with the farmers who grow their food. Situated in Centre Square, the market contributes to the City of Easton’s revitalization by transforming the downtown into a vibrant and important social gathering place. More than fourteen events and festivals are held throughout the season. The market also serves as an economic driver, offering local businesses, artisans and crafters the opportunity to vend their products. 
 The market is open Saturdays 9am -1pm, May through November, and Wednesdays, 4-8pm, June through September.
