Stamford ¶ In a city as spirited, lively, and diverse as Stamford, there is always something to do. Whether you're strolling along the 19 miles of shoreline, dining and drinking at the bars Downtown, or hiking the scenic trails of North Stamford, you'll quickly discover that there's never a dull moment. ¶ The Stamford Town Center mall features 130 shops, including J. Crew, H&M, Macy's, Saks Off Fifth, and Brooks Brothers. It also boasts a pedestrian-friendly restaurant row, home to The Capital Grille, Peter Cheng's, Plan B Burger Bar, The Cheesecake Factory, and Pieology. ¶ With more than 150 restaurants, six major hotels, and 18 movie screens, Stamford offers something for everyone. The city is also home to over 40 parks and recreational areas, many of which offer access to Long Island Sound. These parks feature marinas, tennis courts, softball fields, horseshoe pits, playgrounds, skating rinks, picnic areas, fishing, and more. Mill River Park, often referred to as a mini Central Park, lies in the heart of Downtown Stamford, hosting various events throughout the year in its beautifully landscaped space. Science and nature lovers can explore the Stamford Museum & Nature Center and the Bartlett Arboretum. ¶ One of the city's most anticipated events, Alive@Five, is an outdoor summer concert series held in Stamford's Columbus Park. It draws big names each year, such as Cee Lo Green, Plain White T's, Third Eye Blind, Sugar Ray, and more. With six consecutive Thursday evening concerts, there's plenty of time to catch a few after work. ¶ The Palace Theatre hosts world-renowned performers, major off-Broadway musicals, and local cultural organizations like the Stamford Symphony Orchestra, Connecticut Grand Opera, and the Connecticut Ballet, the state's professional ballet company. Meanwhile, the Avon Theatre offers an \"art cinema\" experience, showcasing the best in independent and art house films on two screens. ¶ Stamford's Thanksgiving Day Parade is one of the largest helium balloon parades in the country, drawing over 100,000 spectators annually.
