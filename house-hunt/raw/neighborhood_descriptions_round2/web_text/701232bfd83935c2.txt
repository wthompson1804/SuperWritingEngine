Norwalk ¶ Norwalk offers beautiful beaches, unforgettable sunsets, and is just 45 minutes from New York City. South Norwalk, known as SoNo, is a unique destination offering a variety of shopping opportunities, restaurants, and vibrant nightlife. The Maritime Aquarium and Stepping Stones Children's Museum attract over 750,000 visitors annually and are complemented by exciting events such as the SoNo Arts Festival, Oyster Festival, and the In-Water Boat Show. ¶ The seaport is home to some of Connecticut’s finest protected marinas, catering to both sports and leisure boating enthusiasts. Norwalk is a coastal gem in Fairfield County, known for its rich culture, scenic beauty, and diverse attractions. ¶ The SoNo area is especially well-loved for its eclectic vibe, where visitors can explore the Maritime Aquarium, join a lighthouse tour, embark on a sunset cruise, or simply enjoy the waterfront scenery. ¶ The Norwalk shoreline offers access to some of Connecticut’s best boating, public beaches, lighthouses, protected islands, and marinas—making it a true coastal haven with both culture and class.
