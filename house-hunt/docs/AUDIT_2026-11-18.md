# Audit of the short list, 2026-11-18

Will refused to believe that "the same three neighborhoods for almost anyone" is real. He was right. Findings, with
the numbers behind them (all from `data/listings.csv`, `docs/RUN_REPORT.md`, `site/concierge_data.json`).

1. **The "best three" came from which site we could re-read, not from the places.** Each building's score is
   multiplied by `sure` (1.0 seen today, 0.8 with a star, 0.6 / 0.48 last seen earlier). Scores are bunched
   (0.575 to 0.40), so that multiplier decides the order. With it: Bloomfield, Stamford, Flushing. Without it:
   Flushing 0.695, Newark 0.634, West New York 0.627, Stamford 0.626, Jersey City 0.61, Bloomfield 0.605. Across 200
   preference mixes, first place without it: Flushing 107, Stamford 32, Newark 13, then seven others.
2. **The 90.5% baseline was circular.** It tested the model against itself, so it measured the model's insensitivity,
   not the market.
3. **Four of the six measured things barely separate places.** Parking is unknown for most buildings (median value
   0.5 = "unknown"); size is missing and filled with the median for 23 of 132; a park is close almost everywhere
   (median 0.87); going out is near zero for most (median 0.12). The score is mostly drive, cost and `sure`.
4. **Listings with one unknown never get ranked if the unknown is dogs or the move-in date.** Only gate status
   `pass` enters the master table. An unknown floor/elevator passes as an open item; an unknown dog policy or move-in
   date does not. At $2,000 or less with a detail page: 540 blocked on dogs alone, 111 on move-in alone, 412 on both.
   About 110 of these have an elevator or floor settled and should be showable with one thing to confirm.
5. **"Pets allowed" without the word "dogs" counts as unknown** (about 370 listings: "allowed=true", "Pet friendly").
   With the floor also unknown that is two unknowns, so the listing is hidden. The two-unknowns rule, not quality, is
   the main filter: 2,793 detail listings at $2,000 or less, 532 pass.
6. **Possible misread of "no dogs" on Zumper** (264 rows "Dog | Policy | Not allowed | Cat | Not allowed"): to be
   checked against cached pages; it may be the site's default when the lister said nothing.
7. **The safety rule cuts 235 of 1,097 costed apartments, 186 of them New Haven** (property 31.2 per 1,000 against
   Jamaica's 25.0, citywide figure). That is the owner's rule working as written; it is listed because of its size.
8. **The three-buildings promise** leaves 16 of 42 groups eligible.
